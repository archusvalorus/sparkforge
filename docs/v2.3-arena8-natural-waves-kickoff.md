## Explicit provisional schedule and accounting

Use Debug: `--arena8-six --arena8-title --arena8-natural-waves`. This fixes the accepted trial interval scale at 0.8 without changing actual game time, shipped stat/XP progression, attacks or warnings. Do not combine other lab/profile flags. Existing baseline/trial comparison remains separate and unchanged for its normal arguments.

- Keep the existing one ordinary startup attempt. Before 30 active seconds, timed emissions are ordinary attempts.
- At or after 30 seconds, reserve two consecutive eligible timed spawn allocations for the three-member carrier packet. On the next eligible emission, try the existing atomic carrier + Spurhound + Linekeeper spawn. Success spends the two reservations plus that current allocation: three actors cost three ordinary allocations, with no borrowed future slots.
- If packet placement is blocked, use the current allocation for an ordinary attempt, retain the two reservations, and retry on a later eligible emission. No extra clock, catch-up burst, unsafe fallback or partial packet. A permanently blocked packet leaves exactly two explicit reservations, not an unbounded starvation of ordinary waves.
- After the packet was introduced, the first eligible emission at or after 34 seconds tries one Signaler, costing one allocation. Blocked source placement falls back to an ordinary attempt and retries later. If the carrier is already dead, skip this source for the run rather than manufacturing another packet.
- Maximum lifetime introductions: one three-actor packet and one Signaler. No replacement after death, reinforcement, troop unloading or extra HP/XP/timing multiplier. Surviving released escorts remain normal living actors.
- The Signaler may automatically prepare one valid same-room order when existing eligibility and safe neighboring goals allow it. Existing first-valid-order arbitration and one-active-second planted warning remain. Once accepted, it never reissues, including after stun, source loss, cancelled warning or completed migration. Eligibility deferral is not a timer reset or free command.

Reservations and introductions use only unsuppressed emissions of the accepted pressure clock. Existing ability suppression consumes emissions without creating/reserving actors. Gameplay holds freeze scheduling and commands; resume does not replay missed work. Death/revive preserves this run's used slots/introduction limits; fresh restart/Title resets the ledger and cancels old commands. Carrier HP is intentionally the existing provisional ordinary baseline (including its low early-run HP); a FIRE-ON player may kill it before 34 seconds and legitimately prevent the Signaler introduction. Positive encounter qualification must explicitly use the existing FIRE-OFF kiting control if needed, and separately prove the carrier-loss/skip branch. Do not describe a kiting-only positive proof as guaranteed FIRE-ON encounter visibility.

Each event has a generation/sequence ticket, one completion and exact accounting: completed emissions = ordinary allocations + reserved allocations + packet cost + source cost. Ordinary thinning and blocked ordinary placements remain observable outcomes, not refund credits.

## Provisional timing revision from qualification

The original implementation used 30s packet eligibility and 45s source eligibility. Actual native exploration repeatedly showed the low-HP provisional carrier lost under normal combat before the source window; weapon-disabled test routes also struggled to survive that long. The candidate now trials source eligibility at **34 active seconds**, soon after the packet can be funded at roughly 32s. This is an explicit reversible first-encounter tuning revision, not an HP/damage/reward/timer cheat or an inherited PASS. Carrier loss still prevents the source; all costs/caps remain fixed. New model/build/device qualification is required against this revised source.

Native positive observation uses normal combat and actual offered upgrades, then requests the existing FIRE toggle at the first native playing-state opportunity at or after 29 active seconds for the introduction window (the successful phone observation switched at 31.333s after its real upgrade queue). It prefers actual offered controlled cards without changing availability. Some on-kill/lingering effects can still damage actors; success requires the carrier/source/order actually survive. This is controlled-build input evidence, not all-card natural encounter acceptance. Earlier failed input attempts and the earlier45s candidate evidence remain preserved separately.
