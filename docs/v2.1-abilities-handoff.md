## Conventions this track established

- **Card copy:** the selection card holds 4 lines × 17 chars (3 beside a
  MORE chip). `description` = a compact line (Claude writes it, Brandon
  approves it at the gate); the approved sentence goes VERBATIM in
  `UpgradeCard.detail`. Each harness has a "lines fit" validator.
- **Eligibility ships with the tree:** every non-signature card
  `requires: [.<tree>Unlocked]`, capstone included. In-tree prerequisites use
  their own capability (`.glacialDrift`, `.whiteout`) and
  `tierRequires[tier]` for a single rung. Gateway pity is SIGNATURES ONLY.
- **Tier numbers are totals; attack-speed % is a real firing-rate increase**
  (`fireRateMultiplier /= 1 + x`, re-based per rung).
- **Removed cards:** delete from the pool, leave a one-line comment with the
  retired id, never reuse an id. Dormant runtime for Arc Wake / Induction
  Step is scheduled for deletion in A7.
- **Kills:** always `onEnemyKilled(…, source:)`; it credits once and removes
  by identity. `EnemyNode.takeDamage` returns false for a dying enemy and
  records `finishingDamage` (remaining-HP-capped) — **that is Sanguinarian's
  input in A4.**
- **Two damage scales (legacy, Brandon knows):** projectiles/"% damage"
  effects = `Int(damageMultiplier)` → 1 at base vs 1–5 HP mobs
  (`PlayerStats.shotFractionDamage`); "% ATK" capstone effects =
  `effectiveAttack` (10 at base). Fractions on the hit scale round to 1.
  Enemy scaling probably starts at Arena 11+.
