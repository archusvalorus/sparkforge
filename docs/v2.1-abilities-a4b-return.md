## What was built

| Piece | Change |
|---|---|
| **Boss-kill chokepoint** (CL-29/30) | Every arena boss now reports its killing blow through a new `ArenaBossNode.onLethalHit` hook. It fires synchronously, once, after `isDead` is set and before the death animation or `onDeath`, and carries the damage the blow dealt to the boss's **remaining** HP. The scene snapshots everything the rewards read (death position, bleeding before the killing damage, damage channel, finishing damage, Bleed generation) into a pure `KillContext`. It marks the boss credited through a pure `BossKillLatch` **before** any effect runs, then resolves rewards through the **same path as enemy kills**. `bossDefeatedThisRun` is now set at the lethal hit, so dying during a boss's death animation keeps its forge-XP bonus. A DEBUG backstop in all six `onDeath` closures logs any death that wasn't credited. |
| **One reward path** | `onEnemyKilled` now builds a `KillContext` and calls `resolveKillRewards` (Forge triggers, Apex Bloodfed, Frenzy, Bloodlust, Siphon, Red Harvest, Sanguinarian) and `resolveDeathBursts` (Open Vein, Glass Blood, Null Bloom, Ember Burst, Chain Reaction). Enemy-only bookkeeping (kill count, Iceburst, seeds, arena gates, codex, XP orb, Ashling split) stays in `onEnemyKilled`. No player rewards once Spark is dead, **including the instant after an unrescued lethal hit**: a Thornwall or Iron Maiden reflect can kill on the very contact that killed him. |
| **"Killed a bleeding enemy"** | The target was already bleeding **before** the killing damage (`EnemyNode.diedBleeding`, plus the boss snapshot). Frenzy, Bloodlust, Red Harvest and Open Vein all use it. Glass Blood is stricter: a Bleed **tick** must deliver the kill. |
| `bleed_1` Nick → **Gouge** | Crit chance totals **10% / 20%** (id kept). |
| `bleed_2` Hemorrhage | **Adds** +1.0 to the crit-damage multiplier (CL-31). Forge Path Deadeye's +0.10 survives (2.10 → 3.10). |
| `bleed_3` **Frenzy** | Killing a bleeding enemy gives **+15% attack speed for 4s**; further qualifying kills **reset** the window (never stack, never extend). The old "any 3-kill streak" is gone. |
| **NEW `v21_berserk` Berserk** | **+50% × the missing-HP fraction**, live. Barrier doesn't count as HP. |
| `bleed_4` **Siphon** | 4 tiers, **1 / 2 / 4 / 5 HP** per kill (totals). |
| **NEW `v21_sanguinarian` Sanguinarian** | Barrier = **max(1, floor(20% × finishing damage))**, overkill excluded (CL-19). With Siphon, **only Siphon's** overheal converts, 1:1 after heal multipliers (CL-20). The pool cap (50% max HP) and 4s expiry are A0's. |
| `v18_bloodlust` **Bloodlust** | **+0.1% attack speed per bleeding kill**, for the whole run, capped at +30%. The old damage stack (counted twice, never decaying) is gone. |
| **Attack speed** (CL-22/23/24) | Frenzy, Berserk, Bloodlust and Forge Bloodrush are each a true-rate multiplier inside **one** `effectiveFireInterval`, read by the gun, the HUD and the hedgehog. A pure **`FireClock`** carries the leftover partial frame, so the **observed** rate matches the calculated one: +15% used to play as +11%, and Bloodlust's +0.1% did nothing for about 35 kills. Catch-up is bounded (at most one interval banked). Nothing banks while idle, paused or on level-up. The HUD's ATK SPD row shows the live rate. No interval floor this unit. |
| **Blood Barrier tell** (CL-21) | A bone-white strip with a dark-red edge sits just under the HP bar, its width proportional to barrier / max HP, with a "+N" label. A **fully** absorbed hit flashes the strip only; a partial absorption flashes both. The Iron Maiden / Apex / Erasure gauge rows moved down 4pt to make room. |
| `v18_glass_blood` **Glass Blood** (CL-27/28) | An enemy killed by a Bleed **tick** bursts into **3 blood-glass fragments**: 30% shot damage (min 1), 150pt range, 0.9× speed. They don't crit or pierce, trigger no other on-hit procs, always Bleed a survivor, and earn full `.fragment` credit. They hit and bleed the boss too, and still respect a Braceguard's shield. **Lineage lives on the Bleed:** a primary wound is generation 0 and a fragment inflicts parent + 1. A refresh keeps the **lower** generation, so a fragment never becomes a fresh root, while a genuine primary hit re-roots at 0. Generations 0 and 1 burst; 2 doesn't. Glass Blood is no longer a Chill bridge. |
| Open Vein | Detail states its burst (2 damage, nearby) and the pre-hit bleeding rule. |
| **Synergies** | Open Wounds (×3) is **25%** against bleeding enemies **and bosses**, applied to DoT ticks **before** rounding (CL-25). Red Harvest is unchanged (1 HP), with its copy restated. |

### Runtime facts that shaped the build (from the recon)

1. **Sanguinarian at a literal 20% is zero** on every normal kill: 1–4 HP mobs, with the no-overkill cap. That's why CL-19 sets a floor of 1.
2. **Fire rate snapped to whole frames**, which is why CL-23 carries the leftover time.
3. **Bosses had no kill context and died on two timelines.** The Unmade Star dies inside `takeDamage` and clears the slot there; the other five die 0.7–1.0s later. That's why the hook fires on the lethal hit.
4. **Glass Blood chains through Bleed ticks**, not through its fragments' own kills, which is why the lineage lives on the Bleed.

## Adversarial review (5 lenses × 2 verifiers)

11 findings confirmed, 3 split, 3 rejected. **All confirmed ones are fixed:**

- **Rewards after death (the ruling's own named case).** A reflect kill on the contact that killed Spark paid rewards to a dead player, because `playerDied()` runs at the end of the contact handler. The resolver now also requires `currentHP > 0`.
- **Glass Blood fragments ignored the Braceguard shield.** They now respect it, like every projectile.
- **The Glass Blood DEBUG check couldn't fail**, because eligibility was counted at the burst site. It's now counted where the Bleed death happens and compared with bursts, as Lyra corrected.
- **The barrier strip went stale on max-HP changes and on the death screen.** Both now redraw.
- **The strip's label overlapped the Kinetic gauge.** The strip is tightened and the gauges moved down 4pt.
- **The barrier could sit above a lowered cap indefinitely.** It now trims on the next grant (damage harness P9j).
- **Stale comments; duplicate harness IDs; Sanguinarian's minimum** moved into `GameConfig`.

## Corrective pass — independent review findings (Sep 22)

The independent review returned **FAIL with exactly two bounded blocking
findings**. Both are fixed; nothing else was touched (no A4c work, no
opportunistic cleanup, no change to barrier rules, timer semantics or damage
math). Re-review is still required — see "Remaining gates" below.

**Finding 1 — an existing barrier must reconcile when max HP falls.**
*Root cause:* the pool was only ever clamped inside `BloodBarrier.gain`, so a
max-HP drop (Glass Engine, Mass Tax) left it above the newly valid cap until
some later grant happened to trim it. Reproduction: 100 max HP + 50 barrier →
Glass Engine → cap 25 but the pool stayed 50, so a 45 hit absorbed all 45.
*Fix:* `BloodBarrier.clampToCap(maxHP:capFraction:)` — **state reconciliation,
never a grant**: it only lowers the amount and never starts, extends or
refreshes the expiry. `PlayerStats.maxHP` gained a `didSet` that calls it
whenever max HP DROPS, so every writer is covered (both card paths, Forge Path
nodes, stat picks, reset). A rise never reconciles. The reviewer's case now
reads: 50 → 25, a 45 hit absorbs 25, 20 reaches HP.
*Coverage:* damage harness **P10a–g** — immediate clamp, expiry untouched, the
45-damage split, a pool already under the cap, a max-HP rise, and the
`PlayerStats` hook itself (drop reconciles + expiry intact, rise doesn't).

**Finding 2 — a barrier-depleting hit must still show its absorption tell.**
*Root cause:* `flashBarrier()` started the flash, but the next `updateHUD` frame
called `updateBarrier(0, …)` and hid the strip mid-flash, so exact depletion (and
any hit that consumed the remainder) showed no tell — against CL-21's readability
intent.
*Fix:* a pure `Systems/BarrierTellState.swift` owns **presentation state only**.
The stored barrier value, the damage math and the 4s expiry are untouched: the
pool still reaches zero immediately for gameplay. While an absorption flash is
playing, an emptied pool defers the hide (the strip holds its last width); when
the flash ends, an actually empty strip hides. With no flash running, an empty
pool hides at once, as before, so there is never a persistent zero strip.
*Coverage:* bleed harness **BT0–BT9** — partial absorption, exact depletion, a
hit larger than the remaining barrier, normal zero-hiding without a flash, and
no ghost strip after the tell completes.

### Corrective pass 2 (Sep 22) — re-review findings

Re-review **closed finding 1** (max-HP/barrier reconciliation — untouched since)
and returned one **P2 blocker** plus one **P3 regression introduced by pass 1**.
Both are fixed in presentation state only; the barrier value, damage ordering,
absorption math and logical depletion are unchanged.

**P2 — a same-frame grant→depletion had no drawable tell.** If the pool was
granted and consumed between two HUD draws, `flashBarrier()` unhid a strip that
had never been given geometry or a number: technically visible, nothing to see.
*Fix:* the flash now takes what the hit absorbed —
`flashBarrier(absorbed:maxHP:)` — and draws the strip for that amount when
nothing has been drawn yet, so the tell always has a real shape and "+N". The
gameplay pool still reaches zero immediately; after the tell, an empty strip
hides as before.

**P3 — a refill during the flash could still hide the strip.** A deferred hide
survived a refill, so the flash's end hid a strip that had barrier again.
*Fix:* any positive pool clears the deferred hide (`BarrierTellState.update`).

**Coverage.** The flash's completion became its own callable step
(`endBarrierFlash()`), and a **new `tools/hud-harness/`** compiles the REAL
`HPBarNode` (SpriteKit builds on macOS) and probes what the strip actually
PRESENTS — visible **plus** geometry **plus** its number, which is the condition
a pure-state test cannot see. **16 checks:** same-frame grant→depletion renders a
real tell (H1), exact depletion after a drawn barrier (H2), an over-absorbing hit
where HP also takes damage (H3), the refill-during-flash regression (H4), and
unchanged ordinary behaviour incl. hiding with no flash and no ghost strip (H5).
Pure-state `BT10–BT12` cover the refill rule. **Negative control:** run against
the pre-fix code, H1b/H1c fail with exactly the reviewer's signature
(`visible: true, hasShape: false, label: ""`) and H4b fails on the refill.

## Brandon's gate — what to feel for

1. **Boss kills pay on the killing blow**, for all six bosses. With Siphon, HP jumps the instant the boss breaks. A boss that was already bleeding also sets off Frenzy. Boss Mode stage advances and the run's end shouldn't feel any slower.
2. **Frenzy:** killing a bleeding enemy speeds you up for 4s. Chained bleeding kills keep it going but never make it faster. A one-shot kill (the enemy never bled) does nothing.
3. **Berserk:** fire rate climbs as HP falls, and ATK SPD moves with it.
4. **Bloodlust:** a slow creep, so ATK SPD should read visibly higher at the boss than at 2:00.
5. **Sanguinarian + Siphon:** the strip grows on kills. A hit it fully absorbs flashes the strip, not the HP bar. The strip drains 4s after your last kill. **At full HP, Siphon T4 (5) plus Sanguinarian (at least 1) fill a 50-point barrier in nine kills.** Does that feel close to immortal in a swarm?
6. **Glass Blood:** only enemies finished by a **Bleed tick** burst, not ones a shot finishes. Fragments are short and red, and each outbreak dies out after two hops. Fresh Bloodthirsty wounds legitimately start new outbreaks. The frame rate should hold in a dense swarm.
7. **Attack-speed stress test:** Static + Surge + Glass Engine + Scatter + Frenzy / Berserk / Bloodlust should hold 60 fps. **Compare the shots per second you actually observe with the HUD's calculated rate.** The old 15/s frame-snap ceiling no longer applies.
8. **Open Wounds on DoTs:** its benefit shows on Burn and Bleed **ticks** against a bleeding target. A 1-damage shot still deals 1; that's the accepted limitation.
9. **Executes:** with Exsanguinate plus Execution Protocol, a boss's last quarter takes one ×2, not ×4, and Execution Protocol still covers the 25–30% band alone.
10. **Cards:** faces read cleanly, Blood Price never appears, no Bleed card shows up before Bloodthirsty, and Glass Blood now appears in runs without Chill.
