## Contracts (written before the dependent code)

### Q — the cascade queue and ancestry
- **Q1 Emission (cascade eligibility).** Every CREDITED death of a non-arena-boss enemy emits ONE Chain Reaction burst while the card is owned, whatever its `KillSource` (full, reward-only, sweep or `.chainReaction`). Emission happens in `onEnemyKilled` right after the `killCredited` guard and the removal from `enemies` — so the first credit wins and one death = one burst. The arena boss never routes through `onEnemyKilled` and never emits. Eligibility is independent of the credit tier (C1).
- **Q2 FIFO, iterative, frame-bounded.** A burst is ENQUEUED (origin = the death position, generation, the emitter's identity), never executed inside `onEnemyKilled`. The scene drains the queue once per frame in `didFinishUpdate()` — after update, actions and physics, i.e. after every death the frame can produce — FIFO, in a `while` loop (no recursion, no timers). A burst that kills appends its children to the SAME queue; they drain in the same pass. The drain is outside every `enemies` iteration, so the collection may change safely; each burst iterates a value copy of `enemies` and credits its lethal victims only AFTER its own loop.
- **Q3 Ancestry.** Roots (any source other than `.chainReaction`) are generation 0. A victim's generation is its lethal burst's generation + 1. The first lethal hit wins: the victim becomes `isDying` and `killCredited` at once, so a later burst neither hits nor credits it. Several victims of one burst all get the same next generation (siblings), in `enemies` order. Competing roots drain in their death (enqueue) order.
- **Q4 Targets.** Every `enemies` entry (the arena boss is never in it) that is not dying, not credited, not the emitter (by identity) and whose centre is strictly within the burst's radius (`distance < radius`, today's boundary preserved). A distinct body exactly on the death position IS a target (the legacy position test is replaced by identity). Hittability (phase/vanish) is not consulted — unchanged from today's bursts.
- **Q5 Bounds.** Each enemy is credited at most once, so a drain processes at most (roots + enemies) bursts; no damage or branch is truncated. Only the visual is capped: at most `chainReactionRingsPerDrain` rings are drawn per drain; every burst still hits.
- **Q6 Lifecycle.** The queue is cleared on restart and at every board wipe that empties `enemies` (Event Horizon's erase, the Unmade Star's wipe, the gauntlet stage end): their pending bursts belong to a board that no longer exists. A scene pause holds the queue (no `didFinishUpdate`); a level-up or pause entered mid-frame still drains that frame's queue (the deaths already happened). Picking or losing the card does not touch pending bursts.

### D — damage, modifiers and rounding
- **D1 Ladder.** Tier bases 1 / 2 / 3, radii 35 / 50 / 70 pt (the purchased tier; never generation-scaled). T1/T2: every generation deals the base. T3: `3 × (1 + 0.1 × min(g, 10))` — additive, not compounded (g0 3, g1 3.3, g2 3.6, g10+ 6). Tuning in `GameConfig.Neutral`.
- **D2 One exact amount, one rounding.** Per victim: `exact = base × bonus(g) × (mini-boss ? BossClass.damageScale : 1)` in `CGFloat` — the half applied once, before rounding. Then ONE chance-rounding on a fresh per-victim threshold (`DirectHitRounding`, the CL-70 / N3 / N4 rule, no floor): `basis = chanceRound(exact)`, `dealt = chanceRound(exact × victim.vulnerabilityMultiplier)` on the same threshold; delivered through `takeDirectHit` so the in-node vulnerability is not applied a second time. Expectations: 3.3 → 3.3, a mini-boss's 0.5 → 0.5; a zero stays zero (a hit whose basis and dealt are both 0 is skipped — no flash, no melt).
- **D3 Modifiers preserved once, nothing new.** Vulnerability (the four channels) joins the one rounding (today it applied at the node). Spurhound/Ramplate's punish window and Whiteout's snowman melt apply as on every source (inside the node, unchanged). No ATK, crit, Fire/Forge Breath, Overcharge, Forge offense, Permafrost/Brittle Cold/Open Wounds, shield or Boss DEF dial enters — Chain Reaction is a flat-base Neutral burst (none of those apply to it today either; the shield and the dial are gun/arena-boss only).
- **D4 Consequence, stated:** with a vulnerability present, T1's legacy `Int((1 × v).rounded())` (e.g. ×1.5 → always 2) becomes a chance-rounding with expectation 1.5. This is the brief's single-rounding rule applied to the real chain, not a new modifier.
