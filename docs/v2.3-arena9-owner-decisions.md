# Arena 9 next decisions — proposals, not approved gameplay

October 30, 2026. The selected release is Arena 8 + Arena 9 + Rainbow. Arena 10 and later timed/chasing-wall gauntlets remain separate. The new inert foundation does not depend on resolving these choices; a playable traversal/eligibility unit does.

1. **Retreat and lifetime — recommend full activated-ground retreat.** Allow walking back through previously reached ground. Do not despawn enemies, reachable XP/health or structures merely because the front advances; preserve their normal death, collection, expiry and whole-run cleanup. No forced autoscroll or trailing kill wall. This preserves kiting/pickups and is simpler to prove, but permits backtracking. A limited rear frontier needs a deliberate rule for investment, pickups, projectiles and pending callbacks; it is not just a camera clamp.
2. **Goal/finale — recommend final encounter complete AND visible goal reached, in either order, with one success.** Intermediate thresholds should not demand every straggler's death. Death restarts the whole run; no checkpoint save. Finale identity/composition remains a content decision; this recommendation does not invent a boss. The existing October 20 proposal contains this recommendation, but overarching gauntlet approval did not settle it.
3. **Grouped card eligibility — recommend considering Growth as the first provisional omission, not banning several entire trees by assumption.** Growth's Terra/Tree/flowers intentionally root territory; an advancing stage can strand that commitment. If Brandon chooses this omission, remove dependent choices through the existing eligibility layer, not runtime disabling or silent dead picks. Retain the other six colors as the *eligible pool*, Neutral and the existing secret Panda route for grouped qualification; this does not certify every retained ability. Preserve the ordinary frozen palette roll, five-active-color cap, leave-one-out rule and sticky player ban. With Growth omitted, six candidates can become five after a user ban, yielding four active colors under the existing rule. Rainbow qualifies against the actual frozen active palette, not all six candidates. Confirm that consequence with the eligibility decision. An alternative is retaining Growth with a specifically approved viability contract; do not move Tree or cultivated ground with Spark by default. That moving-Growth concept belongs to possible Arena 19 work.

Qualify remaining spatial behavior as a shared matrix instead of one tree per slice: fixed Lightning Sentry T1–T3 and its central/unlimited-range T4; Echo/Rift origins and long beams; rooted structures; Panda arrival/roll endpoints; summons, pickups and delayed cleanup. A problematic tier need not imply a whole-tree ban. Preserve combat tuning/terrain exemptions unless a specific behavior change is approved. No exclusions are implemented by A9 F0.
