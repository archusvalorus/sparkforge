# A7-4 proposal — The Switchwarden

**Design/spec only; not an implementation kickoff.** Updated October 17, 2026 after Brandon settled the geometry: “Yeah, do the crossing, that's fine” and added “Let's give him a mustache too.” One crossing danger zone and a mustached expressive face are settled owner direction. No hopping is proposed. No runtime, assets, pipeline replacement or publication accompanies this document.

Current foundation: approved three-crossing layout and published Tollkeeper/Railspike composition at `7e95f463da48c955957d15e5465a6cb2cfab7cc9`. Earlier proposal §5 supplied the working name Switchwarden, not an approved boss specification. This document refines that concept; phases, balance and rewards are not approved by the voice discussion.

## Settled geometry: one crossing

The warden declares **one crossing dangerous**, warns, then briefly denies that passage. Spark can withdraw into either adjacent lane, wait outside the marking, or use an alternate crossing. Spark may remain in the same channel; the attack does not require a whole-channel evacuation. The channel-versus-crossing question is closed.

Recommend the **center crossing only for the smallest first DEBUG slice**. Its marking spans the actual central transfer gap between lane centerlines, rather than Tollkeeper's short half-crossing patch. Starting footprint: x = −0.43R…+0.43R, y = −0.18R…+0.18R, validated against the actual solids and live body margins. This covers the central passage while leaving the north and south crossings untouched. Later selection among all three crossings can follow proven placement/readability; it is not necessary for the first slice.

During the tell the marking is harmless and traversable; there is no gate, wall, shove or teleport. During the surge only the marked crossing damages. Both lanes outside the marking remain usable. The boss and crowd must leave a reachable local withdrawal option; the existence of a distant alternate crossing alone is not a fairness proof.

## Simple encounter recommendation

**Declare → withdraw or reroute → surge → recovery.** These are beats of one proposed attack, not approved health phases.

1. **Declare.** The grounded warden plants its feet and points the semaphore at the selected crossing. A purple hatched footprint and sharp boundary identify the exact danger area. Lock that footprint for the attack; no late tracking or surprise second crossing.
2. **Withdraw or reroute.** Spark leaves the marking for adjacent lane space, waits outside it, or takes an alternate crossing. Verify body-clear local withdrawal at ordinary movement speed plus a reaction margin; no mobility card is required. Defer the declaration if the boss/crowd removes that option. Do not shorten the warning when Spark moves.
3. **Surge.** Brief damage only inside the marked crossing. Render and damage footprints agree, including Spark's live body at edges. Recommend at most one damage attempt per declaration, existing shared boss defenses, and no lingering debuff.
4. **Recovery.** The crossing immediately becomes safe again. The signal arms sag and the boss visibly stalls, normally damageable. Maintain a clear attack sightline from adjacent safe space so recovery can be punished without running around an island. No bonus damage multiplier or immunity phase is proposed.

Use ordinary grounded routing between declarations. Validate a compact body and positions near a crossing that preserve escape clearance and a punishable line of sight; the old northern staging reserve is not a fixed combat anchor. Do not park the body across an exit or enlarge it into a monument. Exact anchors/body dimensions require the later design lock.

For the first boss pass, recommend no summoned adds and no concurrent Tollkeeper/Railspike specials or surviving hostile bolts during the crossing declaration. Drain or defer outstanding specials before starting it. This is a proposed encounter policy; the published two-enemy coordinator does not already implement boss ownership. Keep ordinary crowd pressure bounded during withdrawal. Do not use extra phases or overlapping hazards before the single pattern works.

## Character: a foreman still enforcing a dead timetable

**Silhouette:** a compact, broad-shouldered iron signal operator on two planted feet, with unequal semaphore arms framing a prominent pale foundry mask. One arm is a heavy rectangular signal paddle; the other is a shorter articulated pointer. A squat hood and broken collar frame the face. This differs from Marchwarden's plow-and-mast, Linekeeper's narrow standard and Tollkeeper's fork, while retaining stone, iron, ash and railway machinery. Avoid a giant crown, towering siege body or banner silhouette that borrows the Last Rampart's monument role.

**Face and personality:** two large shuttered eyes, readable brow plates, a hinged grille mouth and a **bold curled handlebar mustache** across the pale mask. Render the mustache as two thick charcoal sweeps with readable upturned tips, not hairline detail. Stern, officious and increasingly exasperated when Spark ignores the signal. Give the face enough contrast and space to read at gameplay size; personality comes from large shapes and timing rather than tiny etching or dialogue.

| Beat | Expression and pose | Lighting/readability |
|---|---|---|
| Warning | One brow and mustache tip lift; eyes glance toward the crossing; the signal arm rises decisively | Purple signal and matching floor hatching build together; shape, pose and rhythm supplement color |
| Attack | Eyes narrow in concentration; grille clamps; arms brace | One crisp purple surge cue; leave Spark, crossing edges and safe destination visible |
| Recovery | Brows become uneven, eyes lose their synchronized glare, grille drops into an exhausted vent; mustache and heavy arm droop | Hostile purple extinguishes; mask stays readable in pale neutral light. The inactive pose invites attack |

Keep the face upright/readable independently of ground facing where necessary. Decorative arms and mustache do not imply extra hitbox reach or an unmarked sweep. Blue remains utility and green health; use neither as a generic safe-floor color. Expression supplements the explicit floor warning.

## Actual art workflow

Source inspection confirms existing bosses are primarily procedural SpriteKit `SKShapeNode`/Core Graphics paths: `BossNode` has eyes and mouth; `MarchwardenNode` builds a plow, mast, banner and glowing eyes; `FacetedLieNode` has plate eyes and a crack mouth. More expressive faces do not require changing render technology.

The repo also already supports raster characters: `docs/character-art-playbook.md` documents raster bodies with procedural effects, explicit point sizing, shared registration for frame sets, genuine alpha and 1×/2×/3× imagesets. Panda/lion assets exist in the asset catalog. Therefore “all enemies/bosses must be vector” is not an established technical restriction.

Recommend first proving this stronger silhouette and expressive mask with procedural layers, retaining independent face/arm/glow animation. If the approved character needs painted texture, use the existing character-body raster workflow with procedural face/light/telegraphs; do not replace the art pipeline. Set and check gameplay size and body/hazard readability before commissioning frames. No art generation or asset import is requested by this proposal.

## Smallest bounded implementation recommendation

One compact grounded boss, one center-crossing declaration attack, one behavior pattern, procedural face/mustache/signal poses and DEBUG-only arena access. No hopping, additional health phases, summons, all-crossing selector, public registry/unlock/reward or release work. Retain the working name Switchwarden. Add explicit boss ownership to special arbitration and drain existing hostile bolts before the tell; do not infer that the current two-enemy coordinator already handles it.

**Provisional engineering starting tuning, not owner-approved balance:** 1.5 s warning, 0.35 s surge, 1.5 s unarmed recovery, minimum 4.5 s tell-to-tell. Lengthen warning or defer if real local body-clear exit tests fail. HP and damage can start from a documented existing boss baseline in the later kickoff, without a new damage type. Body size/anchors must preserve both local withdrawal and a recovery sightline; do not commission a larger body first.

The only immediate remaining scope choice is approval of this **center-crossing DEBUG prototype** and authorization to implement/test it. Geometry type and mustache are settled; individual timing/HP values can stay provisional engineering tuning. Additional phases, final encounter pacing, boss arrival and victory reward are separate later decisions, not blockers for this bounded prototype or approvals inferred here.

Later verification covers exact region/body margins, local exits from the marked crossing at baseline speed and device scales, alternate crossing availability, boss/crowd blocking, special/bolt arbitration, shared boss defenses, death/status/pause/restart cleanup and recovery attack access. Keep hands-on readability, personality, fairness and device performance in Brandon's integrated v2.2 arena/enemies/boss/play-systems playtest. This design document makes no runtime or playtest PASS claim.
