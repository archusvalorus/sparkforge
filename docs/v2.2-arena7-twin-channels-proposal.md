# v2.2 Arena 7 proposal — The Twin Channels

**Status: proposed for Brandon’s design review; not an implementation kickoff or design lock.** Prepared October 16, 2026. Authorization covers this proposal only. No Arena 7 runtime changes, commit, push or release are authorized by it.

Brandon confirmed that the completed shot-type fixes were the fixes he meant; that reconciliation is closed. Do not reopen their implementation or repeat the completed Neutral N6 review. The published integration baseline is `fb680ef608ffc2c86cee862b18698f4d9bfd50a1`.

## 1. Recommendation and inherited constraints

Build a battlefield where **holding a lane is useful, but choosing when to cross is the skill**. Recommend two roughly equal north–south lanes separated by two fixed machinery islands, with three always-open crossings: south, center and north. Introduce one crossing-controller enemy first; add one lane-punisher after its interaction is technically proven. These are two proposed roles, not approval of a broad roster.

The Broken March roadmap already sets the title, two broad lanes / limited crossings, and crossing-controller / lane-punisher roles. Stone, iron, ash, rails and ruined machinery remain the visual grammar. Arena 8 owns chambers and migrating pressure; Arena 10 owns the monument-boss culmination. Avoid borrowing those arenas’ defining mechanics here.

Keep dynamic joystick movement and automatic targeting. Fixed solids use the existing rounded-rectangle footprint / manual-displacement contract. No doors, water physics, moving platforms, destructible divider, navmesh, new input or new player debuff is needed. Health orbs remain non-magnetized. Purple signals hostile attacks; blue remains utility, green health and ember player/progression. The shared music deck continues.

## 2. Layout, dimensions and crossings

All coordinates below are **starting design values**, not measured playtest results. Origin is arena center; +y is north. Let R be the live device-aware arena radius. Recommend `radiusScale = 1.15`, matching Splitworks as the starting size: R = 402.5 world points on current phones (base radius 350). These are world points, not screen dimensions; retain the current camera model.

Author ten guidance nodes at x = ±0.43R and y = −0.78R, −0.43R, 0, +0.43R, +0.78R. Declare vertical lane edges and cross-lane edges only at y = −0.78R, 0, +0.78R. This expresses the intended routes; **current runtime guidance scores visible nodes, not graph edges**, so edges alone cannot enforce navigation. Prove actual steering before relying on this layout.

Three crossings are deliberate: the middle offers a short, exposed transfer; the ends offer longer retreats. Two end-only crossings would resemble Splitworks’ single-obstruction loop and increase travel tax. Keep all three physically open throughout combat. Dangerous attacks may temporarily discourage one crossing, but must not seal the map.

Paint each island’s silhouette exactly inside its solid outline; use restrained rail marks to make the crossing mouths legible. Crossings must read through effects without resembling green pickups or blue utility. No damaging environment floor in this proposal.

## 3. Movement, line of sight and placement

- Player, grounded enemies and grounded bosses resolve against both islands after movement, shoves, pulls and teleports. Preserve separate geometry radius and combat hitbox. Test corners, the central gap, large bodies and iPad scaling; a successful center-point path is insufficient.
- Reuse committed waypoint guidance and its cooldown/backtrack guard. It has shipped around one Splitworks footprint; two-island navigation is an unproven dependency. Check all lane sections against goals in every other section, including displacement recovery and occupied waypoints. Tune authored points first. If steering still stalls, return a bounded routing correction proposal; do not silently expand into a pathfinding rewrite.
- Ordinary player/enemy projectiles stop at solids, including fast swept travel; automatic targeting chooses a visible target or holds fire. Ranged enemies route/reposition when occluded. Never let a lane punisher fire blindly through an island.
- Preserve current ability-family policies: Void exceptions pass terrain; auras/global/falling effects retain their current rules; arc/hop targeting requires its existing clear segment. Long Shot T4 projectiles and the corresponding terrain-passing chaos beam remain terrain-passing. RF T5 without that exception is clipped by terrain. Arena 7 adds no new shot compatibility rule.
- Persistent pickups, wells, flowers, summons and grounded destinations must be valid using the shared placement/resolution contract. Use active body margins and a deterministic valid fallback. Chain Reaction retains its existing policy rather than gaining an Arena-specific wall rule.

Recommend four rim deployment arcs, centered at **60°, 120°, 240°, 300°**, each ±10°. They feed the north/south ends of the two lanes without entering inside a divider. Use authored-zone sampling plus footprint/body clearance. Initial waves alternate lane pressure rather than always counter-spawning into the player’s chosen lane.

**New spawn safeguards are proposed work, not capabilities already guaranteed by the helper:** reject spawns within 0.35R of Spark, at a crossing mouth, or in a live hostile attack footprint. If no zone has a safe candidate, defer that spawn; do not pop a replacement at a nearby anchor. This implies spawn selection/scheduling tests. Controller positions are reached by movement after rim entry, never by teleporting a new enemy into the center crossing.

## 4. Two proposed enemy roles

Working names below are recommendations. Neither enemy gains immunity, phasing or a new player status. Both retain normal damage/status/death/XP/Codex contracts. Hostile actions use active-play time and clear on death, restart or arena swap.

### A. Tollkeeper — crossing controller; build first

A compact iron operator with a horizontal forked silhouette. Its verb is **“wait for the crossing pulse, then take the opening.”** Unlike a Linekeeper’s aimed bolt, it announces and occupies a short passage area.

1. Enter from a lane rim and walk to a valid stand-off beside the nearest usable crossing. If unreachable or Spark is not locally relevant, pursue/reposition; never stand behind stone and attack.
2. Stop, plant the fork, and draw a thin purple rectangle on **one half** of that crossing: initial length 0.30R, width 0.10R, clipped to walkable space and visible from its origin. It must not cover the entire central gap or both exits from Spark’s lane section.
3. Telegraph for **0.9 s**, then pulse for **0.35 s**, then show a visibly unarmed **1.2 s recovery**. Suggested minimum cycle 3.5 s. No aim tracking after the tell begins; shape and damage footprint agree.
4. Counterplay: stop short, pass on the unmarked side, use another crossing, or kill/disable the exposed operator. Keep it a fragile shooter-class target rather than a durable barricade; start from the existing 1-HP shooter convention. Do not require a particular card or speed upgrade.

Initial implementation cap: **one active Tollkeeper and one active crossing pulse**. Attack damage begins from the existing ranged damage/scaling contract, not a bespoke percent-health hit. Slow/freeze interrupt or suspend windup coherently; killing it cancels pending and live pulses. One attack can damage Spark at most once. These lifecycle rules need explicit implementation tests.

### B. Railspike — lane punisher; second enemy candidate

A long low chassis with a narrow barrel. Its verb is **“leave the marked rail, then punish the recovery.”** It pressures staying in the same longitudinal strip, not crossing itself.

Walk to a valid same-lane firing anchor with clear line of sight. Paint a purple longitudinal strip, lock its bearing after a **0.85 s tell**, then launch **one fast non-homing bolt** down that bearing. Initial speed 300 pt/s on phones, range at most 1.2R; use existing swept solid collision and projectile damage. Apply the project’s device scaling conventions when implementing. Suggested recovery 1.3 s and minimum cycle 4 s; retain fragile shooter-class health.

Counterplay: sidestep within the broad lane, shelter behind an island, cross during the tell, or close on the immobile shooter. Do not blanket the lane width or lead-track through the last instant. It earns its place only if its long-axis placement and visible commitment feel distinct from Linekeeper; otherwise reuse Linekeeper for the first composition pass and revise this recommendation before adding another node class.

Composition starts with familiar basic melee bodies, then teaches Tollkeeper alone, Railspike alone, and finally one of each. Timing/density/HP escalation remain tuning for a later kickoff, not a full spawn table approved here. Initial pair scheduling must defer a special attack if its damaging footprint plus the other attack removes every local dodge/retreat option. “Another crossing exists elsewhere” is not sufficient safety when it is unreachable before impact. Do not solve this by granting invulnerability or assuming Swift/Magnetic Core/Overclock.

## 5. Later boss fit — reserve the grammar, not the roster

Working concept: **The Switchwarden**, a mobile channel overseer, not a stationary monument. It alternates a committed lane attack with a single-crossing pressure tell, then exposes a recovery window. The player applies the two learned answers: sidestep a line or choose a transfer window. Keep another reachable escape available; no dynamic doors, simultaneous full-lane carpet, forced teleport or adds-dependent immunity.

The northern merge is a staging reserve, not permission to park a large boss across the escape route. Start investigation with body radius ≤0.08R, validate the real boss footprint through every intended crossing, and move the anchor if clearance fails. Boss attacks must use the shared direct damage, vulnerability/status and `PlayerReachHazards` contracts. Boss Mode must load the same geometry and valid start position.

Later boss scope needs its own design lock: identity/name, body size, phase cadence, damage/recovery, adds policy, bell time and victory reward. No roster count, boss asset, skin or final bestiary prose is locked by this concept. Keep Arena 10’s monument fantasy intact.

## 6. Smallest first implementation slice and dependencies

**Proposed A7-1: fixed geometry shell with existing melee enemies, development access only.** This is the next scope to approve after the layout choice. Add the two footprints, waypoints/edges, spawn zones and safe anchors; a restrained floor/solid silhouette and debug overlay; and a bounded internal way to load it. Do not append an incomplete arena to the production registry merely to expose it: registry membership affects unlock healing and expects a real boss identity. No new enemy, boss, save key, reward or public progression in this slice.

Expected homes (to confirm in its kickoff): `Sparkforge/Config/ArenaGeometry.swift`; `Sparkforge/Scenes/GameScene.swift` for the shell/explicit internal loading seam and existing-enemy spawns; `Sparkforge/Utils/GeometryDebug.swift` if the overlay needs general labels; `tools/geometry-harness/main.swift` and the smallest executed steering seam/probe necessary to verify the live routing behavior. `ArenaConfig.swift` is touched only if a non-shipping preview descriptor is necessary; no placeholder boss in `all`. Shell access must not alter normal campaign/Boss Mode selection or release behavior.

Order after A7-1 technical review:

1. **A7-2 Tollkeeper only:** node + config, scene callbacks/spawn cap, attack-state/shape tests and focused independent review. Resolve real hazard reach, cancellation and freeze semantics before mixing attacks.
2. **A7-3 composition decision:** retain familiar filler; add Railspike only if it contributes a distinct answer. Prove attack arbitration and safe response windows.
3. **A7-4 boss design/implementation:** separately approved boss specification using the proven layout and enemy vocabulary.
4. **A7-5 full arena integration:** registry/unlock healing, campaign/Boss Mode, bestiary/portraits, earned cosmetic, copy, audio cues, final cross-system checks. Reuse the existing arena shipping checklist; keep stable persistence IDs and the shared music deck.

Dependencies are layout choice, two-footprint routing/clearance evidence, explicit spawn safeguards, then enemy/hazard lifecycle. Rainbow, general stat rebalance, broad Boss Mode pool changes and future arena systems are not prerequisites for A7-1. Any newly discovered engine defect should be bounded and reviewed separately rather than hidden in content work.

**Human QA is batched into one integrated v2.2 pass with the new arena, enemies, boss and play systems**, per Brandon’s preference. Carry forward J2/Neutral deferred checks: old-save Codex/discovery, level cap/XP Boost grant and banked queue/Overclock, Boss Mode opener, movement and pickup burst feel, beam/Echo lifecycle across pause/background/resume/restart, terrain-passing shots, chain/Whiteout visuals and dense-pattern device performance. ## 8. Owner choices

| Choice | Recommendation | Alternative and consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Crossing layout | **Three always-open crossings**, equal lanes, R scale 1.15 | Two end-only crossings: stronger commitment, longer travel, closer to Splitworks; redraw/test before implementation |
| First enemy | **Tollkeeper**, one active, short half-crossing pulse | Railspike first: simpler projectile reuse, teaches lane dodging before crossing pressure |
| Second role | **Keep Railspike provisional** until Tollkeeper composition proves the need | Start with existing Linekeeper as filler; less new content and avoids redundant shooters |

Approval can select these recommendations and authorize drafting A7-1’s bounded kickoff. It does not by itself approve the boss, final roster, release or runtime implementation. Names, palette details, encounter tuning and cosmetic/copy choices can follow without reopening the fixed-layout premise.
