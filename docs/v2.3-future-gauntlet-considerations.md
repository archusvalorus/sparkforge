# Future gauntlet considerations — direction, not implementation approval

Owner clarification, October 22, 2026: complete Arena 8 before Arena 9. Arena 9 is the first finite left-to-right gauntlet; its later scope must explicitly assess unsuitable rolls and meaningful viability across trees. Any soft bans, replacement choices or moving Growth behavior require their own decisions. No such rules are authorized by the Arena 8 Growth compatibility slice.

Arenas 11–20 are Growth/plant-themed. A Growth zone traveling with Spark or a tree growing on Spark is a possible Arena 19 thematic idea only. It is explicitly not an Arena 9 rule and has no approved mechanics, tuning, implementation or release commitment. Future timers, chasing death walls and other gauntlet gimmicks likewise remain later designs.
