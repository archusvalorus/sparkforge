## Approved package

- Persistent SUPER! countdown. Final 1 active second warning, 0.75 active second crossing, launches every 20 active seconds even empty; restart countdown at launch. Presentation durations remain tunable after owner review.
- Spark-aimed horizontal lane one-third of captured visible battlefield height, with its vertical front traveling left to right; each eligible target hit once. Wall line of sight from the player origin captured at firing; Long Shot T4 permits passage. Center the proposed lane on Spark’s world Y; the final warning follows him until launch. Capture the interpolated lane/origin at launch, clip to viewport intersect board without shifting away from Spark, then freeze. Camera/player movement does not drag that committed footprint. The richer seven-color palette is retained.
- Gameplay holds freeze clock/front; resume without catch-up or re-hit. Death/title/restart cancels; fresh run clears ownership. Boss Mode board replacement retains ownership/primary bonus, cancels old attack and restarts 20s; camera movement alone does not reset.
- Snapshot attacker damage/crit at launch; target defenses at contact; normal per-target crit. 3.5x ordinary-shot basis excludes Rainbow's 2x primary modifier. Retain passive bonuses and full ordinary kill credit/Chain Reaction eligibility. No weapon on-hit statuses, hit meters, primary counters/charges or extra shot/chain generation. Positive damage still melts existing Whiteout snowmen.

## Contracts and limits

The luminous leading vertical line is the damaging front; its trailing colors/text are decorative. A continuous relative-motion test uses the target body radius and shared rounded-box segment math, avoiding endpoint-only tunneling. A processed hit set is keyed by fixture identity and belongs only to one cycle. Dead/unhittable or wall-occluded contact does not count as a damage hit. The captured rectangle is clipped to the fixture board; no full-level hit or off-board callbacks.

Fixture damage uses a declared ordinary-shot basis (10 by default), real DirectHitDamage exact rounding and target-entry Whiteout semantics. The scene never calls GameScene, PlayerStats, UpgradeManager, progression, Codex, rewards persistence or a live enemy node. Kill receipts prove `.capstone` full credit and existing Chain Reaction queue eligibility in a mock sink; this does not prove production reward integration. No engine performance/device/FPS/Switch claim.
