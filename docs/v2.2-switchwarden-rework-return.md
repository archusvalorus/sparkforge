The approved five points are implemented:

1. **Arena size:** only Twin Channels radius scale changes 1.15 → 1.30. The current camera scale is unchanged. Geometry and radius-derived boss footprint grow with the arena; body fraction remains 0.08.
2. **Signature rotation:** north → middle → south, one crossing at a time, alternating N → ram → M → ram → S → ram. A committed attack advances the sequence, including a subsequently canceled attack; an unsafe/denied declaration does not. The middle band retains its old footprint. Outer bands are fitted to actual island extents including corner padding and the circular boundary; old center anchors/rectangles are not simply translated. Existing minimum 1.5-second warning, 0.35-second surge, 1.5-second recovery and minimum 4.5-second cycle remain; the signature can lengthen its warning for a proved ordinary-foot withdrawal as before.
3. **Grounded pursuit:** 80 literal points/second, with stand-off equal to the larger grounded boss footprint + Spark's live radius + 24 literal points. No chasing during tells/recovery. The boss plants at its current safe casting position; if that position cannot support a fair signature, it approaches a proved station at the same speed and plants there. After a ram finishes inside ordinary pursuit stand-off, this repositioning may withdraw without moving closer to Spark, avoiding a stationary post-ram stall.
4. **Ram:** a full 1.2-second amber capsule/arrow tell locks heading at commitment, then straight movement at 300 literal points/second for at most 0.8 seconds / 240 points. Exact swept grounded-body collision stops at the first island/perimeter, with no endpoint tunneling, sliding, bounce or terrain destruction. Early impact receives the full 1.5-second recovery. If the fixed tell cannot prove a local ordinary-foot escape for the live radius/speed, the declaration waits; growth/slowing or external displacement invalidates an armed declaration. Physics contact and swept end-frame contact share one attempt allowance through the ram cycle, including an invulnerable/shielded attempt. Existing contact damage 36 and boss-class damage pipeline are reused, without added scaling. Death/cancellation clears pending damage; the final movement segment remains eligible for the one end-frame attempt even when that segment starts recovery.
5. **Separate composition knobs:** Tollkeeper minimum replacement 12 → 8 seconds; Railspike 16 → 10 seconds. First arrivals remain 15 / 45 seconds. One-each caps, safe spawn deferral, enemy stats/ramping and serialized attack scheduling remain. These are minimum replacement intervals, not guaranteed spawn frequencies.
