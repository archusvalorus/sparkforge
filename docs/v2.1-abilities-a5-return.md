## Internal adversarial review (self-review; 5 lenses)

The rulings-conformance, lifecycle and geometry lenses stalled on
infrastructure the first time and were rerun. All five finished, with **no
HIGH gameplay findings**. Every finding was verified against source. Fixed in
the same bounded pass:

1. **CL-59 conformance.** The draft's 0.5s per-enemy bounce throttle is
   removed. Contact is edge-triggered, so it could only suppress genuine
   re-touches: a bounced enemy returning in about 0.3s would park on Spark,
   unbounced. It contradicted the confirmed reading ("every real
   touch-begin").
2. **Carrier tunnelling (geometry MED).** Two instant shoves in one frame (a
   2-pellet T2 volley, or a bounce plus a hit) could carry a body through the
   Carrier before the frame's resolve, popping it out the far side. Guard's
   shoves now go through a path-tested `guardShove`, which stops at the last
   free point.
3. **Pins skipped (geometry/rulings MED).** Pins were tested only at each
   frame's end point, and never on the step that lands. They are now tested
   along the whole step, landing step included (`RepulseFlight.sweepOverlaps`).
   A Carrier stop lands at the last free point instead of a step short.
4. **Snowman launches (lifecycle MED).** Whiteout could transform a launched
   body on the same hit, or mid-flight. Snowmen now always land.
5. **Airborne attacks.** Launched Anvilborn and Gravemote no longer slam or
   pull mid-flight; their windups freeze until they land.
6. **Kaiju scaling.** The Ironhide ring, the Grounded arc and the Fortify
   blips drew at 3× under the kaiju; they now step aside.
7. **Self-damage rescue grace.** A lethal self-damage rescue now gets the same
   1s grace every other rescue path gives.
8. **Iron Bloom after death.** Iron Bloom can't pulse in the same frame after
   a death.
9. **Fortify's clock.** It now starts at ownership. Before, standing still and
   then picking Fortify granted up to +30 on the spot; the draft said each card
   keeps its own accumulator.
10. **Test quality (the test lens's HIGHs).**
    - The harnesses now compile the REAL `GameConfig.Guard` block, extracted
      from source, instead of a hand mirror.
    - The retaliation arithmetic and the Ironhide, launch and pin predicates
      moved into tested pure functions.
    - 15 wiring checks read GameScene's source for the calls and their order.
    - Added: Apex in the conversion, 1/60s float boundaries, and a positive
      control for EL1.
    - **All 14 of the reviewer's slip-through mutations are now caught.**

## A5 device-watch items (Brandon, Sep 24 — verbatim)

1. Terra + Guard baseline: already very strong, but it did not trivialize Arena 2 before A5. Do not preemptively nerf it.
2. Ironhide + Aegis: percentage ceiling remains 90%, but feel/readability at high mitigation needs device confirmation.
3. Fortify + Grounded Core: verify staying still feels rewarding without turning passive turtling into the dominant answer.
4. Harden/Aegis bounce: because contact damage occurs on touch-begin, bounced enemies can return and create fresh contacts. Verify this feels defensive rather than self-punishing.
5. Thornwall vs bosses: confirm 50% boss scaling keeps retaliation satisfying without deleting bosses.
6. Unbroken Core: 10 seconds of invulnerability + snapped offensive boost + persistent projectile shield is intentionally capstone-level powerful, but must still feel readable.
7. DEF-fed offense: Fortify can feed Aegis/Iron Bloom and can contribute to the Unbroken rescue snapshot. This synergy is intentional unless device evidence says otherwise.

**Seen in the static sim proof (context for the watch list, not verdicts):**

  DEF 36 ordinary melee floored to 1 damage (#3).

  (radius about 350–400).
- **Launches against 1-HP mobs.** Against early waves the shot usually kills
  the body it would launch, so bowling matters from tougher waves and elites
  on.
