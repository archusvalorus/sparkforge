### Notes on the build

- **The Skybeam trap is closed.** The strike check now sits ABOVE
  `updateSkybeam`'s no-lasso early return, so it fires with nothing lassoed.
  "Nearest" is by distance alone (enemy or boss) — not the priority-target rule.
- **Chain Lightning was one hop, always.** The old function took a
  `remaining` count and never recursed, so Arc + Live Wire + Chain Current
  still jumped once. It is now a real loop over the tier's damage series.
- **Overload's immunity is Overload's alone.** Pandas, Erasure's phase lock
  and snowmen neither grant nor respect it. Under constant 2s stun attempts a
  target is stunned ≤ 40% of the time (harness O7g) — the loop CL-2 stops.
- **Coils, pulses and the crown are on the HIT scale** ("50% of your attack
  damage" = half a shot), which on the integer scale rounds up to 1 — the
  same truncation you already accepted for Ember Burst. The Skybeam strike is
  on the ATK scale (300% ATK = 30 at base), as it always was.
- **CL-13 check:** three coils = 1.875 hit-units/s on one target; the
  network at 0.4s = 1.875/s. Exactly level — your 0.4s call is what keeps the
  merge from being a downgrade (harness C4).

## Open items — all ruled (Brandon, Sep 18)

0. **Lethal hits start chains** ✅ **YES** — from the victim's impact
   position, kill or not; an intermediate victim dying doesn't stop the
   remaining jumps. Built with the three boundaries: **one sequence per
   eligible triggering hit** (started in the hit handler / Electro Pulse,
   never from the death handler); **each victim visited once per sequence,
   the original included**; **the basis is the triggering hit's damage before
   any clamp to remaining HP** (the hit's computed `damage`, which is never
   clamped — killing a 1-HP enemy doesn't shrink the chain).
1. **Arena bosses stay stun-immune for v2.1**; elites and mini-bosses keep
   the approved 0.25s / 0.5s. Arena-boss interrupts get revisited separately
   from A4a's DoT support. Overload's detail now reads: "Elites and
   mini-bosses are stunned for 0.25s, or 0.5s with maxed Chain Lightning.
   Arena bosses are immune."
2. **Static Crown** ✅ interpretation correct — one ring, 4s, each enemy once;
   "slow-pulse" was presentation.
3. **Arc Wake / Induction Step dead code** → remove in A7, after confirming
   no remaining references or saved-state dependencies.
4. **CL-15 (partial):** Red Smile arc = 110° at 80pt — recorded for A4.

## Brandon's gate — what to feel for

1. **Chain Lightning:** count the jumps — 1/2/3/4, never the same enemy
   twice; T4 + Chain Current should visibly hit six. Is it LOUD enough?
2. **Overload:** dazed stars; with a maxed chain it should feel clearly
   stronger (35% / 2s) — and a just-stunned enemy should get ~3s of grace.
3. **Lightning Sentry:** coils land near you on sane ground (try it hugging
   the Carrier in the Splitworks); T4 pulls them into one big central coil.
4. **Electro Pulse:** stand still = nothing; move = a zap every 3s.
5. **Static Crown:** the ring rolls outward over 4s, popping what it crosses.
6. **Skybeam T5:** a strike every 5s even with nothing lassoed, and the
   neighbours of the target die with it.
