## What was built

| Piece | Change |
|---|---|
| **Arena bosses take DoTs** (all six: Slag Titan, Quench Warden, Dynamo Choir, Faceted Lie, Unmade Star, Marchwarden) | A Kindle hit on a boss ignites it (Crucible stacks and CL-16 dormant decay included), and a Bloodthirsty roll can make it bleed. Both tick on game time right after the boss's own update. **CL-17: half damage**, applied to the fractional DoT before rounding (so it's a true half). **CL-18: DoT ticks skip the DEF dial**; vulnerability and the HP dial still apply. Each pays out as its own hit, so a DoT death names its channel (Glass Blood's strict "finishing blow is Bleed" rule needs this in A4b). | **NEW `v21_bloodthirsty` Bloodthirsty** (Bleed signature) | "Primary hits: 50% chance to Bleed: 10% ATK/0.5s, 3s" (approved). Only primary hits roll it, and only a SURVIVOR is wounded (ruling 2). Polar Vortex's icicle counts as a primary hit; shards, echoes and summons never do (ruling 3). **Detail** = the approved CL-1 sentences verbatim + "Bosses and mini-bosses take 50% less Bleed damage." `isSignature`, `provides: [.bleedUnlocked]`. |
| `v18_needlepoint` Needlepoint | **RETIRED** (id not reused). Crit-bleed is gone with it. The rest of the Bleed tree is unchanged until A4b. |
| `fire_1` Kindle | Compact line "Projectiles ignite enemies: +0.5 burn DPS, 2s" (it now sits beside a MORE chip; approved). **Detail**: the old line + "Bosses and mini-bosses take 50% less Burn damage." Numbers unchanged. |
| **Tesla/Burn fix** (`BurnState`) | Kindle and the flat tesla source keep **separate timers**. Before this, the tesla re-applied every frame on Kindle's timer, so standing next to a stacked Burn kept it going forever with no Kindle hits (probe: 15.0 damage where 6.2 was due). The tesla can still hold dormant stacks at its own 0.3 DPS (A1's B5b rule). The CL-17 half applies to Kindle's Burn only; the tesla keeps its full damage on mini-bosses. |

A row is used because the enemy rim tell can't carry over: the Titan's rim is already molten orange, the Marchwarden's turns orange as it advances, and the Lie's damage reads are never red or orange. |

### Runtime facts found in recon (the spec didn't know them)

1. **Bosses resisted all status effects by design.** The boss hit handler said so ("the boss resists them"), and no boss had any status state at all.
2. **`BossClass.scaledDamage` can't halve a 1-point tick** (1 → 1, 3 → 2 — the skunk cloud has lived at 67%, not 50%). The Bleed tick is exactly 1 at base ATK, so any boss scale has to be fraction-first. This is why CL-17 reads the way it does.
3. **Base crit chance is 0%**, so without Nick, Needlepoint never applied Bleed. That is why Bloodthirsty landed here.
4. **Burn, Bleed and the tesla shared one accumulator and one `takeDamage`.** A DoT death could never say what killed it. Fixed by the channel split.
5. **The Unmade Star dies inside `takeDamage`** and clears the boss slot on the spot. So status is applied BEFORE the hit's damage, and boss DoT damage is dealt last in the tick.

### Fixes from the adversarial review (5 lenses, 2 verifiers per finding)

- **The Faceted Lie could die invisible.** A DoT tick landing mid-Pane Shift (while it's faded out) killed it, and the whole death played at alpha 0. The root cause predates A4a (`die()` never restored alpha; some untargeted capstones could already do this), but DoTs made it routine. `die()` now stops the vanish and shows the body.
- **CL-17 was halving the tesla field on mini-bosses.** That was an unapproved Shock nerf. Now only Kindle's share of the burn channel scales (harness D8).
- **The boss status row lingered through the death animation.** It now fades with the HP bar.

## Gate rulings (Brandon, Sep 21) — applied

1. ✅ **Copy.**
   - ✅ Details: "Bosses and mini-bosses take 50% less Bleed damage." (Bloodthirsty) and "…50% less Burn damage." (Kindle). It's an initial boss-balance rule, **applied ONCE to the resulting DoT damage**, never to application chance, duration, tick count or unrelated damage like Ember Burst (harness D9: six ticks and the full 3s on boss-class; only the damage halves).
   - ✅ Brandon's Bloodthirsty line, "Primary hits: 50% chance to Bleed for 10% ATK every 0.5s for 3s.", **wraps to 4 lines** on the 17-character card. Beside the MORE chip the face would read "Primary hits: 50% / chance to Bleed / for 10% ATK ever…". Proposed fit, which keeps "Primary hits" and his wording: **"Primary hits: 50% chance to Bleed: 10% ATK/0.5s, 3s"** (Primary hits: 50% / chance to Bleed: / 10% ATK/0.5s, 3s). ✅ Approved.
   - ✅ Kindle's revised line (Brandon asked to see it): **"Projectiles ignite enemies: +0.5 burn DPS, 2s"** (Projectiles / ignite enemies: / +0.5 burn DPS, 2s). ✅ Approved.
2. ✅ **"Killed a bleeding enemy" = bleeding BEFORE the killing damage.** A wound is established first, then capitalised on. Built:
   - `EnemyNode.diedBleeding` (snapshot at death; for a DoT death, the state when that frame began).
   - Bloodthirsty now lands **only on a survivor** of the hit, enemy and boss alike.
   - Red Harvest and Open Vein read the new flag. Frenzy and Bloodlust adopt it in A4b.
   - Glass Blood keeps its stricter rule (`killedByBleed`: Bleed damage itself dealt the kill).
   - `isBleeding` is live status only; no reward reads the corpse.
3. ✅ **The Polar Vortex icicle inherits primary-hit eligibility** (`ProjectileNode.isPrimaryHit`). Its kill still credits as `.capstone` (A0 taxonomy); the shards it shatters into (`.fragment`) don't qualify.
   - **Frequency reconciled: no discrepancy.** Every primary shot is absorbed, and every THIRD condenses into one icicle (`PolarVortex.glacialEveryN = 3`; card: "every 3 shots fire one shattering icicle"). The packet's "every shot becomes an icicle" was loose wording. Frequency unchanged.
4. ✅ **Brightness = active damage.** Since the A4a fix, "Tesla holding" means *preserving*: the tesla deals only its own 0.3 DPS and keeps dormant stacks from fading, but it no longer sustains Kindle's stacked Burn. So those pips now **dim**. The rim still glows for the tesla's own burn, without the per-stack heat. Pips light only while Kindle's stacked Burn is dealing damage (enemies and the boss row alike).
5. ✅ **A boss death earns its rewards.** Ruled now, built as **A4b's opener**: one boss-death chokepoint for all six bosses. Siphon heals; Frenzy and Bloodlust qualify if the boss was already bleeding (ruling 2); applicable death effects resolve once, at the death position. A phase transition or despawn is not a kill, and run completion is never delayed to squeeze value from temporary buffs. It lands with the cards that consume it (Siphon ×4, Frenzy, Bloodlust, Sanguinarian).
