## What was built

| Piece | Change |
|---|---|
| **Active combat** (CL-33) | New pure `CombatPresence`, the ONE shared definition, which Grounded Core reuses in A5. Active means at least one **hittable** hostile (enemy or boss) exists anywhere. Distance, line of sight and gun range don't matter, and phased or vanished actors don't count. It holds for **0.5s** (`GameConfig.Combat.activeLinger`) after the last one stops qualifying. It is computed once per frame, after this frame's DoT deaths. |
| **Hittability** (CL-40) | One shared predicate, `isHittable`: alive, and not `isIntangible`. `EnemyNode.isIntangible` defaults to false, and PaneStalker overrides it while phased. `ArenaBossNode.isIntangible` defaults to false. The Faceted Lie sets an explicit flag at Pane Shift beat 1, in the same window its hitbox is dropped, and clears it on re-entry and at death. Gun targeting is unchanged. |
| **The cycle and the form** (CL-34/42/44) | New pure `RedSmileState`. The 10s cycle is start-to-start and counts only in active combat: lulls pause it, the form's own time counts, and a sub-frame carry keeps it from drifting. Each form runs a full **3s** in or out of combat, with its **own** melee `FireClock` reading the shared `effectiveFireInterval`. The first swing lands on the form's first frame, which gives **6 swings at base**. There is no swing floor, empty swings still happen, and the carry is bounded. Under the kaiju the cycle pauses, and nothing begins, banks or queues. `beginKaiju` ends a live form on the spot. Death, revive and restart zero the cycle and end the form. |
| **The gun during the form** (CL-43) | `updateAutoAttack` returns **before** its clock advances, the same as for the kaiju, so the remainder freezes and no debt builds. `fireProjectile` itself stays open for echoes, fragments, turrets and every other source. |
| **The sweep** (CL-36/38/41) | A new pure `MeleeSector.overlaps` gives **exact** circle-to-sector overlap: 80pt × DeviceScale, **110°** (`GameConfig.RedSmile.halfAngle`), with reach measured to each target's **body**. A wide body counts when its footprint genuinely overlaps, with no per-boss cases and no angle padding. Enemies use their combat hit circle (12 × scale). Each boss declares `hitBodyRadius`, the same as its physics body: Titan 36, Warden 34, Choir 32, Lie 21, Marchwarden 28.9, Unmade Star ≈335. There is no default, so a new boss must declare one. The **Carrier blocks** the sweep through a new **exact** segment test (`ArenaGeometry.segmentBlockedExact`); see review finding 1. |

Bosses and mini-bosses use the same basis, and the Boss Mode DEF dial applies as it does to any direct hit. **Void's only special consequence:** the Braceguard's shield flashes but doesn't halve the hit. Effects are classified **by their card text**:<br>• **Carried:** crit and Lucky Break, both executes (one ×2), Permafrost, Brittle Cold, Open Wounds, the Forge offense with its per-hit charges (Relentless, Cold Fury, Killing Stroke, Warpath), Overload and Whiteout ("Hits…"), Shatter ("…when struck"), Overkill, the Apex and Erasure hit meters (already cooldown-limited), and **Calculated Strike** ("Every 5th direct attack": one swing is one attack, and a 5th swing crits everything it strikes).<br>• **Not carried:** Kindle, Frost Touch and Repulse ("Projectiles…"), Seed Spore Shot ("Your shots…"), Storm Engine, Glacial Condensation, Erasure Echo, Fracture and Mirror Edge's echo (effects that spawn extra projectiles because a shot occurred).<br>• **Chain Lightning fires at most once per swing**, from the nearest enemy hit, at that hit's damage. ## Independent review #1 — corrective pass (Sep 23, F1 + F2 only)

The reviewer accepted the rest of the contract. This pass changed only the two
findings, and nothing else in production:
- no design, geometry or shared hit-handling change;
- the gun's boss path (and its Apex omission, an A7 item) is untouched;
- the Chain-hittability issue is untouched.

**Root cause.** Each contact site called the hit meters inline, and two sites
didn't reach them:
- **F1:** `redSmileHitBoss` registered Erasure only, copying the gun's boss path. So an eligible, injured boss contact never charged Apex.
- **F2:** the Shatter branch of `redSmileHit` returned before `apexRegisterAttack()` and `erasureRegisterHit()`. A landed Shatter contact charged neither meter.

**Correction.** All three contact kinds (an ordinary enemy hit, a Shatter, the
boss) now end in ONE helper, `chargeRedSmileHitMeters(_:)`:
- It gives each meter exactly one registration opportunity through the meters' EXISTING methods, via the pure `RedSmileContact.chargeHitMeters`. Cooldown, capacity and eligibility stay authoritative.
- The Shatter branch still kills through Shatter, still returns early, and still never seeds the chain.
- Boss damage, Bleed, kill credit and the lethal hit are unchanged.

## Independent review: 5 lenses (rulings, lifecycle, kill credit, geometry, harness)

Every finding was checked against source before acting. **Fixed:**

1. **HIGH: the Carrier never blocked the sweep (CL-38).** The shared
   `segmentBlocked` marches at the footprint's half-thickness (~88pt), so any
   segment shorter than that is only tested at its two endpoints. A sweep's
   endpoints are always outside the Carrier, so a swing round its rounded ends
   went through.
   - **Fix:** a new exact segment-to-rounded-box test, used by the sweep only.
   - **Harness:**
     - TB1 blocks the reviewer's counterexample.
     - TB2, the negative control, shows the legacy march lets that same segment through.
     - TB4 agrees with a 0.1pt SDF march on 2,998 random segments.
   - **In-engine:** 2 terrain blocks in the Splitworks proof.
   - **The shared function is NOT changed.** The gun's line of sight, projectile travel and geometry routing all use it, and the gun survives because its per-frame projectile check catches the wall. This is carried to the geometry track below.
2. **Calculated Strike was excluded because of where its code sits** (in `fireProjectile`), which CL-39 forbids. Its text says "direct attack". A swing now counts once, and a 5th swing crits everything it strikes. See **Q1**.
3. **Violet halo and trail on the Star-Crossed skins (CL-47).** The glow is hidden and the trail turns crimson during the form.
4. **Pre-existing, fixed because it hid the CL-47 countdown:** `playerDied` hides the countdown HUD and only resume or revive showed it again, so after **death then Restart** no RED SMILE row (and no other capstone row) ever appeared. `restartGame` now shows it. (The orb indicators have the same bug and are **not** touched; they're carried below.)
5. **Pre-existing, fixed because it mis-scaled every tell:** `player.reset()`'s `removeAllActions` cancelled the kaiju's shrink-back and the victory pose, so dying as the kaiju left the **next run drawn at 3×**. The reset now sets scale 1. The arrival ring is also sized in world points.
6. **Smaller fixes:**
   - A stale crimson row during a card-pick kaiju (the row now refreshes when a form ends).
   - A paused countdown flashing "urgent" forever.
   - Forms ended by death were never logged.
   - A wrong 90° default on the wedge.
   - One shared `halfAngle` in `GameConfig`.
   - The harness now checks the cycle carry (RS8: dropping it drifts 0.06s by the third start).
   - The FireClock checks are honestly labelled (GF tests the freeze mechanism; the scene's guard is proven by the sim ledger).
   - The card-draw test is bounded instead of `while true`.

**Checked and found correct** (full lists in the reviews):
- Kill credit and collection safety: struck bodies are snapshotted and re-checked, and Overkill, Shatter and Chain kills mid-swing are skipped.
- The "bleeding before the killing damage" invariant, the Star's synchronous death, and Glass Blood never bursting on a melee kill.

## Q1–Q7 rulings (Brandon, Sep 23)

These are recorded here and in closure table §B3. Each one confirms what is
already built, so no code changed.

1. **Calculated Strike: KEEP.** Each Red Smile swing counts as one direct attack, and every fifth swing crits every body that swing strikes.
2. **Mirror Edge: CONFIRMED EXCLUDED.** A Red Smile swing is not a shot or a projectile, so projectile-echo behaviour does not trigger.
3. **A same-swing Bleed, then a Chain kill: KEEP.** Normal timing semantics hold: if the sweep applied Bleed before Chain Lightning dealt its killing damage, the target counts as bleeding before the killing damage.
4. **Relentless: ACCEPTED AS RULED.** It keeps its per-hit reset, even when one sweep strikes several targets.
5. **The 12pt runtime body: KEEP for A4c.** Red Smile uses the authoritative runtime hit body that existing combat uses. Collision size is never inferred from rendered sprite width. Any large visual/hitbox mismatch is logged separately (see Carried: the Ramplate).
6. **Chain Lightning onto a phased target: A7.** A4c does not change the shared Chain Lightning targeting. The broader issue is logged as: *secondary-chain targeting does not respect shared hittability.*
7. **The older lifecycle fixes: KEEP BOTH.** The restart/countdown-HUD fix and the kaiju-death scale reset are bounded compatibility fixes, directly required for correct A4c lifecycle and presentation.

## The questions as asked (answered above)

- **Q1 · Calculated Strike (CL-39).**
  - **Built:** a swing is ONE "direct attack"; every 5th swing crits every body it strikes, the way a critting piercing shot crits everything it passes.
  - **Alternative:** count per target.
  - **Rec:** keep per swing.
- **Q2 · Mirror Edge** ("Attacks can echo once for less damage").
  - **Built:** left out, under CL-39's "effects that spawn extra projectiles because a shot occurred don't trigger", since its echo is a delayed projectile copy.
  - **Rec:** confirm.
- **Q3 · A swing's chain lands on bodies it just Bled.**
  - The single Chain Lightning jumps to any enemy except its source, usually another body the same swing hit and Bled microseconds earlier.
  - If the chain kills it, that kill counts as "killed a bleeding enemy" (Frenzy, Bloodlust, Red Harvest, Open Vein). That meets the letter of the Sep 21 rule and matches the gun's per-hit behaviour, but Red Smile makes it near-certain.
  - **Rec:** keep. **Alternative:** the swing's chain skips bodies the swing struck.
- **Q4 · Relentless while sweeping.** "Normal per-hit semantics" means a multi-body sweep changes the Relentless target on every hit, so the node is mostly inert during the form and the first hit wipes the gun's stacks.
  - **Rec:** accept, as ruled.
- **Q5 · What "physical footprint" means (CL-41).**
  - **Built:** the combat hit circle, the same circle the gun hits.
  - The Ramplate's shield plate draws about twice as wide as its 12pt circle.
  - **Rec:** keep the hit circle.
- **Q6 · Chain Lightning can still hop onto a phased PaneStalker.**
  - This is existing Chain behaviour, shared with the gun: it filters dying enemies only. The sweep's own hits never land on one.
  - **Rec:** log for A7.
- **Q7 · Two pre-existing one-line fixes shipped inside A4c** (review findings 4 and 5), because each broke an A4c tell. Keep them, or split them out?
- **Documented, not changed:**
  - If the Red Smile cycle and the kaiju come due on the **same frame**, the form starts (one swing), then the kaiju ends it. This is conformant (the kaiju wasn't active yet) and rare.
  - Apex horns and the Polar Vortex hat stay drawn on the form, as they do on the kaiju.
