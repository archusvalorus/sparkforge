## 2. The two R4 contracts (written before the dependent code; checkpoint §C1/§C2)

**C1 — the dynamic virtual-volley cadence.** One virtual volley clock accumulates *active beam time* (`.playing`, a target in reach, not suspended) and compares it each frame to the **current** `I = playerStats.effectiveFireInterval`. On reaching `I(now)` one virtual volley fires and `I(now)` is subtracted; the leftover carries, bounded to one interval (the gun's `FireClock` rule, CL-23); at most one per frame. A speed change applies at the next comparison — seconds already accumulated are kept, never rescaled, never released as a burst. Each virtual volley is a primary volley: `recordShot()` (Storm Engine's every-3rd window, whose +2 lanes hold until the next virtual volley), ONE `volleyCounter.register(emitted: true, …)` (Blackhole every 5th at the LEAD lane's terminus, Shadow Edge every 7th), and a Gravity Well once per PARENT lane at its terminus. Damage ticks are a separate clock every 0.5 s of active beam time (same carry/bound rule); within a frame the volley resolves first. The tick reads D and I at the tick. *Executed:* BX8 (cadence, carry, idle-ready, hitch bound, reset), BX1 (live I and D). *Source:* NB3 (frame order), NB4 (wells / Blackhole / Storm window).

**C2 — truthful presentation of the inert Warp Shot and Mirror Edge.** A beam has no travel for Warp's curve and no shot for Mirror Edge to echo, so both are inert in beam mode (no invented bonus). The copy says so on BOTH sides: Rapid Fire's detail ("Warp Shot, Mirror Edge and Erasure's Echo don't affect the beam" — *[superseded by §10: now "Warp Shot and Mirror Edge don't affect the beam; Erasure's Echo re-fires each beam lane as a normal shot."]*), and one sentence inserted into Warp Shot's and Mirror Edge's details ("No effect on Rapid Fire's chaos beam."), before each card's approved closing sentence. ## 7. Bounded choices (each tested; changeable by ruling)

1. **One hit per (parent lane, target, tick)** carrying the summed share k/n; primary when the centre branch is among the contacts, else fragment (BX6).
2. **Exact beam arithmetic:** no integer prefix, no crit floor 2; one rounding on a fresh block threshold; the shield folded into the block as an exact fraction (the gun applies it after the block with a floor — the beam must not resurrect zero). 3. **D** = the gun's integer standard-shot basis (N0 §5), so Overclock's +0.15 enters D only where it crosses an integer, exactly as for the gun.
4. **Riftline rank** = order of distance along the lane among the lane's contacted targets; +25 % range lengthens the beam.
5. **Rider copies + an equality pin** instead of refactoring the A7b-pinned handlers.
6. **Termini** = first of range, first terrain (unless T4), arena boundary; Storm's extra lanes take N3's next outward step.
7. **Contact half-width 4 pt; terrain bisection 14 steps** (≤ 1000/2¹⁴ pt error) — config, not ruled numbers.
8. **Family** in the sidecar: "passive stat, direct projectile" — the beam stops at terrain like a shot; it is not a reality-piercing "void beam".
