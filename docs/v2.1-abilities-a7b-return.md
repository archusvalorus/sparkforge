**G1.7 `endSnowman` no longer cuts an Overload stun.**
- **Today:** `endSnowman` zeroes `stunTimer` (EN:623). The dazed stars hide only when `OverloadStunState`'s own timer ends (EN:879-881).
- **A snowman can be stunned:** neither `rollOverload` nor `applyOverloadStun` has a guard against it.
- **Sequence A:**
  - a 3s snowman takes a 1s Overload stun at 2.5s;
  - at 3.0s the enemy walks again with its stars up;
  - its immunity starts at 3.5s.
- **Sequence B:** the gun and the sweep roll Overload *before* `takeDamage` (GS:11808 → 11840; 12235 → 12253). So a T3 melt on an elite zeroes the 0.25/0.5s stun in the same hit: the proc is wasted and its immunity still starts.
- **Why it's determined:** it breaks the approved principle that one control "can't lengthen or cut short another" (closure table R2) and CL-2's stun durations.
- **Fix:**
  - `isStunned = stunTimer > 0 || snowman.isSnowman`;
  - `becomeSnowman` stops writing `stunTimer` (and drops its now-unused binding, for the zero-warnings gate);
  - `endSnowman` stops zeroing it.
  - Rec delivery: a pure `StunHold` in Systems with executed checks and 4 mutants.
- **Awareness:** a same-hit Overload on a melting elite will now hold it 0.25/0.5s after the melt.
