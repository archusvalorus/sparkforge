## Implemented

- `Arena9StageDefinition`: immutable finite, positive, representably nondegenerate rectangular world/start and stable, ordered encounter IDs/thresholds. No authored stage length, counts, roster, trigger cadence or balance defaults.
- `Arena9StageBounds`: outer actor-body clamp, camera-follow clamp and captured visible-footprint intersection for arbitrarily translated long stages. Oversized viewports center/clip per axis. These are queries, not GameScene movement, internal-wall resolution, retreat enforcement or a live camera adapter.
- `Arena9EncounterLedger`: one owner identity, explicit once-only activation, separate reached-threshold query, generation-scoped tickets, explicit ownership retirement and reset rejecting stale callbacks/reused IDs. It does not spawn, expire entities, delete pickups, award success or persist data. A ticket is bookkeeping, not proof that actual deferred combat callbacks have been integrated or cleaned.
