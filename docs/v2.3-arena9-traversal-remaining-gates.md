# T1 source preparation and remaining qualification gates

This candidate is not yet app-built, Simulator-qualified, final-reviewed or ACP approved. Host model/palette checks are source proof, not gameplay acceptance.

The explicit `--arena9-traversal` route selects a memory-only profile before services initialize. It uses actual GameScene on a provisional 3000×700 open rectangle, two threshold packets (each one Spurhound and one Linekeeper), free retreat and a visible end goal. Counts, HP/XP and spacing are technical fixture parameters, not final composition/balance. Completion is a once-only local test event/badge; there are no production rewards/unlocks or progression writes. Death cancels ownership; normal ad revive is inaccessible in this isolated profile and is not certified.

Growth alone is omitted before the player ban and leave-one-out roll. No ban setting is rewritten. No/Growth ban produces five of six colors; banning another unlocked color produces four of five. Neutral, bridges and Rainbow R0 snapshot semantics remain. Rainbow itself remains unavailable live. `--arena9-controlled-loadout` injects explicit Swift T1 and Spark1000HP technical fixtures and labels them (incoming damage/death is unchanged; normal unflagged traversal HP remains standard); it does not alter palette, future card availability or bans. Automated initial checks use this controlled ordinary-shot setup. Ordinary all-tree drafting remains a separate compatibility gate, not qualified by this setup.

Shared spatial matrix before unrestricted owner handoff:

- Echo currently inherits the one-room adapter, so its origin is on the full stage's perimeter. A bounded local camera/encounter origin would change its sampling domain and needs a concrete owner decision; full-stage offscreen births must not silently become the accepted final policy.
- Panda currently inherits whole-rectangle outer-rim arrivals and inset roll bounds. On a long level the travel distance differs substantially from Arena 8. Qualify actual arrival delay, ordinary/Samurai/kaiju/avalanche attacks, safe rests, pickup landings and cleanup before accepting that domain. A moving/local arrival domain is a new policy, not an engineering default.
- Rift, Sentry, Gravity Well/Null Bloom/Blackhole and long beams need actual scene/artwork-versus-damage tests over distant stage positions. Sentry network still targets all living spawned enemies; inactive encounters do not yet exist, and no new global-retirement rule is added.
- Growth is intentionally absent, so no traveling Tree or moving garden is authorized. Other families may be stronger/weaker here; lack of qualification is not evidence for an omission.
- Test final-clear and goal in both orders, early kill between deferred packet spawns, earlier stragglers, retreat/return, normal pickups/expiry, once-only collection, pause/background/level-up, death/reset/Title and isolated profile retention on phone/iPad. Verify Float-stored body roots near ±stage edges with fractional collision radii.

Final release forward pressure, irreversible retreat boundary, enemy composition/balance, authored level art and final goal/reward presentation remain open. No production flag, save migration, archive or release is part of T1.
