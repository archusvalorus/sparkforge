## Device-watch items (Brandon, Sep 24, verbatim)

1. Warp Shot feel, whether slowing a projectile is satisfying enough relative to its damage payoff.
2. Probabilistic damage rounding, whether 125%/150% reads naturally rather than feeling arbitrary.
3. Anomaly + Singularity boss pressure, especially the projected max-HP damage stacking.
4. Fear routing, particularly Splitworks and other constrained geometry.
5. Well performance, four simultaneous wells plus absorbed/returned projectiles and decomposition.
6. Shadow Edge readability, especially shot 35 when Blackhole and blade happen together.
7. Riftline, whether its +25% range and falloff create a clear identity.
8. Indigo player-Void language, making sure it separates cleanly from danger purple.
9. Red Smile + Void, ensuring those new primary-hit interactions feel intentional rather than like invisible bonus soup.

**Context from the build and the sim proofs, for the items above:**

- **Item 3 (boss pressure).**
  - Anomaly's 3% has a 2s cooldown, so it contributes about 0.75–1.5% of max HP per second.
  - Singularity adds 1%/s while the boss overlaps a hole, and never stacks across holes.
  - In the base proof, the Dynamo Choir (90 HP) took 2-point chunks, with a visible cooldown after each.
  - Riftline's pierce carries the seeded lead shot *past* a boss, so the Blackhole opens behind it. Boss decomposition then rarely overlaps (0 in the proofs). That is correct per CL-76 ("final stop position"); it is worth a look on device.
- **Item 5 (performance).** Every proof ran at 60fps, with 4 live holes and 118 evictions over 80s. Gravity Well spawns a hole on *every* spent primary pellet, so the 4-hole cap churns constantly: Gravity Wells are evicted first and Blackholes survive.
  - Consequence: **Dead Circuit rarely reaches 3 matter on one hole.** The base run had no natural collapse in 60s; Splitworks had one in 80s.
  - The collapse path itself works: with a TEMP collapse-at-1 it fired 19 times in 40s, r=75, 15 damage.
  - This is a balance observation, not a defect.
- **Item 9 (Red Smile + Void).** Swings stack Anomaly and roll Void Horror on surviving targets, and never advance the volley counter (harness WR7/WR8).
- **Anomaly vs normals.** Normals have 1–4 HP and a base shot deals 1, so at **T1** a normal dies before its 4th stack (4 hits kill a 4-HP body). At T1 the erase almost never fires. It bites at T3 (2 stacks per hit, erasing on the 2nd hit) and against elites and bosses. The proofs agree: T3 builds erased 23–49 enemies; the T1 build erased 0.
- **Void Horror.** It rolls only on a hit the target *survives*. With T3 Anomaly erasing on the second hit, survivors are scarce, so fears are rare: 2–3 per minute in the proofs, each exactly 0.5s. A Horror build without T3 sees more.

## Gate questions as asked (Q1–Q6) — ✅ ruled Sep 24; see "Implementation-gate rulings" below

The text below is what went to Brandon with freeze 1.

- **Q1 — Trapped bodies are Repulse-immune.**
  - *Built:* a body held in a black hole isn't shoved or launched by Repulse. Without this, a Guard+Void build's T3 launch would throw every trapped enemy out of the hole on the next hit.
  - Other displacements (the Harden/Aegis bounce, pandas, deer, Erasure, the lethal-save push) still move a trapped body. If it lands outside its live hole, it is released (CL-79 "detachment").
  - *Alternative:* Repulse acts too; a launch ends the trap, as a stronger control.
- **Q2 — What CL-70 rounds.**
  - *Built:* an A6 fraction scales the shot's **legacy** (truncated) damage, and only that fraction is rounded by chance: `round(max(1, Int(multiplier)) × fraction)`.
  - This keeps "no retrofit" and makes the rulings literal: Riftline never climbs (100% → 75% → 56.25%), and a full-speed Warp shot deals exactly a plain shot's damage.
  - The first cut rounded the whole product. That leaked the older trees' fractional multiplier (Overcharge, Forge nodes), so at a 1.5 multiplier Riftline's 2nd body out-damaged its 1st. Two lenses found it independently.
  - *Alternative:* round every body of a Riftline shot, the first included.
- **Q3 — What "primary shot" means for the icicle.**
  - *Built:* the Polar Vortex icicle is primary for Phase T2 (R3), Anomaly and Void Horror (it already carried primary-hit eligibility from the A4a gate ruling), and volley emission and lead.
  - It is **not** primary for **Warp** or **Gravity Well**: CL-14 says "the gun's own primary projectile"; CL-87 says well generation "remains" pellet-scoped.
  - An earlier build stretched R3 to both; it's reverted.
- **Q4 — How far Erasure's purple goes.**
  - *Built:* Erasure's in-world rings and pops are now indigo: Rift Burst, Implosion, Phase Lock, Displacement, the Unstable pop and the T5 player-erase ring.
  - These stay unchanged as UI: the Unstable HUD gauge (0x9B59B6), the "VOID COLLAPSE" countdown label, the full-screen erase tints, and the Void card-face tag colour.
- **Q5 — Which target a returned shot aims at.**
  - *Built:* the nearest hittable target the hole can **see**; with none visible, the nearest of all. CL-4's "nearest valid enemy", read with line of sight, because a shot aimed round a Carrier corner just dies on the iron.
- **Q6 — Dead Circuit matter in overlapping holes.**
  - *Built:* an eligible kill inside several live holes feeds every one of them. Eligible means full credit; the reward-only burst and prune don't count.
  - *Alternative:* feed only the youngest hole, or only the oldest.

**Also disclosed (read at build; flag any to change):**
- **Damage sources.** The collapse's "150% ATK" is `effectiveAttack × 1.5` (15 at base), the same basis as Rift Burst. The blade and returned shots use the effective projectile range and speed, so Riftline's range and Long Shot extend them.
- **The volley counter** runs from run start (VC3). The first Blackhole or blade can arrive fewer than 5 or 7 volleys after the pick.
- **Anomaly credit.** An erase is credited to the hit's own source, so an icicle erase credits `.capstone`.
- **Mini-bosses in holes.** They're pulled at 50% (`debuffScale`), but the ×3 impair slow hits them at full, like every other slow.
- **Terminal holds.** A normal that survives its 2s terminal hold (vulnerability can't make it survive; shields could) can be recaptured by the same live hole.
- **Dead Circuit damage** reaches snowmen (area damage). Holes never pull or trap them.
- **Hittability.** Boss decomposition pauses while the Faceted Lie is vanished. Every new A6 effect respects `isHittable`.
- **Edge handling.**
  - Holes are clamped inside the arena wall.
  - A Boss Mode stage change clears them.
  - The seeded lead shot opens its hole *after* the hit's own kills, so it never feeds itself matter.
  - Returned shots and blades never get a zero heading.

### F1: fear moved an enemy toward Spark at the arena rim

**Root cause.** `FleeRule.goal` clamped the escape point to `R − footprint`. For a body already in the outer band, that lands radially *inward*. With Spark inward on the same ray (e (345,0), Spark (320,0)) the goal was (336,0), and the step closed on Spark. The step itself had no separation guard, so a route node leading toward Spark would be followed too.

**Correction (`VoidState.FleeRule`).**
- **The clamp.** `radiusLimit` is `max(R − footprint, current radius)`, so fear never pulls a body inward to satisfy the clamp.
- **The goal.** Away from Spark, else the nearest deviation (±30°, ±60°, ±90°, better-separated side first) whose straight line **keeps away** (`keepsAway`). Distance along a line is convex, so a line that doesn't approach at its start never approaches. None found → nil.
- **The step (`fleeStep`).** First the route-aware steer target, **if** that step doesn't reduce separation, stays within the radius limit and doesn't enter solid. Otherwise the nearest heading to straight-away that satisfies all three. Otherwise it **holds**.
- **The scene** passes Spark, the steer target and the Carrier test. A step may not sink into the footprint band (margin `footprint − 0.5`), so a slide along a face is the step's own doing, not the resolve's.
- With no goal, the steer pass aims at the body's own spot, which avoids committing a route node toward Spark.
- Route reset when fear ends is unchanged (M1, internal review).

**Behaviour to note (inside F1's mandate).**
- A feared body pinned on a Carrier face with Spark on its open side, or at the rim with Spark radially inward, now **slides** along the face or rim while keeping away. Freeze 2 held it still there, because its truncated goal was its own position.
- This is CL-80's approved "slides along geometry", and F1's rule 4: choose a geometry-valid alternative that preserves or increases separation.
- Open-ground flight is unchanged: straight away at the chase speed (F1f).

### F2: an evicted hole kept acting inside its own damage pass

**Root cause.** Dead Circuit's pass checked `isLive` once per hole. A victim's death resolves synchronously (`onEnemyKilled` → death bursts → Null Bloom → `spawnVoidWell` → eviction of the oldest Gravity Well, the hole that was ticking). The inner loop then kept damaging later bodies.

**Correction.**
- `VoidWellPass.forEachWhileLive` re-checks the hole's liveness before **every** body. The scene also re-checks each body's containment inside it, because matter can grow the hole mid-pass (as freeze 2 did per body).
- A hole that tips into collapse mid-pass is no longer live, so it stops ticking. Its collapse then fires once as before.
- `end()` stays idempotent; the cap and eviction policy are unchanged.

**Tests.**
- **F2a:** the Reviewer's repro, executed with the real `VoidWellState`, `VoidWellCap` and `VoidWellPass`. Four live holes, the ticking oldest Gravity Well evicted by v1's Null Bloom, and **v2 and v3 are never damaged**.
- **F2c:** a hole that stays live damages every body.
- **F2d:** pending collapse stops the tick, and the collapse ends it exactly once.

- **F2b:** a negative control documenting the defect.

### F3: Riftline's realized damage could rise on later bodies

**Root cause.** Each hit drew its own threshold, so an exact 2 → 1.5 → 1.125 could realize 2 → 1 → 2.

**Correction.** `A6Rounding` holds **one threshold per projectile**, drawn at launch (`ProjectileNode.a6Rounding`). Every A6 fractional resolution of that projectile uses it: `shotBaseDamage`, and the returned and Shadow Edge resolvers. The legacy whole-number base and the A6-only scope are unchanged (no retrofit).

**Why it's monotone.** With a fixed threshold `r`, `realized(x) = ⌊x⌋ + [r < frac(x)]` is non-decreasing in `x`. So a projectile whose exact damage falls (Warp over flight × Riftline per body) never realizes a rise.

**Why it's unbiased.** Over the threshold's uniform draw, each hit's mean is its exact value.

**Tests.**
- **F3a:** 2 → 1.5 → 1.125 across 1,005 thresholds, including ~0 and ~1: never a rise.
- **F3b:** Warp + Riftline at 6 multipliers: never a rise.
- **F3c:** 3,000 seeded random projectiles: never a rise.
- **F3d:** per-hit means of 2, 1.5, 1.125 and the Warp value: unbiased.
- **F3e:** a blade's three bodies take identical damage.
- **F3g / F3h:** the production `A6Rounding`, executed. One projectile is one threshold (200 identical resolutions); its sequence never rises; across projectiles the mean is 1.5.
- **RV3:** the scene and node wiring; no per-hit draw anywhere.
- **F3f:** a negative control documenting the rise.
