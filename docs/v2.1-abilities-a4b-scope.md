## 1. Runtime facts that change the design

1. **Sanguinarian, as written, grants 0 barrier on every normal kill.**
   - `finishingDamage` is capped at the HP left (`EnemyNode.swift:744`).
   - Normal mobs spawn with 1–4 HP after `healthScale` 0.75 (`GameConfig.swift:1351-1357`; spawn table at 8177-8190).
   - So 20% works out to 0.2–0.8 per kill.
   - `BloodBarrier.gain(Int)` rejects any value ≤0, and a rejected grant also skips the expiry refresh (`Systems/PlayerDamagePipeline.swift:185-192`).
   - The ATK scale doesn't fix this, because the no-overkill cap is what limits it.
   - On its own at an exact 20%, absorbing one base melee hit (25 damage) would take about 125 kills inside a rolling 4s window.
2. **Siphon's overheal is what actually builds the barrier.**
   - `heal()` returns nothing and clamps the overflow away (`PlayerStats.swift:283-288`).
   - With Sanguinarian, Siphon T4 at full HP converts 5 per kill, which fills the 50% cap (50 at 100 max HP) in 10 kills. That is effectively +50% EHP in a swarm.
   - The Forge Restoration/Defiant heal multipliers truncate to nothing at 1/2/4/5 HP.
3. **Bosses have no kill context.**
   - They have no `KillSource`: rewards come only from the six `onDeath` closures (8259, 9407, 9463, 9549, 9597, 9713).
   - They record no `finishingDamage`: each `takeDamage` subtracts inline (`BossNode.swift:473-503`, `QuenchWardenNode.swift:571-597`, `DynamoChoirNode.swift:681-707`, `FacetedLieNode.swift:698-727`, `MonumentBossNode.swift:189-224`, `MarchwardenNode.swift:535-547`).
   - So every boss death is full credit by construction. The burst/sweep credit tier can never apply to a boss.
4. **Boss deaths happen on two different timelines.**
   - The five mobile bosses call `onDeath` from an SKAction 0.7–1.0s after the lethal hit (for example `BossNode.swift:507-531`, `MarchwardenNode.swift:549-565`). SKActions keep running during `.levelUp`, `.paused` and `.dead`; only `update()` is gated (4645).
   - The Unmade Star calls `onDeath` synchronously inside `takeDamage` (`MonumentBossNode.swift:212-219`). Its `boss = nil` fires `resetBossStatus()` (80-82, 10878-10887) before `takeDamage` returns, so any read of `bossStatus` after the hit sees a wiped state.
   - The Star's `takeDamage` returns `true` when it is already dead (`:190`). Credit must never be keyed off that return value.
5. **A boss killed by a Bleed tick can't be detected when it dies.**
   - `updateBossStatus` writes the ticked status back before it deals the damage (10906-10910).
   - For a fresh Bleed, or one refreshed exactly on the beat, the final tick and the end of the Bleed happen in the same `tick()` (`BleedState.swift:69, 74-77`). So `isBleeding` already reads false on a killing final tick.
   - A refreshed Bleed can deal its last tick up to one interval before it expires, then run out silently (bleed-harness K2c).
   - Today only the DEBUG ledger knows the boss died to Bleed (10934).
   - The fix needs two things: a `wasBleeding` snapshot taken before the tick (mirroring `EnemyNode.swift:673`), and the damage channel published before each DoT `takeDamage` (10923, 10932), because the Star dies inside that call.
6. **Legacy Frenzy is really +25%, triggers on any three kills, and sits outside the shared fire interval.**
   - It applies `effectiveInterval *= (1 - 0.20)` (5201-5203) off a streak counted on every full-credit kill (10160; `PlayerStats.swift:867-881`).
   - `effectiveFireInterval` (`PlayerStats.swift:794-798`) never includes it, so neither does anything that reads that property: the HUD (`StatHUDNode.swift:89`) and the hedgehog (4300-4331).
7. **Legacy Bloodlust counts every kill, counts its bonus twice, and never decays.**
   - It records a stack on every full-credit kill (10161).
   - The bonus is added inside `effectiveDamageMultiplier` (`PlayerStats.swift:932`) and multiplied in again in both hit handlers (10663-10665, 10826-10828).
   - Through `effectiveAttack` it also inflates Bleed ticks (`PlayerStats.swift:364`), the %ATK capstones and Everglow.
   - Removing it, and Blood Price (`PlayerStats.swift:934-936`), lowers all of those for builds that took either card. That is intended, but players will notice.
8. **Fire rate is locked to whole frames.**
   - The fire loop resets `timeSinceLastShot = 0` (5208) at 60 fps (`GameViewController.swift:31`), so the real period is ceil(interval×60) frames.
   - Effect: +15% plays as +11%, +30% plays as +25%, and Bloodlust's +0.1% steps do nothing until about +3.45% (roughly 35 qualifying kills).
   - The main gun has no minimum interval. A fully stacked build sims to 15 shots/s × 5 pellets, about 75 projectiles/s, roughly double today's ceiling.
9. **Open Wounds does nothing on almost every hit, and never applies to bosses.**
   - It computes `Int(damage × 1.15)` (10659-10661) on 1-damage projectiles (10607).
   - At +15% nothing changes below 7 damage. At the spec's +25%, nothing changes below 4.
   - The boss handler (10805-10866) has no Open Wounds line.
10. **Exsanguinate duplicates Execution Protocol, and the two stack to ×4, bosses included.**
    - Both are "×2 below 30%" (`UpgradeManager.swift:859` sets `executionThreshold`; `:1480` sets `executionProtocolThreshold`).
    - They are applied one after the other (10638-10645; on bosses 10818-10824).
    - The spec copy says 25%.
11. **Hemorrhage overwrites crit instead of adding to it, which erases Deadeye.**
    - It sets `critMultiplier = 3.0` (`UpgradeManager.swift:1165`), wiping Forge Path Deadeye's +0.10 (`ForgePathManager.swift:172`). That breaks the "rungs add, never re-set" rule (`UpgradeManager.swift:79-80`).
    - Nobody can see it today: crit is `max(2, Int(d×mult))` (10631/10812), so the loss is 0 below 10 damage.
    - Base crit is 0% (`PlayerStats.swift:295`), so without Gouge or a Forge crit node, Hemorrhage is a dead pick.
12. **Glass Blood chains through Bleed ticks, not through fragment kills.**
    - A survivor bled by a fragment dies to a tick 0.5–3s later through the `.statusTick` path (4945-4950, 4973-4975), then bursts again.
    - Iceburst's generation limit rides on the projectile (`ProjectileNode.swift:44`, checked at 10088), so it would not stop this chain.
    - On 1-HP mobs the chain ends by itself. On 2–4 HP swarms and elites it keeps hopping.
    - So the generation limit has to live on the enemy's Bleed.
13. **Projectile fragments pick up every on-hit effect.**
    - `handleProjectileHit` (10587-10798) runs:
      - a crit roll made at fire time, with no gate (5324)
      - Kindle ignite plus a Crucible stack (10680)
      - Frost Touch (on by default, `ProjectileNode.swift:41`)
      - Overload, Shatter and Seed embed (10750)
      - Whiteout, the Apex and Erasure meters, and Chain Lightning on every hit (10795)
    - Bloodthirsty won't roll (`isPrimaryHit` is false), so Glass Blood needs its own Bleed-on-hit hook.
    - The radial alternative, `damageEnemiesInRadius` (7724-7745), credits kills as `.burst`. That means XP only, no on-kill effects, and no Bleed hook.
14. **Burn damage is applied before Bleed** (`EnemyNode.swift:692-703`). If both would kill in the same frame, the kill counts as Burn and Glass Blood does not burst.
15. **Dropping Glass Blood's `.chill` tag** makes it offerable in every Bleed run (bridge rule, `UpgradeManager.swift:386-392`). It also removes a card from the Chill ladder; Chill keeps 8, so ×7 is still reachable.
16. **There is no Blood Barrier tell, and a hit the barrier fully absorbs looks like an HP hit.**
    - Nothing on the HUD shows the barrier.
    - A barrier-only hit still plays the HP bar's `flashDamage` (9875, 10475, 10559), the damage sound, the shake and `playHitFeedback` (5905).
    - The kickoff requires a placeholder tell to ship with the mechanic (`docs/v2.1-abilities-kickoff.md:29-31`).
17. **Existing bug that loses a boss reward.** `bossDefeatedThisRun` is only set in `onDeath`. If the player dies during a mobile boss's 0.7–1.0s death animation, `playerDied` (11284-11289) drops the boss forge-XP bonus, while the arena unlock still lands afterwards.
18. **Nothing in the runtime supports Red Smile yet.**
    - `PlayerNode` has no facing vector; the only candidate is `GameScene.lastMoveDirection` (164, set at 4695-4697).
    - No "active combat" concept exists anywhere.
    - There is no arc test, and no way to hit an enemy with melee: all hit resolution is inline in `handleProjectileHit` and needs a `ProjectileNode`.
    - "Void damage" means nothing at runtime. The only Void rule that changes damage is `erasureVoidTouched` bypassing the Braceguard shield (10674-10678).
    - A sweep that bypasses physics would hit the vanished Faceted Lie and phased PaneStalkers (`FacetedLieNode.swift:599`, `PaneStalkerNode.swift:120`).
19. **Build mechanics.**
    - Nine Bleed cards use trailing-closure syntax, so adding `requires:` or `detail:` means converting each one to a labeled `apply:`: `bleed_2` 1161, `bleed_3` 1168, `bleed_4` 1175, `v13_execution` 1476, `v16_blood_price` 1509, `v16_open_vein` 1516, `v18_bloodlust` 1780, `v18_glass_blood` 1811, `v18_red_smile` 1832.
    - bleed-harness E2 hard-codes Bleed = 12 (`tools/bleed-harness/main.swift:250-256`), and D4 tests the legacy ×1.5 hook (171-173).
    - Six harnesses compile the real PlayerStats and UpgradeManager against `tools/signature-draw-harness/Stubs.swift`. Every new GameConfig value read there must be mirrored (the Bleed block is at :104-109; the BossClass stub only has `dotScale`).
    - `GameConfig.DamagePipeline` is not mirrored, so barrier tuning has to be passed in from the scene.
    - The atlas regex (`tools/generate-card-atlas.py:84`) can't show a card that requires two capabilities.
20. **Name and mechanic overlaps.** The new Berserk card shares a name with Forge Path's "Berserker" (`ForgePathManager.swift:142`). The new Frenzy works much like Forge Bloodrush (a kill gives +5% ×3 for 4s, hard-coded numbers at 10106-10110).

## 2. Proposed unit split

**Recommendation: move Red Smile's form change and melee into its own unit, A4c, right after A4b.**

Why:
- **Size.** Red Smile alone is about 700–900 lines including its harness. That matches a whole recent unit (A4a was 1137+/116−, A3 924+/125−), and A4b without it is already unit-sized (A4a-sized, by estimate).
- **Its open questions are separate.** Red Smile needs about 12 rulings (§4b), and none of them block A4b.
- **The dependency only runs one way.** A4c uses two things A4b builds: the single attack interval (for the sweep's cadence) and the boss-death chokepoint (so melee boss kills pay rewards).
- **It keeps the risk contained.** A4c is the unit that touches the most-run code path (pulling hit resolution out of `handleProjectileHit`) and adds a third body form next to the kaiju. Its gate is about feel and readability; A4b's gate is about numbers.
- **The dual gate can already be tested.** The Void signature exists today (`void_3` Phase provides `.voidUnlocked`, `UpgradeManager.swift:1245-1264`). A4c can ship "Void" as a hit flag that A6 extends later.

**A4b contains, in order:**
1. The boss-death chokepoint, plus one kill-reward resolver shared by enemy and boss kills (opener).
2. The single attack interval, with Frenzy, Berserk and Bloodlust on it.
3. Siphon ×4, heal overflow, Sanguinarian and the Blood Barrier tell.
4. The Glass Blood rework.
5. Gouge, the Hemorrhage fix, Open Vein's tooltip detail, Blood Price removal, the synergy re-tooltips, and bleeding-boss support for Open Wounds and Bloodhound (if approved, D14).
6. `requires: [.bleedUnlocked]` on every non-signature Bleed card, the capstone included.
7. **Red Smile stays on its legacy effect and copy**, gaining only `requires: [.bleedUnlocked]` so it is never a dead pick. The legacy low-HP hook (4939-4941, 10903-10905) stays until A4c.

Counts after A4b: Bleed 12 → 13, pool 78 → 79 (−Blood Price, +Berserk, +Sanguinarian).

**A4c contains:**
- The Red Smile form, the 10s start-to-start cycle, the melee sweep and the pure arc test, with a harness.
- The dual `requires: [.bleedUnlocked, .voidUnlocked]` and the atlas dual-requires regex fix.
- A `KillSource.melee` case.
- Removal of the legacy low-HP hook.
- The placeholder body with a facing tell.
- **One shared "active combat" definition**, which A5's Grounded Core (Q-G3) reuses.

## 3. Boss-death chokepoint: proposed applicable-effects list

### How it works (recommended)
- **Hook.** Add `onLethalHit: ((Int) -> Void)?` to `ArenaBossNode`, wired once in the `boss` didSet.
  - Each of the six bosses calls it once, synchronously, in its lethal branch: at the top of `die()` for the five mobile bosses, and right after `isDead = true` (before `onDeath`) for the Star.
  - It passes `finishingDamage = min(dealt, healthBefore)`, where `dealt` is the `challengedDamage` result (after vulnerability and the DEF dial).
- **Scene side.** `onBossKilled(finishingDamage:)`:
  - It has an A0-style once-guard, reset in `resetBossStatus`.
  - `diedBleeding = pendingBossDoT?.wasBleeding ?? bossStatus.bleed.isBleeding`
  - `killedByBleed = pendingBossDoT?.channel == .bleed`
  - `updateBossStatus` sets `pendingBossDoT` (the pre-tick snapshot plus the channel) before each DoT `takeDamage`, and clears it afterwards.
- **Why the lethal hit, rather than `onDeath` or a wrapper around every damage call:**
  - Rewards land on the killing blow for all six bosses: no 0.7–1.0s lag, and never during a level-up or pause.
  - It runs before `bossStatus` is wiped.
  - It costs the same six boss edits that `finishingDamage` already needs.
  - A wrapper would have to cover 21 call sites in 19 functions, and one missed site silently drops rewards.
  - A DEBUG line in each `onDeath` logs "boss died uncredited" if the guard never fired.
- **Run completion is never delayed.** The chokepoint is synchronous with no waits, and it never touches the gauntlet advance (4780-4782), `gauntletCleared` (8608) or `resolveFalseEnding` (8846).
- **Invariant to keep.** Every Bleed source (Bloodthirsty, Glass Blood fragments, and Red Smile later) bleeds the boss only after its damage, only if the boss survived, guarded by `!isDead`. That way `bossStatus` at the lethal instant always means "bleeding before the killing damage".
- **"Death position"** is `boss.position` at the lethal instant. For the Star that is its anchor, deep inside its body. The board is already wiped when it arrives, so bursts there are effectively cosmetic, but they fire anyway: one rule, no special case.
- **If Spark is already dead** (`gameState == .dead`, for example a reflect kill on the contact that killed him), player rewards are skipped.

### Despawns and other paths that never count as a kill
- **`restartGame`** (11614-11807) removes the boss and sets `boss = nil`: a despawn. A corpse still mid-animation has already been paid at the lethal hit.
- **Phase changes:** the Star's `onHalfHealth`/`phaseDidChange` (`MonumentBossNode.swift:198-211`), the Faceted Lie's Pane Shift, enrage and `isAdvancing`. None of them set `isDead`.
- **`advanceGauntlet`** (8581-8605) despawns leftover adds and enemy shots with no credit; the boss is already dead by then.
- **The monument's arrival wipe** (8220-8221) despawns enemies with no XP.
- **`eraseArena`** (6876-6900) despawns enemies silently (those are not kills). It **kills** the boss with `takeDamage(b.health)` (6884), so the chokepoint fires: that one is a real kill.
- **A second `takeDamage` on an already-dead Star** returns `true` (`:190`). Not a kill.
- **Run-enders that don't involve a boss kill:** `moteScriptedKill` (8782-8802), `resolveFalseEnding` (8846-8865), `forcePlayerErased` (6904-6921), `gauntletFinishRun` (8661).
- **Boss Mode interstitial** (8600-8604, 2.4s): not a kill. Frenzy and barrier timers keep ticking through it and are never extended.

## 4. Decisions needed before building

### 4a. A4b (blocking)

1. **CL-19** · **[PARAM] Sanguinarian's value on the real damage scale.**
   - **Rec:** each full-credit kill grants `max(1, Int(0.20 × finishingDamage))`.
   - On bosses, 20% of the HP actually removed (after vulnerability and the dial, no overkill), including executes. The pounce execute grants about 18; Erasure's full-HP erase hits the 50% cap.
   - The detail copy adds "(at least 1)".
   - **Why:** a literal 20% is 0 on every normal kill. This follows the existing convention that small fractions on the hit scale round up to 1 (`docs/v2.1-abilities-handoff.md:89-93`). The alternative, carrying fractions exactly, gives 0.2 per kill, which is close to invisible.
2. **CL-20** · **[PARAM] Which heals turn into barrier.**
   - **Rec:** Siphon only (Q-B3), 1:1, measured after the Forge heal multiplier. Red Harvest, Apex lifesteal and Bloodfed, health orbs and regen do not convert.
   - Tweak the linked line to "With Siphon: Siphon's overhealing becomes Blood Barrier."
   - **Why:** the approved line can be read as "any overheal". That would turn 20-HP orbs into barrier and undercut the non-magnetized health-orb canon.
3. **CL-21** · **[ARCH] Barrier tell, and what a fully absorbed hit looks like.**
   - **Rec:** a bone-white strip with a dark-red edge in the free ~24pt under the HP bar. Its width tracks barrier/maxHP (at most 105pt at the cap) and it shows "+N".
   - A fully absorbed hit flashes the strip instead of the red HP flash, and keeps the sound and shake.
   - **Why:** a placeholder tell must ship with the mechanic; green, red, blue and purple are already taken; and today a fully absorbed hit looks exactly like losing HP.
4. **CL-22** · **[PARAM] How the attack-speed bonuses combine.**
   - **Rec:** fold every live bonus into `effectiveFireInterval`, each as its own real-rate divisor: `base × fireRateMultiplier / ((1+Bloodrush)(1+Frenzy)(1+Berserk)(1+Bloodlust))`. No minimum interval for now.
   - **Why:**
     - Each card's percentage stays true whatever else is owned, which is the stated convention.
     - There is one source of truth for the HUD, the hedgehog and Red Smile's cadence.
     - The worst case is ×2.58 on top of permanent cards (×2.1 if added together instead), and at 60 fps both hit the same 15 shots/s ceiling.
5. **CL-23** · **[ARCH] Keep the leftover time between shots.**
   - **Rec:** yes: `timeSinceLastShot -= interval`, clamped to one interval.
   - **Why:** without it, Frenzy's +15% plays as +11% and Bloodlust does nothing for about 35 kills. Side effect: every existing attack-speed build gets a small real buff (they finally fire at their advertised rate).
6. **CL-24** · **[ARCH] The HUD's ATK SPD row.**
   - **Rec:** show the live rate, including Frenzy, Berserk and Bloodlust (Bloodrush is already live).
   - **Why:** it is the only visible sign of Berserk and of Bloodlust's slow climb.
7. **CL-25** · **[PARAM] Open Wounds.**
   - **Rec:** 25%, since the approved copy says 25% and the code has 15%.
   - It applies to bleeding enemies and bleeding bosses, at full strength (not boss-scaled).
   - DoT ticks on a bleeding target get it before rounding, through `StatusDoTs`' fractional scale hook, the one the legacy Glass Blood/Red Smile bonus used.
   - Direct hits stay integer, so the bonus is visible from 4 damage up.
   - **Why:** the copy is the contract, and DoT ticks are where +25% survives rounding at base damage.
8. **CL-26** · **[PARAM] Exsanguinate.**
   - **Rec:** 25% threshold, per the approved copy. Keep it stacking with Execution Protocol: ×4 below 25%, ×2 from 25–30% with Execution Protocol alone, bosses included.
   - **Why:** A0's preserve-legacy rule, with the copy deciding the number. Watch the boss's last-quarter melt at the gate.
9. **CL-27** · **[PARAM] What a Glass Blood burst is.**
   - **Rec:** 3 fragments shaped like seed fragments (range 150×DeviceScale, speed 0.9×, no pierce, no crit), each dealing 30% of shot damage (min 1). Their kills credit as `.fragment`, which is full credit.
   - They Bleed survivors 100% of the time at `bleedTickDamage`, and carry no other on-hit effects.
   - Fragments hit and Bleed the boss (survivors only).
   - **Why:** this matches the seed and icicle fragments. Full credit lets fragment kills feed Siphon, Sanguinarian and Frenzy. Stripping the other effects keeps it a Bleed card rather than a Kindle/Chain/Overload proc engine.
10. **CL-28** · **[ARCH] Stopping the Glass Blood chain.**
    - **Rec:** store a generation on the enemy's Bleed. A Bleed from a Glass Blood fragment is its source's generation +1. A death bursts only while the generation is below 2 (the same limit Iceburst uses). When a Bleed is refreshed, the lower generation wins.
    - **Why:** the chain re-enters through ticks 0.5–3s later. Without a cap it can sweep a 2–4 HP swarm.
11. **CL-29** · **[PARAM] Boss-kill effects not yet ruled.**
    - **Rec:**
      - Red Harvest, the Forge Path kill triggers and Apex Bloodfed (+1) apply.
      - Open Vein, Glass Blood, Null Bloom, Ember Burst and Chain Reaction fire once at the death position (for the Star, its anchor).
      - `killCount` and bat growth do not count a boss.
      - No rewards if Spark is already dead.
    - **Why:** one resolver is shared with `onEnemyKilled`, none of this delays completion, and counting bosses in `killCount` would change forge XP and the results screen.
12. **CL-30** · **[ARCH] Fix the lost boss forge-XP bonus in the chokepoint.**
    - **Rec:** set `bossDefeatedThisRun` at the lethal hit.
    - **Why:** it is one line, safe to run twice, and today dying during the 0.7–1.0s death animation loses the bonus.
13. **CL-31** · **[PARAM] Hemorrhage.**
    - **Rec:** change it to `critMultiplier += 1.0` as a bug fix, copy unchanged. Log a "needs a crit source" gate for A7.
    - **Why:** the rule is that rungs add and never re-set, and this restores Deadeye's +0.10.
14. **CL-32** · **[PARAM] Bleeding bosses in the rest of the tree** (A4a's suggestions).
    - **Rec:** yes to both:
      - Open Wounds gets a boss arm (covered in D7).
      - Bloodhound treats a bleeding boss as a candidate, measured to its surface, with the nearest bleeding target winning.
    - Calling `apexRegisterAttack` on boss hits waits for A7, since Apex is "no changes".
    - **Why:** bosses bleed since A4a, and "bleeding enemies" should include them.

**Defaults the build will use unless Brandon objects:**
- Berserk reads live `currentHP / maxHP`. Barrier doesn't count as HP, and changes to max HP (Glass Engine, Bloodfed) are followed.
- Frenzy, Bloodlust, Siphon and Sanguinarian count only full-credit kills (A0 taxonomy).
- Buffs run on game time and keep ticking through Boss Mode's interstitial.
- Burn stays ahead of Bleed in the damage order.
- A snowman melt counts the HP it had left as the finishing hit.
- Open Vein and Glass Blood both fire on the same Bleed-tick death.
- Card ids: `v21_berserk` and `v21_sanguinarian`, one tier each. Gouge keeps the `bleed_1` id. Blood Price's id is retired and not reused.

### 4b. A4c (Red Smile): must be settled before A4c, not A4b
*Superseded Sep 22: these a–l drafts were re-checked against source and moved into the closure table as **CL-33…CL-48** (`v2.1-ability-closure-table.md` §B3), which is where they're ruled. Several recommendations changed with new facts (on-hit table, Chain once per swing, terrain, the active-combat definition).*

- **a. What "Void damage" means.** Rec: a hit flag that bypasses the Braceguard shield (the Void-Touched precedent) and ignores walls (Ruling 3). A6 attaches Phase T2 and Anomaly to it later.
- **b. What "active combat" means.** Rec: a valid auto-aim target exists, i.e. the gun would be firing. Define it once and reuse it for Grounded Core in A5.
- **c. Facing.** Rec: `lastMoveDirection`, which persists while Spark stands still ("facing still matters", CL-15), plus a facing tell on the placeholder body.
- **d. Damage basis for "2×".** Rec: `shotFractionDamage(2.0)`, which is 2 at base. Not 200% ATK, which is 20 and ten times too high.
- **e. On-hit rules.** Rec:
  - A melee hit counts as a primary hit for the damage chain (crit rolled per target, both executes, Open Wounds, Exsanguinate) and for Kindle.
  - No Chain Lightning per target: that would start 8 chains per swing.
  - Swings don't advance the per-shot counters (Storm Engine, Calculated Strike, the glacial counter, A6's 5th/7th-attack triggers).
- **f. Phased and vanished targets.** Rec: respect intangibility with a new `isHittable` check.
- **g. Reach and arc.** Rec: reach scales with `DeviceScale.gameplay`, and the arc test widens by the target's body radius (needed for the Star).
- **h. Kaiju overlap.** Rec: the kaiju wins, and Red Smile's clock pauses.
- **i. Void synergy tag.** Rec: add `secondaryTag .voidT`, so the bridge counts toward both ladders.
- **j. The gun during the form.** Rec: its clock freezes (kaiju precedent). Echoes scheduled before the form may still fire.
- **k. Empty swings.** Rec: swing on cadence even when nothing is in the arc; the arc effect is itself the facing tell.
- **l. Mini-bosses.** Rec: full damage (Red Smile is not a capstone).

## 6. Gate checklist (Brandon, on device)

1. **Boss kills pay out on the killing blow.**
   - With Siphon, HP rises the instant the boss breaks, not a second later. A boss that was bleeding also sets off Frenzy right then.
   - Try the Titan, Warden, Choir, Lie, Marchwarden and Unmade Star.
   - In Boss Mode, stage advance and the run's end should feel no slower.
2. **Frenzy.** Killing a bleeding enemy speeds you up for 4s. Chained bleeding kills keep it going but never make it faster. Kills of enemies that weren't bleeding do nothing.
3. **Berserk.** Fire rate climbs as HP falls, and the ATK SPD row moves with it.
4. **Bloodlust.** A slow creep: the ATK SPD readout should be visibly higher at the boss than at 2:00.
5. **Sanguinarian.**
   - The strip grows on kills.
   - A hit it fully absorbs flashes the strip, not the HP bar.
   - It drains 4s after your last kill.
   - With Siphon at full HP it fills in about 10 kills. Does Siphon T4 + Sanguinarian feel close to immortal in a swarm?
6. **Glass Blood.**
   - Only enemies finished by a Bleed tick burst; a shot finishing a bleeding enemy does not.
   - Fragments are short and read as Bleed, and the chain dies out after two hops.
   - Frame rate holds in a dense swarm.
7. **Attack-speed stress test.** Static + Surge + Glass Engine + Scatter + Frenzy/Berserk/Bloodlust should hold 60 fps. Check that keeping the leftover shot time feels right on existing speed builds.
8. **Crits and executes.** Gouge crits read clearly; Hemorrhage crits hit harder. With Execution Protocol, Exsanguinate's ×4 last-quarter melt on a boss shouldn't feel abrupt.
9. **Bleeding bosses.** The Apex Bloodhound bat goes after a bleeding boss, and Open Wounds visibly adds to damage on it.
10. **Cards.**
    - Card faces read cleanly beside the MORE chip.
    - Blood Price never appears.
    - No Bleed card shows up before Bloodthirsty.
    - Glass Blood now appears in runs without Chill.
11. **Expected softening.** Builds that took Bloodlust or Blood Price hit a little softer, Bleed ticks included. This is intended.
