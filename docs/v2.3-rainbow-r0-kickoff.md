# v2.3 Rainbow R0: isolated qualification, offer and exclusion proof

Status: owner-authorized bounded R0 implementation, October 20, 2026. No live Rainbow drafting or effects. Baseline published HEAD: `312bf4d251d1f4fe68398433ee1508cb18d20ae3`; the full dirty-worktree baseline and index are captured outside the repository.

## Decided; do not relitigate

- Qualify by acquiring at least one card representing every color in the frozen run palette, excluding Neutral. Signatures and Growth count; bridges count both active tags; repeat tiers, Neutral and Panda add no breadth. Smaller palettes need no new permanent unlock gate.
- Qualification makes a manual one-pick special reward eligible. It does not activate Rainbow. First offer uses the next fresh ordinary level-up spread with an unprotected seat. Preserve gateway, in-progress capstone and Panda guarantees. Retain on rerolls; after a decline, reoffer every other level while eligible, carrying an entitlement forward when every seat is protected. No extra card, level or cap extension. Exclude automatic random-opener grants and bonus draws.
- Rainbow, Rapid Fire T5 and Polar Vortex T4 use the approved first-acquired-wins fork. Lower tiers remain; other existing card/capstone rules remain. Revalidate at acquisition, including stale Extra Pick alternatives.
- Later effects work: 2x ordinary primary-shot damage; separate 3.5x ordinary-shot basis without Rainbow's 2x; every 20 active seconds; visible HUD countdown and periodic prismatic SUPER! spectacle. Owner now approved full-height left-to-right traveling contact, one hit per eligible enemy, within the captured visible battlefield; walls block by line of sight from the captured player origin at firing, except Long Shot T4 passage. Those effects are not part of R0.

## Representation and seam contract

The proof reward is a dedicated one-rank `breadthReward` descriptor, **not** an UpgradeCard with a no-op apply closure and not a tenth regular Neutral card. It is absent from the catalog and every production draw. R1 must implement truthful catalog/UI representation and complete effects together before enabling a live offer.

The planner consumes final seats with explicit protection reasons and ordinary owed-pick count. R0's harness instruments a temporary copy of the real draw to record its actual scheduled claims, without changing production source. Later integration must supply these claims directly and update gateway bookkeeping from the final visible spread; inferring protection from card tags alone is insufficient. The planner returns a seat and commitment receipt; it neither draws cards nor grants levels nor applies gameplay effects.

Call snapshot refresh only after completed successful acquisitions. It recomputes breadth from real acquired non-secret cards. The run owner must call reset alongside UpgradeManager.reset and construct a new snapshot of its newly frozen palette. A mismatching palette fails closed until explicit reset. Each newly owed ordinary level-up uses its actual level; rerolls reuse that level. Bonus/random-opener calls do not advance the normal offer schedule. The same level cannot create a new Rainbow entitlement after qualification mid-spread.

## Definition of done

Deterministic, executed positive and negative coverage; meaningful fault controls; unchanged live drafting/catalog and preserved old fixture bytes; relevant regressions passing; independent review status recorded against exact candidate identity. Native compilation is required for the new compiled policy source; no simulator/gameplay or performance claim. R0 proves an off-live-path policy, not the full Rainbow feature or future integration correctness.
