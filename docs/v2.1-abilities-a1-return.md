### The Fire bucket (Q-F1)

`fireDamageMultiplier = 1 + fireDamageBonus` multiplies **Fire-owned**
damage and nothing else:

- **Burn** — applied at ignition (`effectiveBurnDPS`), so Spreading Flame's
  half-strength Burn carries it too.
- **Ember Burst**, **Everglow** pulse + eruption.
- **Inferno Crown** (Fire ×7 arena burn) — classified Fire-owned under
  Q-F1's "classify any other Fire-tagged effect consistently."

Not Fire: the projectile that ignites (Q-F1 explicit); Relay Burn's arcs
(Shock); the Shock ×5 tesla field, which only *rides* the burn channel.

### Burn stacks (Q-F2 + CL-16) — `BurnState`

- First application = stack 1. Only a **Kindle hit** adds a stack: one per
  3s per enemy (the limit starts at ignition), cap 5. Spread and other
  sources re-ignite but never add; a burning enemy never stacks on its own.
- Each stack deals full per-stack DPS; per-stack DPS and duration keep the
  legacy strongest / longest rule.
- **Dormant stacks:** when Burn ends its damage stops, the stacks stay and
  fade one per 2s. Any new application reactivates the survivors and resets
  decay progress; the 3s limit keeps running through all of it. None left
  → the next application starts at one. All on game time.
- **Without Crucible the cap is 1 and Burn is exactly the pre-A1 model**
  (harness B2 compares total damage against a transcription of `a893457`).
- The tesla field is a flat DPS: `max(kindleDPS × stacks, flatDPS)` — it
  can keep stacks from fading, but never multiplies by them.

**Placeholder tell:** the rim burns hotter per stack; from 2 stacks a row of
ember pips counts them above the enemy; dormant = pips dim, rim gone cold.
Pips are built lazily, so only enemies that reach 2 stacks pay for them.

## Brandon's gate — what to feel for

1. **Kindle first:** no Fire card other than Kindle appears until you own it.
2. **Crucible on a mini-boss (1:30 bell):** the rim gets hotter, ember pips
   climb 2 → 5, never past 5, roughly one every 3s while you keep hitting it.
3. **Stop hitting it:** Burn gutters out, pips dim and drop one at a time
   (every 2s); hit it again and the surviving pips relight.
4. **Forge Breath** should make Burn/Everglow visibly stronger — and should
   NOT make your shots hit harder any more.
5. **Cauterize:** under 25% HP you tick +5 every 3s; heal above 25% and the
   clock restarts.
6. **Glass Engine:** feel whether double fire rate is worth half your HP.
7. Pause → tap a Fire chip: the detail panel shows the full wording.

Debug console: `[A1] Burn reached N stacks`, and `burn[stacks+=… max=…]` in
the run-end `[A0]` summary.
