## What A4a + A4b built that A4c builds ON

- **DoTs** (`BleedState`, `StatusDoTs`): Burn and Bleed are separate channels
  with fractional carries; a DoT death names its channel; boss-class takes 50%
  (CL-17) fraction-first; DoTs skip the Boss Mode DEF dial (CL-18); Open Wounds
  rides ticks before rounding (CL-25); Bleed carries a Glass Blood generation
  (CL-28).
- **Kill rewards**: ONE path for enemies and bosses —
  `GameScene.resolveKillRewards` / `resolveDeathBursts`, both fed by a
  `KillContext`. "Killed a bleeding enemy" means bleeding BEFORE the killing
  damage; Glass Blood needs a Bleed tick to land the kill. No player rewards
  when Spark is dead **or at 0 HP before `playerDied()` runs**.
- **Boss kills**: `ArenaBossNode.onLethalHit` fires once, synchronously, in each
  boss's lethal branch with `min(dealt, healthBefore)`; `BossKillLatch` credits
  once; a DEBUG backstop logs any uncredited death. **A new boss MUST call it.**
- **Attack speed**: one `effectiveFireInterval` (Frenzy, Berserk, Bloodlust,
  Forge Bloodrush each a true-rate multiplier) + pure `FireClock` with bounded
  carry. A4c should reuse this shared rate infrastructure rather than inventing
  a parallel speed model, but the exact melee cadence / clock behavior remains
  part of the A4c decision batch and must be approved before implementation.
- **Blood Barrier**: granted by Sanguinarian and Siphon's overheal, with a strip
  under the HP bar; the capstone gauges sit 4pt lower to make room.

**Scope** (`docs/v2.1-abilities-a4b-scope.md` §2): Red Smile's form change and
melee, the dual `requires: [.bleedUnlocked, .voidUnlocked]`, a `KillSource.melee`

**Its 12 open questions are already drafted** in `v2.1-abilities-a4b-scope.md`
§4b (a–l), with recommendations: what "Void damage" means, what "active combat"
means, facing (`lastMoveDirection`), the 2× damage basis (`shotFractionDamage(2.0)`,
not 200% ATK), on-hit rules for a sweep, phased/vanished targets, reach and arc
scaling (110° at 80pt, CL-15), kaiju overlap, the Void secondary tag, the gun's
clock during the form, empty swings, mini-bosses. **Put them in the closure
table as CL-33… and get them ruled before building.**

**Do not infer terrain behavior from Void affinity.** Whether Red Smile's sweep
can cross walls / Carrier geometry requires an explicit A4c ruling. Approved
supernatural terrain exemptions are mechanic-specific, not a universal property
of Void damage.

**Runtime facts A4c will need** (verified Sep 21–22):
- `PlayerNode` has no facing vector; `GameScene.lastMoveDirection` (set in the
  movement update) is the only candidate.
- Nothing in the runtime defines "active combat" yet. Do not define "active
  combat" solely from gun auto-aim state without examining consequences for
  Red Smile and A5 Grounded Core, which will reuse the shared definition.
- There is no arc/cone damage helper, and all hit resolution is inline in
  `handleProjectileHit` and needs a `ProjectileNode`. `kaijuStrike` is the
  closest melee precedent (a circle at an impact point, `.capstone` credit).
- "Void damage" means nothing at runtime today; the only Void damage rule is
  `erasureVoidTouched` bypassing the Braceguard shield.
- The firing pause precedent is `guard !kaijuActive` in `updateAutoAttack`;
  it must gate only the auto-attack, never `fireProjectile` itself (shards,
  echoes and fragments go through it).
- A sweep that ignores physics would hit the vanished Faceted Lie and phased
  PaneStalkers — gate on an explicit hittability check.
- Bleed on a boss must go through `inflictBossBleed` (survivors only), or the
  "bossStatus means bleeding-before-the-kill" invariant breaks.
