## R4 technical contracts owed before dependent code (written Oct 13, before the beam code)

### C1 — the dynamic virtual-volley cadence (R4: "the beam keeps a volley cadence at `I`")
- The beam keeps ONE virtual volley clock. It accumulates **active beam time** (seconds the beam is firing: `.playing`, a target in reach, not suspended) and compares it each frame to the **current** effective interval `I = playerStats.effectiveFireInterval` (every attack-speed source once: the Rapid Fire ladder and Static/Surge/Glass Engine in `fireRateMultiplier`, Bloodrush, Frenzy, Berserk, Bloodlust, Overclock).
- When the accumulated time reaches `I(now)`, one virtual volley happens and `I(now)` is subtracted; the leftover carries, bounded to one interval (the gun's `FireClock` rule, CL-23). At most one virtual volley per frame. So a speed change takes effect at the next comparison: progress already made (in seconds) is kept, never rescaled, never lost, and never released as a burst.
- Each virtual volley is a primary volley exactly as a gun volley: `recordShot()` (Storm Engine: every 3rd is a spread volley), `volleyCounter.register(emitted: true, …)` (ONE counter — Blackhole every 5th at the LEAD lane's terminus, Shadow Edge every 7th), and a Gravity Well at each PARENT lane's terminus (never per branch or per rendered segment).
- The lane count used by damage ticks is the count set by the most recent virtual volley — `volleyPelletCount(isSpreadVolley:)`: Storm's +2 lanes hold for that cadence window (until the next virtual volley), then fall back. Before the first virtual volley the normal count applies.
- Damage ticks are a separate clock: every **0.5 s of active beam time**, same carry/bound rule. Within one frame the virtual volley is resolved first, then the damage tick (a tick never uses a lane count from a volley that has not happened yet).
- Lane tick damage reads `D` and `I` **at the tick**: `0.75 × D × (0.5 s ÷ I)`, before Fracture and target modifiers. A buff that rises or ends between ticks is reflected at the next tick; nothing is integrated across the window.

### Transitions (stated, then tested)
- **Acquisition of T5:** from the next `.playing` frame the gun stops (no further projectiles from the auto-attack) and the beam takes over. The beam's two clocks start at zero elapsed; projectiles already in flight finish their own lives. Taking T1–T4 never changes the mode.
- **No target in reach (range and line of sight; T4 ignores terrain for both):** the beam is not drawn and does not tick; its clocks hold "ready" without banking time (the `FireClock` idle rule). When a target appears the due volley and tick fire on that frame — no catch-up beyond one.
- **Melee and transformation (Red Smile's form, the kaiju):** the auto-attack returns before the beam's clocks advance, so their remainders are frozen; the beam is hidden; it resumes from exactly the frozen remainders when the form ends — no tick debt.
- **Suspended play (level-up selection, reveals, placement, pause, background, death):** the update that drives the beam does not run, so no time is spent and none is owed. On death and restart the beam is hidden and its clocks reset.
- **Polar Vortex T4 ↔ Rapid Fire T5 (R4, ruled):** Rapid Fire's T5 tier provides `pelletEffect` and is blocked by `glacialCondensation`; Polar Vortex's T4 is blocked by `pelletEffect` (unchanged). Whichever is owned first closes the other's tier. Rapid Fire T1–T4 and every pellet card (Fracture included) stay compatible with both.

## Bounded design choices (each tested; changeable by ruling)
- **Damage resolution per (lane, target, tick).** Branches of one parent lane that contact the same target in a tick are ONE hit carrying the sum of their shares (k/n): damage conserves exactly (k × 1/n), and the procs, riders, crit and credit roll once per target per tick per parent lane (R4). Distinct lanes are separate hits. The hit is primary (`.primary`, Void affinity, Anomaly) when the lane's centre branch is among the contacts, otherwise a fragment hit (`.fragment`).
- **Exact arithmetic for the beam.** The lane tick is fractional, so the beam does NOT go through the gun's integer prefix (`shotBaseDamage`, the crit's `max(2, …)`, integer executes and Forge). It multiplies exactly: `0.75·D·(0.5/I)` × Riftline falloff (0.75^rank) × crit multiplier (and Lucky Break) × execute × Forge offense × the block's amplifiers × Overcharge's factor (× vulnerability for `dealt`) × the Braceguard shield (folded in as a fraction) — rounded ONCE, unbiased, with no floor (zero stays zero), on a fresh block threshold. Then the Fracture share on the projectile's own share threshold (N3's `fractureBranch`); on a boss the DEF dial is applied to the parent and then the share (N3's `fractureBranchOnBoss`). No spent threshold is reused.
- **Crit once per parent lane per tick** (one `rollShotCrit(directAttack: true)` — Calculated Strike counts one direct attack per lane-tick); Lucky Break rides that crit once per lane-tick.
- **Riftline on the beam:** the rank of a target along a lane is its order of distance from Spark among that lane's contacted targets (100 / 75 / 56 % …). Riftline's +25 % range applies to beam length; its pierce count is moot (the beam pierces all).
- **Riders:** the beam lands its hits through `landBeamHit` / `landBeamHitOnBoss`, whose bodies are VERBATIM copies of the gun handlers' post-damage sections (a source check pins their executable tokens equal), so Kindle, Frost Touch, Repulse, Overload, Shatter, Overkill, Seed, kill credit, Anomaly, Void Horror, Bloodthirsty, Whiteout, Apex, Erasure and Chain Lightning behave exactly as for a shot. The gun handlers are not touched. (A shared-function refactor would have moved accepted, pinned A7b contracts; the copy-plus-equality pin keeps them byte-stable.)
- **Termini:** a parent lane ends at the first of: its range (T0–T2, Riftline included), the first solid terrain (unless Long Shot T4), the arena boundary. Gravity Well and the Blackhole open there.
- **Storm's extra lanes** take the tier's next outward step (N3's `AttackPattern.lanes`), so 9 + 2 lanes × 17 branches = 187 at the maximum.
