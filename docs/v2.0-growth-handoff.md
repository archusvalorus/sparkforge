## TWO DESIGN INTERPRETATIONS I made — your call to keep or redirect

**1. Vine Wall = a garden-perimeter HEDGE, not a placeable linear wall.**
The creative handoff imagined Vine Wall as a placeable linear barrier for choke
points. But your "rooted to Terra" law (everything grows on cultivated ground)
contradicts a loose placeable wall — so I built it as a hedge on the garden's
own edge. It's defensive, territorial, rooted, and needs no pathfinding. If you
wanted linear walls, the repel+slow primitive carries over; only the
shape/placement changes.

**2. The T5 animal roster is a PLACEHOLDER (yours to design).**
Per the `tree-capstone-animal-pool` memory ("bring the roster to Brandon"), I
built the *architecture* — the three-part model (base timer · ~150% damage mult
· per-animal modifier) + the rare pet — and populated it with 5 emoji animals
(🐰🐿️🦌🦔🦊) with simple modifiers (damage/radius/one leaves a thorn patch) plus
the 🦁 lion pet. **These are stand-ins.** The launcher is data-driven
(`GameScene.treeAnimals`), so your real 5–10 roster + behaviours is a table
edit, not a rewrite. Design session whenever you're ready.
