### Chilled ground — `ChillGround` (CL-11)

- T1–T3 lifetimes are **per segment, from creation**; each melts on its own
  clock (the visual now melts on game time too — the old one faded on real
  time under pause).
- **Merging is the bound:** a drop landing within 60% of an existing
  segment's radius refreshes / grows / perma-freezes THAT segment instead of
  stacking another. 8,000 drops wandering a 600×600 yard settle under 1,500
  segments (harness G2e). Lookups are a 3×3 bucket check, not a scan.
- T4 ground is permanent **for the current arena** — a Boss Mode arena swap
  (and a new run) thaws it. A later timed drop can never un-permanent it.
- **T5 Ice Rink replaces the trail:** segments clear, the whole arena counts
  as chilled ground (so Glacial Spikes roll everywhere), no more drops. The
  −50% / +25% are plain stats on the card; the rink re-lays itself after an
  arena swap because it belongs to the build.
- Frost patches are sprites off ONE shared texture (they batch; hundreds of
  `SKShapeNode`s would not).

### Snowmen — `SnowmanState` (Q-C3, CL-7)

- A transform is a **stun with a costume** — every enemy subclass already
  respects `isStunned`, so Spurhounds, Linekeepers and Ramplates need no new
  code. Elites transform at the BossClass debuff scale (half duration).
  Arena bosses can't (they aren't `EnemyNode`s) — "bosses immune" is structural.
- **T3 melt, exactly per your clarification:** the form is consumed FIRST,
  then the damage lands — normals die outright; an elite takes the
  triggering hit and THEN an additional 20% of max HP, and can die to
  either. A second hit can't re-trigger it (harness S2d). Any damage melts —
  including a Burn tick.
- Placeholder art: two snowballs, coal eyes, a carrot; the melt is the blue
  smiling puddle (A9 replaces both).

### Frost Touch T3 and the shards (CL-3)

Runtime finding: **shards already applied Frost Touch** — the on-hit slow
never checked `allowModifiers`, so every player projectile slowed. For T3 to
mean anything, `ProjectileNode.appliesFrostTouch` now gates it: true for
everything EXCEPT Iceburst shards and icicle fragments, which earn it at T3 —
those two sources specifically, not a blanket flag. Echoes, splits, seed
fragments, acorns, needles and flower shots keep slowing as they always have.
(Net: a Polar Vortex owner's shards stop slowing until Frost Touch T3.)

**Iceburst cascade capped** (A0 finding F5): a shard's kill may burst once
more (gen 2); a gen-2 shard's kill doesn't. Without the cap, T3's slowing
shards would chain indefinitely.

## Open items — all ruled (Brandon, Sep 17)

1. **Shard slows gated behind Frost Touch T3** ✅ accept — this is the T3 we
   designed. **Documented behavior change:** Iceburst shards and icicle
   fragments LOSE the slow they previously inherited until Frost Touch T3;
   every other projectile source (echoes, splits, seed fragments, acorns,
   needles, flower shots) keeps its existing behavior.
2. **Iceburst limited to one additional generation** ✅ accept as the starting
   bound: initial burst → one secondary burst → stop. Final-generation shards
   still deal damage and still apply eligible Frost Touch; they just can't
   spawn another generation. (That is how it's built — the cap is only on
   the burst trigger.)
3. **Pity restricted to signatures** ✅ accept. Unlocking a dependent card
   doesn't make Glacial Drift or Whiteout a gateway; their ordinary
   prerequisites still work.
4. **Survivor Loop = Phase Skin + Hoarfrost** ✅ accept — avoiding hits plus
   regeneration is a coherent pairing.
5. **Permafrost lit by other global slows** ✅ intentional. "Any source"
   includes Absolute Zero and today's Fortify slow; when A5 turns Fortify
   into DEF growth that interaction disappears naturally.
6. **Snowman contact damage** ✅ **RULED: harmless on contact while
   transformed**, normal contact restored when it reverts. Built:
   `handlePlayerEnemyContact` ignores a snowman entirely (no damage, no
   Iron Bloom / thorns trade either — it's not a fight, it's a snowman).
7. Spikes landing on the enemy (not the mark) and the compact card lines
   stood through the feel pass.
