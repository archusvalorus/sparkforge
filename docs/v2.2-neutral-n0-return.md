## 3. The pick budget, proven on the real flows (R1 evidence)

**What a "selection" is.** One card is acquired per level-up screen. `+1 Card` (ad, or free for ad-free owners; once per run) adds a fourth *offer*, not a selection. `Extra Pick` (ad, or free for ad-free owners; once per run) adds **one** selection from the same spread. Neither is a free-path source: both exist only through the optional ad or the ad-removal purchase, so the free path's selections are the level-ups alone.

**Campaign.** A run starts at level 1; XP (curve 5 · 1.3^(L−2): 633 cumulative to reach 15, 2,410 to reach 20) levels it. Selections = level-ups:

| Cap | Level-ups (selections) | The first is forced | Free choices left |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | 14 | the signature | 13 |
| 20 (XP Boost T3) | 19 | the signature | 18 — of which 3 are XP Boost itself |

- **Three-card maximum** (Rapid Fire 5 + Scatter 4 + Long Shot 4 = 13): fits cap 15 exactly — 13 + signature = 14. ✔
- **Four-card maximum** (+ Fracture 4 = 17): needs XP Boost (3) to reach cap 20: 17 + 3 + signature = **21 selections against 19 available — short by 2.** XP Boost T3's granted level is one of the 19 (it advances the level number; it adds no selection beyond the cap). Extra Pick would cover one, and is monetised.

**Boss Mode.** The gauntlet opens with `openerCards = 8` granted levels (a draft of 8 real level-up screens, or 8 random grants), then stage XP at 5×, 6×, … Selections are still one per level: 14 at cap 15, 19 at cap 20, the opener's eight included. Same arithmetic, same shortfall; the random opener's grants are selections the player did not choose.

**The two honest resolutions** (the caps and tiers are kept as approved until ruled):

- **(a) Intentionally unattainable in one normal run.** The four-card maximum becomes an aspiration: a player chooses which of Long Shot T4 / Fracture T4 / Rapid Fire T5 to leave at the penultimate rung, or spends the once-per-run Extra Pick and is still one short. Design-coherent (depth rewards commitment; the four cards compete), costs nothing, needs no number change. **Recommended.**
- **(b) A small explicit free-path adjustment:** XP Boost T3 raises the cap to **21** instead of 20 (one number in `GameConfig`), giving 20 selections — still one short; or to **22**, giving 21 — exactly enough with zero slack and nothing else taken all run. Either changes an approved number and the XP curve's top (level 21 costs 730, level 22 ≈ 950).

Neither option uses ads or adds picks silently. R1 asks only for the call between (a) and (b).

## 4. Decision batch — recommendations (one ruling each; dependencies marked)

### R1 — four-card maximum (blocks nothing; informs N2's cap numbers)

Recommend **(a)**: keep caps 15/20, accept the four-card maximum as unattainable in one natural run, and record it as a design property in the spec. If Brandon prefers reachability, (b) with cap 22 is the smallest change that actually reaches it.

### R2 — Rapid Fire: classification and beam damage (blocks N4)

- **Classification: ordinary Neutral card.** No `capstone` flag, no guarantee seat, no lockout, no signature. The catalog has no "ordinary tiers ≤ 5" rule to amend — the loader accepts any ladder length, and the draft's capstone logic keys only on the flag — so no amendment is needed; only the harness CA expectation "one capstone per coloured tree" stays as is (Neutral has none).
- **Transformation priority:** a five-tier ordinary card that replaces the primary attack collides with Polar Vortex T4 (icicle replaces the pellet) and with Red Smile (temporary melee). See R4.
- **Damage formula (validated against the code):** the gun's interval today is `fireInterval × fireRateMultiplier / (1 + Bloodrush)(1 + Frenzy)(1 + Berserk)(1 + Bloodlust)`; Rapid Fire's ladder lives in `fireRateMultiplier` (as a real rate: ÷1.10, ÷1.25, ÷1.50, ÷1.90 from base). Per lane per tick:

  `tick = 0.75 × D × (0.5 s ÷ I)` where `D` = the pre-conversion standard-shot damage basis (`Int(effectiveDamageMultiplier)` as the gun computes it, Overcharge split included) and `I` = the effective pre-conversion interval with every attack-speed source in it (ladder, Overclock, Frenzy, Berserk, Bloodlust, Bloodrush).

  Example at T5 alone (fire interval 0.5 s, +90% → I = 0.263 s): tick = 0.75 × D × 1.9 = 1.425 D per 0.5 s = 2.85 D/s, i.e. 75% of the 3.8 D/s the T4 gun would have dealt. With Overclock active (+35% attack speed): I = 0.195 s → 1.92 D per tick; its +15% damage enters once, through D. Nothing enters twice: attack speed only via I, damage only via D.
- **Crits:** one roll per lane per tick (the lane is "a shot"); Calculated Strike counts a lane-tick as a direct attack. **Boss class:** a tick is a direct hit (`takeDirectHit` chain: vulnerability, Permafrost, boss DEF dial), per target.
- **Fractional resolution:** compute in `CGFloat`, resolve per lane-tick with the existing chance-rounding (`A6Rounding`, CL-70: expected value preserved, never a free 1). Shared with Fracture branches and Chain Reaction's generation bonus (R6).
- **Live buffs:** `I` and `D` are read at each tick, so Frenzy/Overclock/Berserk enter and leave on their own clocks; Overclock's 3 s is already active-play time (its timer ticks only in the `.playing` update).
- **Timing:** a `BeamClock` on game time; overlays, pause, backgrounding and death don't advance it (same gate as `fireClock`).

### R3 — Scatter + Fracture geometry (blocks the combined-fan part of N3; Scatter-alone and Fracture-alone are not blocked)

**Scatter alone** (tuning proposals, to `GameConfig.Neutral`): T1 two parallel lanes 12 pt apart (today's spacing); T2 lanes at 0, ±15°; T3 at 0, ±15°, ±30°; T4 nine lanes at 0, ±14.75°, …, ±59° = **118° total** ("almost 120°"). **Fracture alone:** approved 30/60/120/180° fans, branches evenly spaced, centre included (steps 15°, 15°, 15°, 11.25°).

**Combined — the shared forward-spread rule (recommended):** Scatter lanes are the parents; each lane's Fracture fan is centred on the lane's heading. If the outermost branch would exceed **±90°** from the aim, every angle in the composed set is scaled by the same factor so the outermost lands at exactly ±90°. All counts survive (Scatter 9 × Fracture 17 = 153 branches), all branches stay distinct (uniform scaling preserves distinct angles), no branch is dropped or merged, every parent's damage is still divided only among its own branches. Numbers: Scatter T4 (±59°) + Fracture T4 (±90°) = ±149° → scale 90/149 = 0.604: lane 4's fan spans 35.6°–90°, lane 0's ±54.3°; Scatter T2 (±15°) + Fracture T2 (±30°) = ±45°, under the bound, unscaled.

- **Distinct vs coincident:** two branches are coincident only if they share origin and heading; with scaling that happens only when a Scatter step is an integer multiple of a Fracture step at the same lane — Scatter T3 (15°) + Fracture T1/T2 (15°) produces exact overlaps (lane +15°'s centre branch coincides with lane 0's +15° branch). **Rule: coincident branches both hit** (they are different parents' damage shares); visually one line.
- **Several branches on one enemy per tick: yes.** A wide enemy across three of a lane's seventeen branches takes 3/17 of that lane's tick — the division is conserved exactly; it is not "one hit per enemy per lane". (Procs are a different matter: R4.)
- Scatter T1's two origins carry over to beams as two parallel lanes.

### R5 — level cap, XP, granted levels, Overclock (blocks N2's two sub-points only)

- **Cap, campaign and Boss Mode alike:** at the cap, `addXP` keeps adding XP and never levels; the XP bar shows full. **Capped XP is banked**, not discarded: when XP Boost T3 raises the cap, banked XP may level the player as soon as the granted level is resolved, one level-up screen at a time (a queue), never two modals at once. No periodic trigger at cap.
- **The granted level preserves XP** as an absolute amount: a new `grantLevel(keepingXP:)` leaves `currentXP` alone; the next threshold is the new level's. Today's `grantLevel()` (clears XP) stays for the gauntlet opener, which has no XP to keep.
- **T3 taken at or near the cap:** finish the current screen and its reveals; then `pendingGrantedLevels += 1` and `completeLevelUp` triggers the next screen. Taken at level 15 with cap 15: the grant takes the player to 16 under the new cap 20. Taken at 19: the grant makes 20 (the cap); nothing further.
- **Overclock:** refresh, never stack (`start` replaces the timer — today's behaviour); the 3 s counts only in the `.playing` update (already true: the timer ticks nowhere else), so selection screens, reveals, pause and backgrounding don't spend it. **Acquisition level: recommend yes** — picking Overclock on a level-up screen starts its boost when that screen closes (today it misses the pick's own level because the trigger runs before the pick). Chained granted levels: each level-up refreshes (not extends) the 3 s, and the clock starts when play resumes after the last screen.
- **Small XP:** `Int(amount × mult)` rounds 1 × 1.25 to 1 today. Recommend a **carried remainder** in `PlayerNode` (`xpCarry += amount × mult; take floor; keep the fraction`): deterministic, exact over time, no free XP. Applies equally to Boss Mode's 5×–9× multipliers (already integral). Post-run Forge XP untouched.

### R6 — cascade credit and numbers (blocks N5)

- **Where the explosion fires:** today `chainReactionAt` runs only inside `resolveDeathBursts`, i.e. only for *full-credit* deaths, and it discards `takeDamage`'s result, so an explosion's victims die without passing `onEnemyKilled` from that call (their credit comes from elsewhere — to be verified in N5, not assumed). Recommend: **"enemies explode on death" means every credited death of a non-boss enemy, whatever the kill source** (reward-only included), so an explosion kill explodes in turn — the cascade the spec wants.
- **Kill credit of explosion victims:** a new `KillSource.chainReaction` with **reward-only** credit (XP orb, Codex, bestiary; no Siphon/Frenzy/Apex growth/Open Vein), matching how every other burst is credited (CL-29 lineage). Chain Reaction's own explosion is exempt from that rule by the point above. Each victim: one death, one explosion, one reward — guarded by `killCredited` + `isDying` as today.
- **Boss class:** the arena boss is not a target (unchanged, like every burst); **mini-bosses are targets** at the approved damage with BossClass scaling applied (half), consistent with capstone bursts. If Brandon wants mini-bosses unscaled, that is one flag.
- **Geometry:** point burst at the death position; a target is in range when its centre is within the radius (today's rule; the dying body excluded).
- **Parent and generation:** explosions are processed from a FIFO queue in death order within the frame; a victim's generation = its parent's + 1 where the parent is the explosion that *reduced it to zero* (the first lethal hit wins; `isDying` makes later hits no-ops) — deterministic without timers. Independent roots (a gun kill) restart at 0. The bonus is additive on the tier base: `3 × (1 + 0.1 × min(gen, 10))`.
- **Fractional policy, shared:** compute in `CGFloat`, resolve per victim with the chance-rounding (CL-70) so 3.3 is 3 or 4 with expectation 3.3 and a sub-1 branch is never a free 1. T1 (base 1, no bonus) is unaffected.

**Performance plan (N3/N4/N5; visual batching only, never a hit-rule change):**
- Hit resolution for beams is geometric, on the 0.5 s tick: 153 branch segments × ≤ ~60 enemies ≈ 9,000 segment-circle tests per tick — negligible; no physics bodies for beams.
- Rendering: one `SKShapeNode` per lane with its branches as sub-paths (≤ 9 draws), rebuilt only when the aim or the pattern changes; lanes clipped to terrain / the arena bound.
- Sizzles: a pooled set of ≤ 40 small sprite nodes placed at contact points, recycled; purely visual, so the cap never affects damage.
- Unlimited-range projectiles: culled at the arena bounding circle + margin (the cleanup rule above) — nothing reachable is lost, nothing accumulates.
- Cascades: an iterative FIFO queue within the frame (no recursion); worst case every enemy on screen explodes in one frame — bounded by N² distance checks at N ≈ 60; the ring visuals pooled/capped, the damage never.

**On-device checkpoints (Brandon's, smallest useful):** after N1 (movement feel), after N3 (fan shapes and Fracture at small values), after N4 (the beam — the one that matters most), after N5 (cascade feel), and the final v2.2 bundled playtest after N6, which also carries J2's deferred checks (startup, Codex, a full draft on device).
