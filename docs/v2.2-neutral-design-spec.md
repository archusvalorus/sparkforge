# Sparkforge v2.2 — Neutral rework: approved design and interaction register

Design decisions: Brandon / Command Center, October 7–9, 2026. Prepared October 9, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles).

## Status and authority

The nine-card design pass is complete. Brandon has requested a Claude handoff. This document consolidates the decisions in that conversation; it does not claim they are implemented. Numerical ladders and explicitly approved interactions below are locked for implementation, subject to later playtest tuning. Section 4 distinguishes unresolved interactions and recommendations from approved decisions. Read the companion `v2.2-neutral-kickoff.md` for execution scope.

The current catalog still has ten regular Neutral cards. It uses the new authored JSON/native registry architecture. Earlier v2.1 statements that Neutral ships unchanged describe that release, not this task. Do not rebuild the old Swift catalog or treat the migration's gameplay-preservation requirement as a prohibition on this now-requested content change.

## 1. Design intent and boundaries

- Neutral supplies legible, broadly useful tools that reward experimentation and surprising combinations. Gradual discovery through investment and tree unlocks is intentional.
- Nine regular cards remain. **Keen Eye merges into Magnetic Core.** Its freed slot stays empty.
- **Attack size / beam width is banked, not in this release's Neutral scope.** Do not add a tenth card to fill a count.
- Panda remains a separate secret. Neutral is not an affinity tree; do not add a signature, synergy ladder or Rainbow rule.
- All percentages in tier tables are **total bonuses at that tier**, not successive additive grants. Native upgrade effects apply the delta needed to reach each tier without erasing contributions from other sources.
- Preserve health-orb positioning: pickup-radius changes affect XP collection; health orbs remain non-magnetized.
- Preserve stable IDs for retained cards. Retire `neutral_2` (Keen Eye) through the existing retirement/discovery mechanism. Do not reuse that ID, silently transfer historical discovery to Magnetic Core, or rename save keys.

## 2. The approved roster

### Swift — `neutral_1` — 3 tiers

| Tier | Permanent movement-speed bonus |
|---|---:|
| T1 | +8% |
| T2 | +20% |
| T3 | +35% |

The increasing marginal gains (+8, +12, +15 percentage points) deliberately reward deeper investment. This is the permanent movement option alongside Magnetic Core's conditional burst.

### Magnetic Core — `v13_magnetic_core` — 3 tiers

| Tier | XP pickup-radius bonus | Movement-speed burst on XP collection |
|---|---:|---:|
| T1 | +15% | +10% |
| T2 | +30% | +20% |
| T3 | +50% | +30% |

Every burst lasts **1.5 seconds**. Each XP pickup refreshes the duration; repeated pickups do not stack the burst's strength. XP pickup effects remain active at the run's level cap. Keen Eye's former separate +20% pick is removed.

### Rapid Fire — `neutral_3` — 5 tiers

| Tier | Effect |
|---|---|
| T1 | +10% attack speed |
| T2 | +25% attack speed |
| T3 | +50% attack speed |
| T4 | +90% attack speed |
| T5 | Standard shots become a visually continuous chaos-energy beam. Damage ticks every **0.5 seconds**. Retain **75% of standard-shot damage before attack-speed conversion**; accumulated attack speed, including the ladder's +90%, becomes beam-damage scaling. Later attack-speed gains continue to contribute. |

- The original 50% damage reduction was explicitly replaced by **25% reduction**. Do not restore the earlier value.
- Auto-aim at the **nearest valid enemy within range and line of sight**. The beam pierces enemies along its path and initially stops at solid terrain. Long Shot T4 overrides the terrain/line-of-sight restriction as specified below.
- Scatter supplies its full beam pattern. Fracture divides each incoming beam's damage among branches. Overclock's attack-speed bonus feeds the conversion.
- Palette: **neon green + astral blue + orange**. Small pixel-flame **sizzles** appear at enemy contact points, including multiple enemies along a piercing beam. They are visual feedback only: no Burn status or additional damage. Exact color values and animation treatment can be proposed in the implementation; the three-color direction is locked.
- Five tiers do not by themselves settle `isCapstone`, special draw guarantees or transformation priority. See R2/R4.

### Long Shot — `neutral_4` — 4 tiers

| Tier | Effect |
|---|---|
| T1 | +25% shot range |
| T2 | +50% shot range |
| T3 | Remove the shot's distance limit; it continues indefinitely rather than expiring for distance traveled. |
| T4 | Retain unlimited range; shots pierce enemies and pass through solid obstacles and walls. |

All tiers apply to the chaos beam. T3 removes its range limit while terrain still blocks it; T4 lets it penetrate terrain and lets automatic targeting acquire enemies behind terrain. The beam already pierces enemies, so acquiring an already-present attribute does not multiply its damage.

Unlimited **range** is not permission for an unbounded collection of unreachable objects in memory. Claude must propose a cleanup rule that preserves all reachable gameplay. Do not invent projectile wrapping, bouncing or a visible range cap. Ordinary projectile piercing at T4 has no approved damage falloff or finite penetration count; surface a proposed limit if one is necessary rather than silently adding it.

### XP Boost — `neutral_5` — 3 tiers and a run-level cap

| Tier | Effect |
|---|---|
| T1 | +25% run XP gain |
| T2 | +50% run XP gain |
| T3 | Retain +50% XP gain, raise the run's level cap to **20**, and immediately grant **one full normal level-up**, including the usual card and stat reward. |

- Introduce a **normal run level cap of 15**. The source currently has no enforced cap. This is an intentional systems change, not an existing rule to preserve.
- Taking T3 during the level-15 selection can advance the player to level 16. Finish the current selection/reveal cleanly, then present the granted level; do not overlap modals or discard an earned choice.
- Preserve XP already earned toward the next level when granting the bonus level (the conversation's stated design reading; do not blindly use today's `grantLevel()`, which clears XP). Define remaining XP semantics under R5.
- **At either cap, XP orbs still collect and trigger Magnetic Core.** Stop further leveling without disabling collection feedback/effects.
- This is run XP, not Forge Path progression or the post-run rewarded-ad XP feature.

### Scatter — `neutral_6` — 4 tiers

| Tier | Total primary shots per volley | Pattern |
|---|---:|---|
| T1 | 2 | Two parallel shots |
| T2 | 3 | One center shot, one angled shot to either side |
| T3 | 5 | One center shot, two to either side |
| T4 | 9 | One center shot, four to either side; **almost 120 degrees** total coverage |

These are total counts with Scatter alone, not extra-projectile bonuses. Each is a full-strength primary shot carrying normal shot effects. With Rapid Fire T5, the same counts and patterns become full-strength beams under the beam's damage rules. Exact T2/T3 fan angles, T4's precise near-120-degree value, and the parallel offset are tuning details to propose, not previously approved numbers.

### Chain Reaction — `v13_chain_reaction` — 3 tiers

| Tier | Base explosion damage | Blast radius | Effect |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| T1 | 1 | 35 points | Enemies explode on death. Enemies killed by an explosion also explode, allowing cascades. |
| T2 | 2 | 50 points | Stronger, wider cascades. |
| T3 | 3 | 70 points | Each subsequent generation along a chain gains +10% base explosion damage, capped at +100%. |

At T3, generation 0 starts at 3; generation 1 = 3.3; generation 2 = 3.6; generation 10+ = 6, before separately applicable combat modifiers. The bonus is additive, not compounded. If one explosion kills three enemies, all three child explosions get the same next-generation bonus. Unrelated chains begin at generation 0. Radius stays at the purchased tier; it does not grow along a chain. Fractional progression must survive damage resolution. These are approved starting values for playtest.

The same enemy must never produce duplicate death credit or repeat its explosion. Boss reach, other on-kill effects, competing kill sources and precise cascade scheduling are R6, not new implied combat rules.

### Overclock — `v17_overclock` — 1 tier

After a level-up: **+35% movement speed, +35% attack speed, +15% damage for 3 seconds of active gameplay**, beginning when play resumes. One pick, no ladder.

The name stays **Overclock**: Frenzy is already the Bleed card `bleed_3`, and Brandon selected this fallback. Its attack-speed bonus feeds chaos-beam damage conversion. It remains a conditional level-up benefit; no replacement trigger at the level cap was requested. Refresh/stack behavior, the acquisition-level trigger and interactions with granted levels need the R5 timing definition. Do not double-count its damage bonus through the beam conversion.

### Fracture Shot — `v18_fracture_shot` — 4 tiers

| Tier | Total branches per incoming shot/beam | Total fan coverage |
|---|---:|---:|
| T1 | 3 | 30 degrees |
| T2 | 5 | 60 degrees |
| T3 | 9 | 120 degrees |
| T4 | 17 | 180 degrees |

Every fan retains a center branch; side branches double 2 → 4 → 8 → 16. **Divide each incoming shot's damage evenly among its branches, including the center.** Do not retain a full-strength original shot plus fragments, and do not give every branch the old 50% damage. The same division applies to each beam's damage per tick. Preserve fractional values; rounding each tiny branch up to at least 1 would manufacture damage.

Scatter supplies more full-strength incoming attacks; Fracture distributes each attack's damage across branches. The combined spatial arrangement is not yet precisely ruled: the suggested overall 180-degree bound was an assistant recommendation, not an explicit separately adjudicated geometry algorithm. See R3 before composing the two fans.
