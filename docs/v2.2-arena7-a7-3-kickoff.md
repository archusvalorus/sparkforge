## Bounded behavior

- Railspike is a fragile low longitudinal chassis, 1 HP / 3 XP, ordinary status, kill-credit and hostile-projectile contracts. Its separate stable bestiary ID stays hidden until full arena integration. No existing IDs change.
- Seek a valid anchor on Spark's sampled x in the same broad lane, at either y = ±0.55R. Require at least 0.35R stand-off, body/circle clearance, actual routed reachability, clear bolt path and a clear local side step. No anchor means approach/reposition, not teleport or diagonal fire.
- Start only when the shooter is within the current player-centered viewport with a 32-gameplay-point inset. At commitment lock the world origin, north/south direction and terrain/circle-clipped range. Moving Spark does not redirect either shot.
- Telegraph for 1.0 s with the narrow projectile corridor and two purple charge marks. Launch twice, 0.45 s apart. Speed starts at 300 × gameplay scale; range at most 1.2R. The corridor is a warning, not damaging floor. Each mark extinguishes with its shot.
- Recover unarmed for 1.3 s after shot two; minimum 5 s tell-to-tell. All authoritative time is active-play time; a long frame cannot skip the tell or bunch both shots.
- Death, freeze, stun, fear, trapping, airborne control or shove outside the 6-point anchor tolerance cancels queued launches. Slow affects approach movement. Dispatch happens after physics and the Chain Reaction death cascade, with fresh status/position checks. Already-fired bolts persist through shooter death and retain ordinary defenses/removal behavior.
