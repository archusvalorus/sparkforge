## 1. Release purpose and boundaries

Rainbow rewards breadth across a run's available colors. The Lockworks teaches players to anticipate threats moving between chambers. The Long Breach turns those spatial lessons into a finite advance toward a goal. Together they expand what existing builds must navigate and accomplish.

Preserve the completed core-tree and Neutral reworks, current stat tuning, Arena 7 and accepted menu direction. Keep free joystick movement, dynamic joystick recentering, automatic targeting, non-magnetized health orbs and existing defense/damage contracts. No platforming, jumping, manual aiming or new control scheme is proposed.

Do not add another tree, a replacement Neutral card, checkpoints, mid-run saves, broad Boss Mode redesign, paid content or unrelated cleanup by default. Public Atlas publishing and the deferred Codex F8 direct-read cleanup remain separate work. Arena 10's fortress-controlled monument fight and Verdant reveal stay out of v2.3. Neither arena owes a new skin or card set simply because it ships.

The old "Rainbow IN v2.0/v2.1" notes are superseded by CL-105's deferral. Current production has shared palette, acquisition and timer infrastructure but no Rainbow catalog entry, qualifier, offer/award path or combat implementation. This is feature work, not enabling a dormant switch.

## 2. Rainbow: earned qualification, chosen commitment

### Approved foundations

Qualification covers every color in the fresh run's frozen choice palette, excluding Neutral. One distinct acquired card representing each active color is sufficient; Growth and signatures count, a bridge represents both active color tags, and repeated tiers add no breadth. Neutral and Panda represent no color. The accepted counting description includes smaller early-save palettes, with no newly invented permanent unlock gate.

Qualification does **not** activate Rainbow. The player must knowingly select it and see the paths that selection will close. The following locked offer and commitment rules make that choice concrete without reopening the core/Neutral reworks.

### What the existing cards actually do

| Existing path | Current code contract | Consequence for this design |
|---|---|---|
| Seven affinity capstones | Tree, Everglow, Iron Maiden, Skybeam, Apex, Erasure and Polar Vortex have five tiers. Focus-and-finish protects an unfinished, advanceable ladder. Completed capstones can coexist. | There is no permanent one-capstone-per-run slot to occupy. A global Rainbow-versus-all-capstones rule would be a new balance system, not reuse of an existing slot. |
| Rapid Fire | T1 through T4 grant attack speed; **T5 changes shots into a chaos beam**. This regular Neutral card is not flagged as an affinity capstone. | A shot-form exclusion must target T5 specifically, retaining useful lower tiers. |
| Polar Vortex | T1 through T3 retain the pre-icicle path; **T4 creates Glacial Condensation**, and T5 then strengthens the storm. Existing pellet-effect ownership can already close T4. | Locking only its literal T5 would fail to exclude the shot transformation. |
| Long Shot, Scatter, Fracture Shot | Their highest tiers are T4. They alter range, lanes and branches rather than all sharing a T5 form. | Do not remove these by interpreting "capstone" as every powerful Neutral tier. |
| Warp Shot, Mirror Edge, Erasure's Echo | Existing compatibility rules are explicit. Warp/Mirror conflict with the beam; Echo remains supported by the beam under its own rules. | No new Rainbow exclusion follows merely from their names or hypothetical cost. |

### R1: offer timing and guarantee, owner-approved

Use a **one-rank special breadth reward offered in the normal level-up spread**, requiring a normal player pick. It is not a new tree, regular Neutral ladder, affinity-capstone ladder or automatic grant. The exact catalog/offer classification must truthfully represent this special reward; do not silently relabel it as an ordinary Neutral card to bypass eligibility. Account for any new selectable entry explicitly while retaining the nine regular Neutral cards and preserving accepted historical fixtures.

- Latch qualification after a completed acquisition. Never replace an offer under the player's finger or interrupt the current choice/reveal/placement flow.
- Guarantee its first offer at the next new level-up spread with an unprotected normal seat. If passed over, guarantee it every other new level thereafter while eligible. Once seated for a level, rerolls retain it. A reroll does not create an earlier entitlement.
- Preserve the existing gateway-pity, in-progress-capstone and secret-Panda guarantees. If every seat is protected, carry the Rainbow entitlement to the next eligible spread instead of displacing another promise or silently adding a fourth card.
- Taking Rainbow uses the ordinary pick. Extra Pick may still use its remaining selection, with stale alternatives revalidated immediately. Rainbow is absent from automatic random-opener grants and from the separate bonus-card draw. A later ordinary choice in those runs can still offer it.
- **No free level, extra selection or cap increase** when qualification occurs on the final available level-up. It remains earned but cannot be selected unless another legitimate choice is owed. The owner accepted this late-cap outcome.

The guarantee cadence and protected-seat/late-cap policy are approved. Tests must prove the feature remains meaningfully reachable within the existing 15/20 level budgets without granting extra rewards.

### R2: mutually exclusive shot forms, owner-approved

Make Rainbow a separate shot-form commitment, **mutually exclusive with Rapid Fire T5's chaos beam and Polar Vortex T4's icicle conversion**. This is a gameplay identity choice, not a claimed framerate fix.

- First acquired form wins. If the beam or Glacial Condensation already exists, Rainbow is not offered/selectable. Qualification alone reserves no form and closes nothing.
- Selecting Rainbow preserves every owned lower tier. Rapid Fire T1 through T4 remain available; its T5 closes. Polar Vortex T1 through T3 remain available; T4 closes and therefore its sequential T5 cannot be reached. A stalled ladder releases only the existing focus-and-finish lock as it already does.
- No refunds, removals or retroactive effect disabling. The gate is enforced at normal/bonus/random/granted acquisition boundaries and on stale Extra Pick choices, even where Rainbow itself cannot be auto-granted.
- Other affinity capstones and their normal focus-and-finish guarantees remain available. Keep Long Shot, Scatter, Fracture, Warp, Mirror and Echo under their existing rules. Additional exclusions would require a new owner decision.
- Future transformations join an explicit reviewed conflict group, not a generic "all T5", "all capstones" or whole-family ban.

The narrow fork is approved. Do not reopen it as a Rainbow-versus-all-affinity-capstones system.

### R3: agreed damage identity and detailed interaction boundaries

The agreed tuning contract is: **2x existing ordinary primary-shot damage**, plus a **3.5x-reference sweep every 20 active seconds**, beginning after a full 20 seconds following selection. Qualification alone starts no effects or timer.

- Primary scaling is applied once through the existing damage pipeline, not by redefining ordinary gun damage as base ATK or adding a global modifier that doubles unrelated DoTs, summons and status effects.
- Under approved R2, Rainbow and Rapid Fire T5 cannot coexist. The first draft's simultaneous Rainbow-beam recommendation is withdrawn. Rainbow's periodic giant prismatic beam remains part of Rainbow itself; it is not the excluded Rapid Fire continuous attack.
- Scatter keeps its parent lanes; Fracture shares each enhanced parent's damage normally; Echo and derived attacks inherit their existing fractional basis at most once. Preserve crit, defense, final rounding and true-zero behavior. Rainbow's sweep does not schedule extra primary/Echo/Storm/Fracture volleys. Specify ordinary credited-kill effects, including Chain Reaction, through the existing paths.
- The sweep's agreed 3.5x reference uses the ordinary primary-shot basis **without Rainbow's 2x modifier or beam attack-speed conversion**. This avoids accidental 7x scaling. No new boss-resistance percentage or execute effect is implied.
- Preserve a Spark-aimed, view-bounded world-space lane captured at commitment, one hit per eligible enemy per cycle and no whole-level/offscreen wipe. The traveling-front hit rule and captured-origin wall/Long Shot interaction below are now owner-approved. Warning/crossing use the approved initial P1 timings below, subject to owner presentation review; the P1 lifecycle rules are settled.
- Reveal the chosen form, its lockouts and own countdown clearly. Clear run-owned state on restart/end. Keep ownership across Boss Mode stages, cancel old-board attacks and restart the sweep clock on each new board. The owner-approved P1 ledger below defines pause, level-up and interruption behavior.

The 2x modifier, 3.5x basis, 20-active-second cadence, visible-battlefield scope, periodic spectacle and visible countdown are agreed. Traveling contact and the captured-origin wall/Long Shot rule are also now approved. P1 approval settles the attribution/lifecycle package below. Actual live adapter wiring and verification still belong to a separately authorized feature unit.

### Periodic "SUPER!" beam: concise presentation and interaction brief

**Locked fantasy:** once every 20 active seconds, a giant prismatic beam crosses the screen with "SUPER!" streaming across it. Its HUD countdown lets the player deliberately kite enemies into the impending attack. This is Rainbow's periodic spectacle, not Rapid Fire's continuous beam. The effect uses the agreed 3.5x ordinary-shot basis without Rainbow's 2x modifier.

**Owner-approved P1 behavior, with initial visual timing still tunable after owner review:**

| Aspect | Proposed behavior |
|---|---|
| Direction and coverage | A vertical prismatic front travels **screen-left to screen-right** through a horizontal lane one-third of captured visible battlefield height, centered on Spark’s world Y at launch. The union covers that lane, not the full screen. Spark positions the aim point; this is not a burst originating outward from his body. |
| Damage aligned to the visual | Deal one hit when a target's live hittable body intersects the moving front inside the captured footprint. Sweep the front between frames to prevent missed targets on long frames. A per-cycle hit set prevents repeated damage from a thick band, trails, particles or repeated overlaps. The trailing light and "SUPER!" lettering are visual only. |
| Moving targets | A target ahead of the front can still be struck; a target entering behind a front that already passed receives no retroactive hit. Live death/hittability/defense checks run at contact. Do not pre-damage every visible target at launch while showing a later traveling impact. |
| Footprint commitment | During the final warning, a faint lane guide follows Spark’s current world Y. Compute visible battlefield as viewport intersected with board bounds; proposed lane height is one-third of that height. At launch interpolate viewport/Spark at commitment, center the proposed lane on Spark Y, intersect with captured visibility without shifting its center, and capture the firing origin. Near top/bottom the lane trims rather than moves away from Spark. Lock that rectangle through the sweep. Camera/player movement does not drag the attack into new rooms or previously unseen Arena 9 regions. HUD overlays do not create artificial holes in the gameplay footprint. |
| Countdown and warning | Add a persistent **SUPER!** row to the existing capstone countdown HUD while Rainbow is owned. Start at 20.0s. Reuse its existing last-three-seconds urgency treatment; use a restrained footprint cue during the final 1.0 active second. This warning occurs inside the 20-second cycle, not after it. |
| Sweep timing | Use the approved initial 0.75-active-second crossing animation beginning at the 20-second launch. This is approved initial prototype timing, adjustable through owner presentation review without changing damage rate. Start the next countdown at launch, so later launches remain 20 active seconds apart instead of becoming 20 seconds plus animation duration. |
| Beam and text | A bright prismatic core with a long rainbow trail carries the large **SUPER!** text left to right. Keep the traveling effect behind essential HUD and readable hostile tells; no opaque full-screen white flash or per-target text burst. Prefer a small fixed set of reused visual nodes over creating a separate beam for every branch or victim. |
| Primary and sweep damage | The primary 2x modifier applies once. Snapshot the sweep's agreed ordinary-shot reference at launch, excluding that modifier and beam conversion. Use existing target damage/mitigation and credit paths once per eligible target. Do not create additional Echo, Storm or Fracture volleys from the sweep itself. Snapshot attacker damage/crit inputs at launch and roll normal crit per eligible target (including existing Lucky Break); read target defenses/vulnerability at contact. Retain passive bonuses and full capstone kill credit, including existing Chain Reaction rules. The sweep applies no weapon on-hit statuses, hit-meter charges, primary counters/charges or extra shots/chains. Positive damage still melts an existing Whiteout snowman through the shared target entry; zero remains zero. |
| Walls and Long Shot | The approved rule tests line of sight from Spark's captured launch position to the target through real wall geometry. Walls block the hit unless **Long Shot T4 was owned at launch**, which permits the terrain-passing exception. Passing through enemies does not consume or shorten the sweep. Bright damaging coverage/hit feedback must match this rule; decorative rainbow trails alone never promise a hit through a wall. |

**Owner-approved temporal hit rule:** the lane-height front travels left to right across the captured Spark-aimed lane, dealing one hit on contact per eligible enemy. The approved lane revision supersedes the earlier full-height coverage. Artwork and actual hit area must match; no invisible full-height damage.

**Owner-approved wall rule:** test line of sight from the player origin captured at firing; walls block unless Long Shot T4 permits passage. The prototype must make this rule readable in the Lockworks' rooms and doorways.

**Owner-approved P1 lifecycle ledger:**

- Only `.playing` active time advances the warning, front and cadence. Pause, level-up and app/background holds freeze them; no delayed SpriteKit action can deliver damage while gameplay is held. On foregrounding, use the existing pause/resume policy rather than forcing play to resume. Countdown urgency animation is frozen/hidden consistently with its owner HUD.
- Pausing during a sweep retains its captured footprint, front progress, damage snapshot and hit set. Resume continues the same attack without re-hitting earlier victims or replaying "SUPER!" from the start.
- Player death, return to title, run restart or scene/region retirement cancels pending front damage and visuals immediately. Already credited hits are not undone. A new run clears qualification, offer cadence, selected form and countdown with the other run-owned state.
- Boss Mode board/stage handoff cancels old-board attacks and clears their hit set. Preserve owned Rainbow and its ordinary-shot modifier across stages, but begin a fresh 20-active-second clock for the new stage. No entering-stage free strike or off-board callback is created.
- Do not advance several missed cycles or deliver catch-up sweeps after a pause/background interval. Use the existing game-time delta bound (0.1s per update), with swept moving-body contact and no real-time catch-up debt. The prototype must preserve the agreed active-time cadence.

The warning and crossing durations are the approved initial prototype settings. They may be adjusted through the presentation review without changing the agreed 20-second launch cadence, damage basis, eligible hit set or one-hit rule. The actual effect must not be advertised as validated until the prototype and interaction tests exist.

### Performance is a separate proof obligation

Rainbow has no production combat implementation yet; the isolated P1 fixture supplies **no measured device Rainbow framerate finding**. Existing host probes exercise high branch counts and cascades, but their CPU timings are not phone/iPad GPU or full-frame evidence. The owner's concern is reasonable; it is not proof that every high-tier card must be excluded.

Keep amplification bounded by the existing attack pattern plus one owned sweep event at its approved cadence: no sweep per branch, target hit, child explosion or Echo; no recursive Rainbow event generation. Render-only batching or visual budgets must preserve every legitimate gameplay hit and proc. Track live entities, pending attacks, cleanup and effect lifetime so a long Arena 9 run cannot accumulate unbounded offscreen work.

Profile representative and worst intended builds, wall/doorway sightlines, visible target density and Arena 9's active/retired regions. Record frame-time, memory/object growth and visual readability on appropriate devices. Prefer pooled/reused visuals, batched rendering, bounded visual-only effects, lawful off-world cleanup and correct encounter retirement. Preserve real projectile counts, damage, range, proc cadence and reachable pickups. Any gameplay cap or extra card lockout requires a separate balance decision, not a performance fix disguised as design canon.

Carry this matrix forward as a future-platform engineering requirement, including the Switch concern. Validate iOS devices that are actually available now; record a hardware-specific budget and port proof only when that platform work is authorized and measured. No FPS target, enemy cap, projectile cap or Switch performance result is invented by this proposal.

## 3. Arena 8: The Lockworks

**Approved direction:** a building-like layout with rectilinear rooms, corridors and open doorways. Spark can move in and out of rooms to kite mobs rather than being confined to connected circular combat bowls. The established pressure lesson remains forecasting threats that migrate between chambers. Stone, iron, ash, gates and machinery remain the Broken March grammar.

**Recommended bounded shape:** one static authored building floor with room corners, wall segments, corridors and real doorway gaps. Thresholds remain open. No moving doors, destructible walls, loading screens or circular room chain is proposed. Threats visibly prepare in one room and migrate along reachable paths. Prototype a continuous map rather than sealed kill rooms.

### Existing support and bounded engineering

- `Footprint` already represents rotated or axis-aligned rounded rectangles, with configurable corner radius; arrays of solid wall segments can form rectilinear rooms and leave doorway gaps. Segment visibility, actor-radius clearance, routes, spawn zones and safe anchors are reusable. A bounding-circle broad-phase test does not make the visible room circular.
- The **outer boundary is still circular** in current player clamps and many spawn, displacement and cleanup consumers. A rectangular building footprint therefore needs an opt-in non-circular bounds contract and an audit of those consumers. Drawing box walls inside an unchanged circular clamp would not fulfill the approved vision.
- Keep circle behavior as the default for the seven existing arenas. Scope the new bounds and room/doorway routing to the authored floor first; do not introduce a navmesh, physics solver, arbitrary building generator or general engine rewrite by default.
- Prove corners, wall joins, narrow door mouths, kiting loops, largest intended grounded bodies, growth/shrink and displacements using real geometry. Existing sequential footprint resolution was written for authored non-overlapping obstacles; continuous wall joins need explicit proof and a bounded correction if necessary.
- Existing player-follow camera can travel between rooms. Room pressure still needs authored activation/migration rules, safe spawns and fair tells. It does not require a new control scheme.

Select the smallest roster delivering the signaler/carrier/chamber-migrant jobs, then a boss synthesizing the room lesson. Those remain roles, not three automatically approved enemy classes. Boss identity, roster count, attack plan, cadence and rewards return in focused design briefs. Reuse registry/unlock, discovery and Boss Mode foundations where the authored fight fits.

## 4. Arena 9: The Long Breach

**Existing creative direction:** deliberately bounded left-to-right progression, a moving battlefront, pursuit and reinforcement pressure. Enemy roles are pursuers, forward defenders and breach-point ambushers. Brandon describes a gauntlet in which Spark advances, fights and reaches a goal.

**Accepted overarching shape, with detailed behavior below still proposed:** one finite authored route, free joystick movement, automatic targeting and the current player-follow camera as the starting model. Crossing authored progress thresholds activates encounters once. Allow retreat within activated ground; do not introduce forced autoscroll or a lethal following wall by default. Avoid requiring every straggler to die at every threshold.

Recommend success requiring both the final encounter's completion and reaching the goal. The finale's boss/encounter design must be chosen before implementation. Death restarts the whole run under existing rules. No checkpoints or saved mid-run progression are proposed. **Countdown timers, chasing death walls, forced-scroll pressure and other gauntlet gimmicks are parked for later gauntlets, not v2.3 requirements.** Boss Mode should reuse a compact authored finale arena if a new boss joins its roster, rather than forcing the traversal stage into every boss gauntlet.

### Architecture to prove before content expansion

| Concern | Existing foundation | Required bounded extension or decision |
|---|---|---|
| World and camera | Player-follow camera, authored solids, route graph, safe anchors. | Stage bounds and progress policies. Current player clamps and many spawn/cleanup queries use distance from the world origin and a circular arena radius. Do not implement a long stage by changing the radius alone. |
| Encounter activation | Time-based waves; Arena 7 has scoped arrival and attack coordination. | A small region/progress encounter director with one-time activation, reinforcement/pursuit ownership and clear completion conditions. Keep timing and damage separate from visual tells. |
| Goal and result | Existing boss credit, result screens and Boss Mode stage victory. | Goal-entry plus final-encounter completion must produce one success. Define how this connects to boss defeat, unlocks, rewards, discovery and old-save reconciliation without duplicate credit. |
| Cleanup and lifetime | Restart/end cleanup, arena swapping, deferred attacks and transient Boss Mode geometry. | Define retirement of encounters, projectiles, pickups, delayed Echoes, beams, cascades and summoned structures as the front advances. Do not silently truncate Long Shot, delete reachable XP or leave hostile callbacks attached to a retired sector. |
| Persistence | Stable arena/boss IDs and registry-derived unlock reconciliation. | Existing arena selection/completion storage may suffice for a whole-run restart. Do not add checkpoint/save schemas unless separately approved. Goal-based completion still needs new wiring because current unlock flow is boss-defeat based. |
| Boss Mode | Shared boss factories, arena swap and cleanup. | Approve a compact finale home geometry and consistent fight identity. Keep traversal progression distinct from the existing mode already called the gauntlet. |

Prefer small testable bounds/director/completion components around the existing game loop. This proposal does not authorize a general engine rewrite. Preserve the old seven arenas' behavior while proving each new primitive.
