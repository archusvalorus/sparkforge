# Sparkforge v2.1 — Geometry Reconnaissance Packet

*Claude, Aug 2026. The technical discovery requested in
`arena6-splitworks-design-lock.md` §11, answered against the code as of
commit `953fe51`. Read-only reconnaissance — no implementation. Structured
as Lyra's ten questions, then the decision table, then proposed unit
boundaries with proof criteria.*

## Executive summary (read this if nothing else)

**The engine is more ready for the Fallen Carrier than expected, for a
surprising reason: Sparkforge has no physics-solver collision at all.**
Every physics body in the game sets `collisionBitMask = 0`; the physics
world is purely a contact/trigger bus, and all containment is manual
position math. That sounds like a gap — it's actually the green light. The
monument boss (Unmade Star) already implements a solid obstacle by
displacement (`pushPlayerOutOfMonument`, GameScene:2539), with a comment
explicitly declaring this the house pattern. The Carrier is that pattern,
generalized and made data-driven — not a fight with SpriteKit.

The real work is that **nothing steers and nothing checks visibility**:
ten enemy `chase` implementations all beeline by direct position writes,
auto-aim picks nearest-by-distance with no line-of-sight, ~15 copy-pasted
"random point in the arena" samplers have no rejection concept, and ~18
systems displace actors through space with no geometry consult. Each of
these has a single best insertion point, catalogued below.

## 1. How enemies pursue and steer

Direct per-frame position assignment; no SKActions, no physics velocity,
no steering, no avoidance, no arena clamp for enemies (only bosses
self-clamp, and only mid-charge — BossNode:355, which usefully *ends the
charge on wall contact*: the precedent for the Ramplate/Marchwarden).

- Base beeline: `EnemyNode.chase` (EnemyNode:294) — `position +=
  direction * speed * dt` toward the player, every frame.
- Ten subclass variants (StarNeedle's 4-phase lock/dash state machine,
  Anvilborn's anchor, CircuitWasp/StaticHalo orbits, EchoLeech backoff,
  PaneStalker's phase-teleport…) — ALL still direct position writes,
  driven from one loop in `updateEnemies` (GameScene:4623).
- **No steering seam exists.** Cheapest insertion: a post-move resolve
  pass in `updateEnemies` (one site, mirrors `pushPlayerOutOfMonument`),
  plus a route-aware target substitution *before* `chase` (feed a
  waypoint instead of the player when the direct line is blocked). That
  substitution keeps all ten subclass behaviors intact — they beeline at
  whatever point we hand them.
- `VectorMath.swift` is 68 lines: add/sub/scale/distance/normalize only.
  Segment-vs-shape, dot product, and lerp must be added (small).

## 2. Physics bodies, masks, contact assumptions

`GameConfig.Physics` bit ladder: player«0, enemy«1, projectile«2,
**boundary«3 (DEAD — declared, never referenced; reclaimable)**, xpOrb«4,
enemyProjectile«5, healthOrb«6, magnetOrb«7, forgeCoin«8. **Next free bit
«9** (update the Notion registry on claim — or reclaim «3 for
`obstacle`, which reads better and costs nothing).

- Every body: `collisionBitMask = 0`. Contacts only, resolved in one
  `didBegin` tuple ladder (GameScene:9178).
- The arena boundary is a *drawing*, not a physics object. Player clamps
  radially every frame (PlayerNode:703); enemies never clamp; projectiles
  fly through the wall and die by max-range; pickups clamp at spawn only.
- MonumentBossNode:96 documents the house rule: solidity by displacement,
  never the solver. **The Carrier should follow it** — a physics body on
  the footprint would give us contact events we don't need and collision
  behavior we can't have (nothing else collides).

## 3. How spawn locations and random world points are selected

Polar math (`random angle × radius expression`), copy-pasted ~15 times,
zero rejection sampling, no shared helper. Full site list surveyed:
enemy ring spawns (outside the wall — safe by construction, but the walk
inward crosses the Carrier), four identical boss `spawnPosition()` ring
statics, monument fixed anchor `(0, 0.52r)`, health/magnet/coin
`randomArenaPosition()` statics, XP-at-death (unclamped), forge-coin
*animated toss* across the field, Terra ghost (rim-clamped only),
Singularity wells at `0…0.6r`, Erasure Rift Cannon origin at `0…0.6r`,
Erasure echo at `0.85r` edge, panda entry/destination, flower rooting
(the ONE correct area-uniform sampler in the codebase — GameScene:4299,
good reference).

**Unit 1 must introduce the shared sampler** (`sample a point, rejecting
blocked footprints + margins`) and route the interior sites through it.
Found in passing: `GameConfig.Wave.spawnDistance` is a frozen `static
let` evaluated once per process — it doesn't track per-arena
`radiusScale` (a latent bug Star Anvil already dodges by hand;
GameScene:8047 comment). Fix rides Unit 1.

## 4. Attacks and card effects that assume an unobstructed field

By travel type (this taxonomy matches the lock's collision policy §4):

- **(a) Travel-based — a wall should block:** the entire projectile
  pipeline. Player + enemy projectiles are twin straight-line
  integrators dying only by range or enemy-contact (ProjectileNode:115,
  EnemyProjectileNode:66). ~12 card effects funnel through
  `fireProjectile`/`updateProjectiles` — **one insertion point covers
  them all.** Blocking precedent already exists twice: Vine Wall T3 eats
  enemy projectiles by point-in-circle (GameScene:4218), and shots that
  reach a sitting panda "simply stop existing" (GameScene:3294).
  Tunneling watch: Star Needle dash 640pt/s, XP vacuum 700pt/s — use a
  segment test, not a point test, for fast movers.
- **(b) Arcing/falling — should cross the obstacle:** Skybeam strikes,
  Unmade Star Starfall/Collapse Marks, panda bamboo rain, Nature Canon
  pounces. Damage applies to the target reference directly, not by
  traversal — **naturally obstacle-immune, and exactly the grammar the
  Marchwarden's Standardfall needs** (the lock demands a cross-obstacle
  attack; it's already the house style for sky attacks).
- **(c) Auras/orbits around the player:** Everglow, Windchill, Tesla,
  Static Field, Aegis, kaiju aura, Apex orbit — all
  distance-from-player tests via `damageEnemiesInRadius` (15 call
  sites). They *leak through* a thin wall. Recommendation: **auras
  ignore the wall by policy** — cheap, defensible ("supernatural"
  per the lock's carve-out), and avoids the most expensive insertion.
- **(d) Global:** Everglow eruption, Event Horizon, passive DPS —
  positionless, safe. Two globals place things randomly and need the
  Unit-1 sampler: Singularity wells, Rift Cannon origin (its beam also
  crosses everything — either reroll origins out of the footprint and
  declare the beam reality-piercing, which the Void fiction supports, or
  clip it).
- **(e) Ground zones:** Terra placement (rim-clamp only; the ghost
  preview is the natural validity affordance), garden growth can swallow
  the Carrier footprint (design call — recommendation: allow overlap
  visually but exclude footprint from `covers()` membership so no
  healing-while-inside-a-wall), flowers/gravity wells/chill trail/
  poison clouds inherit their parents' positions.

## 5. Growth, Panda, orbitals, dashes, knockback, large bosses near geometry

Every mover is a direct `position +=` or `SKAction.move` with no geometry
consult. Highlights (full table in survey): Undertow drags enemies
through anything; **Gravemote and the Unmade Star's Weight-of-the-Center
drag THE PLAYER and never re-run the solid resolve** (a pull can push
you into geometry today — the resolve pass must run after pulls, not
just after `move`); knockback is ONE shared primitive
(`EnemyNode.applyKnockback`, 5 callers — fix once); Erasure Displacement
and Panda `.pin` teleport actors to arbitrary points (need the
placement-validity check); Apex familiar lerps (clips corners by
construction — acceptable, it's a spirit); **Mote's entrance is a
scripted one-time cinematic and should be explicitly exempt from all of
this**; Iron Maiden T5's comment already says "cannot miss, ignores
collision" — keep it true, decide only whether the tracer visually clips.

Large actors: collision radii do NOT track `setScale` (a 2.2× mini-boss
still has a 12pt body) — **route-gap fit requires an explicit per-actor
footprint property**, which the lock's Ramplate ("occupy a route") and
Marchwarden sizing both depend on. Monument = `0.40 × arena radius`
body, the biggest thing we've ever made solid.

## 6. Single-open-playfield assumptions in arena + Boss Mode code

- Camera: `camera?.position = player.position`, unclamped, one line.
- **Boss Mode `swapArena` teleports the player to `.zero`** — dead
  center, i.e. inside a centered Carrier. Needs a safe-spawn anchor from
  the geometry descriptor (trivial once the descriptor exists).
- `ArenaConfig` describes size with ONE scalar (`radiusScale`); no
  geometry field exists yet. Ambient emitters, ability ranges, and
  Growth caps all derive from `Arena.radius` and remain valid.
- Auto-aim (`findNearestTargetPosition`) and the summon-brain
  (`findPriorityTarget` — deliberately built as the shared targeting
  brain, its comment says so) have no visibility concept. With manual
  targeting locked OUT by canon, **auto-aim firing into stone is a
  player-facing failure, not an edge case** — LoS filtering in those two
  functions is mandatory scope for Unit 2, not polish.

## 7. Smallest reusable data structure (recommendation)

Per the lock §10.1–10.2, deliberately minimal:

```
ArenaGeometry (new, in ArenaConfig or sibling file)
  blockedFootprints: [Footprint]     // v2.1: exactly one
  routeNodes:        [RouteNode]     // authored waypoints, ~6-10
  routeEdges:        [(Int, Int)]    // adjacency, hand-authored
  spawnZones:        [SpawnZone]     // labeled arcs/regions w/ gate ids
  safeAnchors:       [CGPoint]       // player spawn, boss anchor, orb margins
Footprint = convex rounded-rect (center, halfExtents, cornerRadius, rotation)
  → contains(point, margin), resolve(point, radius) -> point,
    intersectsSegment(a, b, radius) — three functions, all cheap
```

- **Convex rounded-rect, not a polygon mesh**: covers "asymmetrical
  carrier slightly off-center" (rotation + offset), keeps
  resolve/segment tests a handful of FLOPs, and `CultivatedZoneNode`
  already models the membership-primitive pattern to imitate.
- **Authored waypoint graph, not pathfinding**: decision-point model per
  the lock — an actor picks a route at a node, commits, re-decides at
  the next node (Spurhound's behavior lock is literally this). No
  GameplayKit, no navmesh. Route choice = "is the direct segment to my
  target blocked? if yes, seek the best next node."
- **Enforcement = displacement resolve, not physics**, per the monument
  precedent: one resolve call for the player (after move AND after
  pulls), one pass for enemies post-chase, sampler rejection for
  placements. `showsPhysics` won't draw any of this — the debug overlay
  is hand-drawn (flag + shape + route nodes, following the Panda
  seam-banner idiom, GameScene:998).

## 8. Performance risks on the oldest supported device class

- No enemy cap exists; spawn interval floors at 0.25s; late-run counts
  are unbounded. `for … in enemies` appears **67 times** in GameScene;
  ~7 full-array scans already run per frame (auto-aim, priority
  targets, Windchill ×2, cultivated ground, wells, chill trail, arc
  wake — the last is the worst existing loop).
- The cheap path: ONE static convex footprint, segment tests behind a
  bounding-circle pre-check, LoS only in the two targeting funnels and
  the projectile step — additive cost is a few comparisons per
  enemy-frame. The expensive path (don't): occlusion inside
  `damageEnemiesInRadius` (15 sites, some nested).
- No stated frame budget or device floor exists anywhere in the code;
  60fps on Brandon's iPhone 15 is the only observed target. Recommend:
  measure a worst-case swarm in the Splitworks shell during Unit 1 and
  write the number down as the arc's budget.

## 9. Existing test/debug infrastructure

None automated — no test target exists; validation is playtest +
sim-driven checks. Usable seams: `showsFPS`/`showsNodeCount` (on in
DEBUG), the Panda seam-banner idiom for announcing active debug flags,
`CodexManager.debugSummary()` console dump at scene start (a home for
geometry diagnostics), and the nodes/fps HUD. Unit 1 builds the §10.5
overlay by hand: footprint fill, route nodes/edges, per-actor selected
route, rejected-sample markers, stuck events. All behind one announced
DEBUG flag.

## 10. Recommended unit boundaries (refines lock §12 with evidence)

- **Unit 1 — Geometry foundation + Splitworks shell.** ArenaGeometry
  descriptor + Footprint math (+ VectorMath primitives) + player resolve
  generalized from `pushPlayerOutOfMonument` (called after move AND
  pulls) + enemy post-chase resolve + shared placement sampler routed
  into pickups/wells/rift-origins/Terra ghost + `swapArena` safe anchor
  + frozen-`spawnDistance` fix + debug overlay. Existing enemies only.
  **Proof:** both routes traversable by player + representative enemies,
  nothing clips or traps, no pickup/well/zone lands in the footprint
  (sampler telemetry counts rejections), 60fps under a worst-case swarm,
  overlay verifies visually.
- **Unit 1b — Route graph.** Waypoint graph + choose/commit/recover API;
  prove it by making EXISTING melee enemies route around the Carrier
  (blocked-segment → seek node). **Proof:** an enemy on the far side
  reaches the player via either route, re-decides when displaced, never
  oscillates at a decision point.
- **Unit 2 — Roster** (Spurhound/Linekeeper/Ramplate per the lock), plus
  the two mandatory policy applications the roster depends on:
  projectile blocking (Linekeeper's shot respects the Carrier) and
  auto-aim/priority-target LoS (no firing into stone). Ramplate needs
  the explicit footprint property (§5).
- **Unit 3 — Marchwarden.** Charge reuses the boss wall-termination
  precedent; Standardfall reuses the sky-strike grammar; Muster Signal
  uses spawn zones from the descriptor.
- **Unit 4 — Progression/reward/codex/art/sound + Boss Mode compat.**
  Registry entry = 3 fields (Unit 0 already shipped). Marchworn skin via
  the earned-skin pipeline; bestiary via the Arena 5 process.
- **Unit 5 — Integration, tuning, telemetry closeout** per lock §14.

## Design conflicts needing a call (Brandon/Lyra)

1. **Auras through walls**: recommend "supernatural, passes" — cheap and
   fiction-clean. Confirm.
2. **Growth zones vs. footprint**: recommend visual overlap allowed,
   membership excluded (no mending inside stone). Confirm.
3. **Rift Cannon / Erasure Echo random origins**: recommend reroll out of
   footprint + beam declared reality-piercing. Confirm.
4. **Ranged mobs behind cover** (base RangedEnemy, pre-Linekeeper): they
   stop at range and shoot the wreck forever today. Cheap fix: keep
   approaching while LoS is blocked. Confirm this applies to legacy
   shooters in the Splitworks spawn table, not just Linekeeper.

   Carrier: recommend accept (both read as supernatural).
6. **Exemption list to canonize**: Mote entrance, Event Horizon,
   sky-strike family, panda systems (their tolerance for imprecision is
   high by design — cheapest to exempt entirely and let pandas be
   pandas).
