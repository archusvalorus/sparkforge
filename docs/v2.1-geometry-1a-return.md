## Final data structures + insertion seams

Exactly the reconciliation §4 shape:

```
ArenaGeometry { blockedFootprints, routeNodes, routeEdges, spawnZones, safeAnchors }
Footprint { center, halfExtents, cornerRadius, rotation, label }
  contains(p, margin) · resolve(p, actorRadius) · intersectsSegment(a, b, travelRadius)
PlacementSampler.randomPoint(in:, minRadius:, maxRadius:, margin:)   // area-uniform, rejects, never nil
```

- **Enforcement = displacement**, monument precedent, no physics body.
  Physics bit «3 (`boundary`) left as-is (reconciliation: not work for its
  own sake).
- **`resolve` iterates** (≤4 gradient steps): a rounded-box SDF is exact
  outside but a lower bound inside, so a one-step push undershoots from
  deep interior. Found by the proof harness, fixed, re-proven idempotent.
- **`geometry` is a builder on ArenaConfig, materialized once per
  `setupArena`** — layouts size off the live arena radius, and a stored
  geometry inside a `static let` config recursed into its own
  `dispatch_once` (SIGTRAP at first launch; same class of bug as the
  frozen `spawnDistance`, fixed the same way).
- **Seams (all announced by the HUD banner while on):**
  `GeometryDebug.showOverlay` (draw footprint/margin/routes/gates/anchors)
  and `GeometryDebug.forceSplitworksShell` (play any selected arena AS the
  shell, via the transient Boss-Mode override — cleared on return to
  title, never persisted; the shell is not in `ArenaConfig.all`, so
  progression can't see it). Both `false` at HEAD; sim reinstalled cold.
- **Actor footprint radius** is explicit and separate from contact bodies
  (§7.2). Reads scale, unlike the physics body.

# Unit 2a — Travel & vision policies (return, Sep 2026)

*The two shared-system prerequisites the reconciliation §5 puts ahead of

(runs kept dying to laptop sleep). **GATE PASSED — Brandon's device run, Sep 8

Carrier, shots dying on the iron from both sides — "looking solid."** Files:
`GameScene.swift`, `RangedEnemyNode.swift`, `GeometryDebug.swift`,
`GameConfig.swift` (`Geometry` enum). Next = roster 2b Spurhound.*

## What landed

- **Swept projectile blocking** — player AND enemy projectiles: each frame,
  the segment from last position to current is tested against blocked
  geometry (`GameConfig.Geometry.projectileTravelRadius = 3`). Blocked →
  resolved to the impact point, muted ash-gray impact ring, removed.
  Gravity Well still spawns where the shot died (canon: wells form where
  shots die). Covers ~12 card effects via the single `updateProjectiles`
  funnel. Open arenas: one bool, zero cost.
- **Auto-aim line of sight** (`findNearestTargetPosition`): occluded
  enemies/boss are skipped; if nothing is visible the result is nil =
  HOLD FIRE (the clean fallback — firing into stone was the failure).
  Lasso priority stays first; if the lassoed prey is occluded it falls
  through to nearest-visible.
- **Summon-brain line of sight** (`findPriorityTarget`): the shared
  targeting brain (turrets, flowers, lion, kaiju, tree launches, Iron
  Maiden) tests visibility from ITS OWN origin. Cheap-skip before the
  segment test: only candidates that could beat the current best get
  tested.
- **Ruling 4 — legacy shooters seek line of sight**:
  `rangedChase(canFire:)` (default true — every legacy call site + open
  arena unchanged). While occluded, a shooter never stops-and-fires; it
  keeps approaching, and since the route steer hands it waypoints,
  "approaching" IS relocating around cover.
- Counters: `projectileBlocksPlayer/Enemy`, `losSuppressedTargets`,
  `rangedHeldFire` in the `[GEO]` dump.

# Unit 2b — Spurhound (return, Sep 8 2026)

*Roster #1 of the Splitworks, per the reconciliation's "one enemy at a
time." Spatial verb: GO AROUND. Built on 1B + 2a. **GATE PASSED — Brandon's
device feel pass, Sep 15 2026.** Files: `SpurhoundNode.swift` (new),
`EnemyNode.swift`, `GameScene.swift`, `GameConfig.swift` (`Spurhound`),
`GeometryDebug.swift`.*

## What was built

- **`SpurhoundNode`** — four phases: **hunt** (follows the 1B steer target;
  while `isOccludedFromGoal` the sprint ramps to 2.3× base over 0.9s, and
  decays over 0.5s after re-emerging so it comes AROUND the corner fast
  instead of braking at it) → **tell** (in sight, within 210pt, off
  cooldown: exhaust flares wide + bright, hull crouches, lunge line
  committed at tell START — the Star Needle grammar, punish late movement
  not random movement) → **lunge** (600pt/s for 0.38s ≈ 228pt, fixed
  direction, no re-aim) → **recover**. Three lunge outcomes: **hit** (short
  0.6s recovery), **miss** (1.4s, punishable), **clang** (ran into the
  Carrier: bounce, ceramic-rim flash, 1.4s, punishable). Punishable =
  1.5× incoming damage, implemented in a `takeDamage` override so card
  marks on `vulnerabilityMultiplier` (Apex T4) are never stomped.
- **Flank preference** = the promised scoring tweak. `EnemyNode.
  routeNodeBias(_:goal:occupancy:)` (default 0 → every pre-2b enemy routes
  bit-identically to 1B). Spurhound: nodes on its own side of the player
  cost up to +140, far-side nodes earn up to −140, plus +60 per hound
  already committed to a node (the lock's "subject to crowding"). The
  scene rebuilds a per-frame `routeOccupancy` map (one O(n) pass, skipped
  on open arenas).
- **Two published facts** the scene already computed: `isOccludedFromGoal`
  (set in the steer pass — no second geometry query) and
  `geometryDisplacedThisFrame` (set by the resolve pass — "you hit the
  Carrier"). `didStrikePlayer()` fires from the contact handler BEFORE
  the i-frame guards, because the CONTACT is the outcome.
- **Splitworks spawn table** (`spawnSplitworksEnemy`, arena id 5): basic
  bodies from 0s, Spurhounds at 15s (35% of spawns, Beat 2 "remember the
  flank"), legacy shooters at 45s as the Linekeeper placeholder (2c),
  melee thinning inherited. Spawns come from the **authored gate zones**
  (`splitworksSpawnPoint` — random zone, random angle in its arc, resolved
  out of any footprint) — the lock's spawn zones start paying rent.
- **Visual grammar** (procedural, lock §5.1): low forward-canted wedge in
  charcoal iron with an oxidized-teal rim, pale-ceramic rail-skate spurs at
  the rear corners, kiln-orange exhaust plume that brightens with the
  sprint and flares on the tell. Chassis rotates to face travel.
- Counters: `hound[lunge/hit/miss/clang]` in the `[GEO]` dump.
- Dials: `GameConfig.Spurhound` (speeds, ramp, range, tell/lunge/recover
  timings, punish multiplier, cooldown, flank/crowd weights, spawn table).

## Brandon's gate — what to feel for

1. **Does a hound going behind the Carrier feel like a threat you have to
   remember?** Watch the exhaust brighten as it disappears; it should come
   out the OTHER side faster than it went in.
2. **Is the tell fair?** 0.5s of flare + crouch, then a straight 228pt
   lunge. You should be able to sidestep on reaction; a late dodge should
   get clipped.
3. **Punish window** — after a miss or clang the hull dulls to overheated
   brown for 1.4s and takes 1.5×. Is that long enough to feel earned,
   short enough to stay dangerous?
4. **Pack behavior** — do two or three hounds split across both routes, or
   conga-line one side? (`flankBias` / `crowdPenalty` are the dials.)
5. Anything that feels like the Star Needle in a different coat is a
   tuning note, not a design note — the differentiator is the corner.
